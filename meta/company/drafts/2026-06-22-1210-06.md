## Post

Meta just added a 5% fee on ads in Austria, France, Italy, Spain, Turkey, and the UK.

It covers Digital Services Taxes -- money going to governments, not to better targeting or improved reach.

Paid acquisition in those markets just got more expensive with no change in performance.

This is the direction EU ad policy is heading. And it probably won't stop at 5%.

The brands navigating this well aren't just absorbing the cost. They're reducing dependence on paid acquisition by building audiences they own.
