## Post

37% of buyers now start their product research inside ChatGPT or Perplexity -- not Google.

That's not a trend anymore. That's a channel.

The buyer's journey in 2026 doesn't start on your website. It starts with a conversation with an AI assistant that either mentions your brand or doesn't.

Most B2B brands have no strategy for this. They're optimizing landing pages and running retargeting ads while a third of all research sessions start somewhere they're completely invisible.

What makes a brand show up in AI answers: consistent, clear presence across multiple surfaces -- your own site, LinkedIn, review platforms, community discussions, third-party coverage. The AI learns what brands are relevant to which problems by seeing your name consistently alongside the right topics.

The brands that figured this out early are getting cited. Everyone else is a gap in the answer.

Save this for your next marketing strategy review -- this is worth a line item on the agenda.
