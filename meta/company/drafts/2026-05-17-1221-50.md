## Post

AI is driving a 10x productivity boost for the individuals who know how to use it.

That's been proven. Developers shipping faster. Marketers creating more. Analysts processing data in hours instead of weeks.

Here's what hasn't been proven yet:

AI making companies 10x more productive.

The gap between individual gains and company-level results is real — and growing. AI tools are everywhere. The org-level ROI isn't matching the hype.

The reason: you can't swap the tools without redesigning the systems.

The data flowing into your AI stack is the constraint. If your customer data is fragmented, your first-party signals are unused, and your workflows aren't built to act on AI output — better tools won't close the gap.

The companies winning with AI didn't just buy better models.

They built better inputs first.

Save this for the next conversation about which AI tool to buy.
