## Post

D2C brands are opening physical stores. Not because digital failed -- because their customers already know them before they walk in.

The ones doing it well aren't choosing between online and offline.

They're using digital to build the relationship. Physical to close it.

That shift changes what marketing means for a D2C company. You're not starting from scratch with a walk-in customer. You have the data -- what they browsed, what they almost bought, what brought them back. The store becomes the end of a journey you've already been running.

Most brands opening stores haven't figured this out yet. They treat the location as a separate channel instead of the final step in a funnel they already own.

The channel isn't the strategy. The customer journey is.

Save this if you're thinking about where physical retail fits in your growth plan.
