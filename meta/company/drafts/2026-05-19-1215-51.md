## Post

CAC is up 60% in 5 years.

The most common response? Spend more.

More budget. More channels. More campaigns.

Here's the problem with that logic:

If your conversion rate stays flat, doubling your spend doubles your cost — not your revenue.

The teams cutting CAC in 2026 aren't outspending the problem. They're converting a higher percentage of the traffic they already have.

Same visitors. Better outcomes. That's a fundamentally different lever.
