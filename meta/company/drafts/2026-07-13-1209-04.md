## Post

The market for knowing who visits your website is projected to grow from $2.85B to $7.81B by 2035.

That growth isn't because the technology suddenly got interesting.

It's because the math changed.

When paid traffic was cheap, a 2% conversion rate was acceptable. The 98% who left were treated as noise -- a cost of doing business.

As CAC keeps rising, the math flips. Every visit costs more to generate. The 98% who don't convert start looking less like noise and more like the biggest untapped asset in your marketing stack.

The visitor identification market is growing because ignoring anonymous traffic stopped being affordable.

Save this if you're rethinking how much value you're leaving on the table from traffic you already paid for.
