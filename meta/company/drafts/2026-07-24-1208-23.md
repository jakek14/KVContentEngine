## Post

3.3% monthly churn sounds manageable.

Run the annual math. That's roughly a third of your customer base gone by December.

Most subscription businesses track churn monthly and feel okay about the number. Annualized, it tells a very different story.

The brands cutting churn fastest in 2026 aren't doing it with better onboarding sequences. They're identifying at-risk customers before the cancellation email arrives -- watching engagement patterns, usage drops, login frequency. The signals are already in your data.

Most companies just aren't watching until it's too late.

If you run a subscription business, save this and pull your annualized churn number today. That's the one worth optimizing.
