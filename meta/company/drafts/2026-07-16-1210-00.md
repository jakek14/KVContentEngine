## Post

The average e-commerce site converts at 1.8%.

Most brands look at that number and buy more traffic.

More ad spend. More sessions. More clicks.

1.8% of a bigger number is still almost nothing.

The brands growing efficiently are doing the opposite. They're not throwing more traffic at a leaky bucket -- they're understanding the 98% who showed up and didn't buy.

What brought them there. What pages they hit. Whether they came back days later.

That's not a traffic problem. That's a signal problem.

More reach won't fix it.

Save this the next time someone suggests the answer is more ad spend.
