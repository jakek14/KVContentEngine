## Post

60% of knowledge workers are now remote or hybrid.

That's a problem hidden inside most B2B marketing stacks.

Traditional B2B visitor identification works by matching IP addresses to companies. When someone visits from their office, the IP resolves to the company. You know the account.

When that same person visits from home, the IP resolves to Comcast. Or AT&T. Or their apartment building's ISP.

No company match. No identification. Invisible.

The remote work shift happened gradually enough that most teams haven't recalculated what it means for their visitor data. But the math changed.

A high-intent B2B buyer working from their kitchen table looks exactly the same as a casual browser to any tool built around IP matching.

B2B marketing teams are making targeting decisions based on traffic data that's only partially visible.

Save this for your next stack review — the identification gap is probably larger than your current analytics show.
