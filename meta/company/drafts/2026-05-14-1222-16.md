## Post

Signal-qualified leads convert 47% better.

They produce 43% larger deals.

The brands hitting those numbers aren't doing more outreach. They're reaching people at the right moment — when there's actual evidence of buying interest.

Here's the gap most teams haven't solved:

You can't act on a signal you can't see.

Most website visits leave no trace. No form. No click. No record. Just a person who showed up, spent real time on your site, and left — and your team has no idea who they were or how close to buying they actually were.

The most powerful intent signal in B2B isn't a third-party score.

It's someone actively on your site right now.

If you can't see that, you're reaching out blind.

Save this and share it with your sales team.
