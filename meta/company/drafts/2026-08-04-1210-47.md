## Post

CAC payback just hit 18 months.

That's the new median for B2B SaaS. Three months longer than it was in 2023.

Most marketing teams respond by trying to lower CAC.

That's not wrong. But the math improves faster on the other side.

A customer who stays 36 months instead of 18 just doubled the lifetime value without you spending one extra dollar on acquisition. That's a 2x revenue outcome from the same upfront cost.

Companies generating the most efficient growth right now are obsessing over months 1 through 6. What creates the habit. What signals churn before it happens. How quickly the product becomes part of the customer's workflow.

CAC is an acquisition problem.

Retention is a revenue problem.

The two get solved very differently. And in 2026, the retention lever has more upside.
