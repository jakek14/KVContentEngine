## Post

56% of your ad performance comes down to the creative.

Not the audience. Not the bid strategy. Not the placement.

The creative.

On Meta specifically, UGC-style content -- videos and images that look like a real person made them -- consistently outperforms polished brand production. 28% higher engagement. 29% higher conversion.

High-budget creative can work against you. It signals "ad" before anyone reads a word. UGC signals "content."

That distinction is doing a lot of work.

If your Meta creative looks like it belongs in a TV spot, test something that looks like it belongs in a feed. The results might surprise you.

Save this for your next creative brief.
