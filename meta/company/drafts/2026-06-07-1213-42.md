## Post

Not all anonymous visitors are the same.

Some are browsing. Some are buying. The signals are there before they ever fill out a form.

Three things that separate a serious buyer from a casual browser:

Pricing page. Returned more than once.

Case studies. Visited multiple in the same session.

Time on page. They actually read it, not a 12-second scroll.

Someone who hits all three isn't cold. They're warm -- maybe very warm.

The problem is most teams never see it. The visitor left without submitting a form, so they don't exist in the CRM. But the intent was there.

That's the intelligence gap most marketing teams are operating with right now.
