## Post

Gartner predicts 60% of brands will use AI to deliver personalized 1:1 experiences by 2028.

That number is probably right.

Here's what's missing from that prediction:

You can't deliver 1:1 to someone you can't identify.

Most brands will invest in the personalization layer — AI recommendations, dynamic content, behavioral triggers — without solving the identity layer underneath it.

The result: sophisticated automation running on a fraction of the people who were actually on your site.

The personalization revolution has an identity problem.

That's the layer most brands skipped.
