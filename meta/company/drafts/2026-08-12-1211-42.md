## Post

You'll pay again to reach them with retargeting. Then again if they still don't convert.

Picture having their name and email the moment they left.

No ad spend to re-engage. A direct line. A personal follow-up based on what they actually looked at.

The traffic you already paid for is more valuable than you're treating it.
