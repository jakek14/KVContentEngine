## Post

AI made it easy to produce 50 ad variations instead of 5.

Most teams took that as permission to run more.

The ones pulling ahead figured out something different.

Making more creative is just the input. The output is knowing which concepts actually convert -- and why.

Creative intelligence is what separates them. Not production speed.

Production is basically free now. The bottleneck moved. It's not "can we make enough to test." It's "do we understand our creative well enough to know what to test next."

The brands winning in 2026 solved the second problem.

Save this for your next creative review.
