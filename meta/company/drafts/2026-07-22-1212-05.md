## Post

The 3:1 LTV:CAC ratio used to be the target. In 2026, it's the floor.

The 2026 ecommerce benchmarks put the median LTV:CAC at 3.2. Top-quartile operators are running 4.6 to 6.2.

That gap doesn't come from spending less on acquisition. It comes from keeping customers longer.

Top-quartile brands retain more than 60% of customers past year one. The median brand loses 69% by that same point.

The lever isn't the ad creative or the bidding strategy. It's retention -- and retention starts with knowing who your customers actually are, what they bought, and when they're likely to come back.

The brands compounding LTV aren't spending differently at the top of the funnel. They're just much better at everything that happens after the first purchase.
