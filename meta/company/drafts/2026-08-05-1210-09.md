## Post

An abandoned cart email sent 45 minutes after they leave recovers 8-12% of those carts.

The same email sent 3 days later? Less than 1%.

The cart isn't what changed. The timing did.

Most ecommerce brands are sending abandonment emails too late -- usually because they're waiting for a batch to process, a sequence to trigger, or a team member to approve the flow.

The fastest brands in the world automate this window down to minutes. Not hours. Not the next day.

If you're not recovering carts in the first hour, you're leaving most of that revenue on the table.

Save this for when you're auditing your email flows.
