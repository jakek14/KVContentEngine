## Post

Most brands track email delivery rate.

That measures whether the email left your server.

The number that actually matters is inbox placement: did it land in the primary inbox, or in promotions, or spam?

Average commercial inbox placement in 2026: 89%.

B2B SaaS: 92%.

The gap between those two numbers is where revenue gets left behind. An email sitting in the promotions tab might as well not exist.

And open rates? Apple Mail Privacy Protection has made them nearly meaningless. Over 95% of Apple Mail users opted in -- which means Apple servers are auto-opening emails before anyone reads them. If you're still celebrating a 35% open rate, you're celebrating a number.

The metrics that actually tell you if email is working: click rate. Revenue per email. List growth.

Save this before your next email audit.
