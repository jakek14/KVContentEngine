## Post

Acquiring a new customer costs 60% more than it did in 2020.

That number hasn't peaked yet.

Ad auction inflation, iOS privacy changes, rising CPCs on every platform -- the cost of buying attention keeps going up. And the brands treating paid acquisition as their primary growth lever are watching margin compress in real time.

The shift the smart D2C brands are making: moving spend from acquisition into retention. Not abandoning paid -- rebalancing it.

A customer you keep costs a fraction of one you acquire. And the brands that shifted 20-30% of paid budget toward email, loyalty, and owned channels in 2025 saw LTV go up while CAC held flat.

But retention has a prerequisite most brands overlook.

You can't retain someone you can't identify.

The shoppers hitting your product pages, browsing your collections, abandoning carts -- most leave without converting. And without a name, there's no email to send. No follow-up. No second chance.

KnownVisitors identifies those visitors before they're gone. Verified name, email, and phone -- compliant and ready to use. The person who showed buying intent but didn't check out isn't lost. They're reachable.

When acquisition costs keep rising, making more of the traffic you already have is the move.
