## Post

App users generate 5x higher lifetime value than web-only shoppers.

Most ecommerce brands have a mobile-optimized website. Very few have a mobile strategy.

The gap between the two is where LTV gets built or doesn't.

A mobile website is a visit. An app is a relationship. Push notifications with actual timing. In-app loyalty that keeps the brand alive after the transaction. Post-purchase touchpoints that turn one-time buyers into habits.

A web visitor converts once and leaves. An app user is inside your ecosystem.

The brands pulling ahead on LTV in 2026 aren't running harder acquisition campaigns. They're building habits -- the kind that look like repeat purchases, higher AOV, and referrals you didn't have to pay for.

The biggest underused retention lever in ecommerce right now isn't a smarter email sequence.

It's mobile.

Save this for your next planning session.
