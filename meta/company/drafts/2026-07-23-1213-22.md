## Post

11 major ecommerce brands opened their first physical stores in 2026.

For years, pure D2C was the winning formula. No retail markups. Direct customer data. Higher margins.

But something shifted.

The brands winning on retention aren't staying digital-only. They're treating physical and digital as two sides of the same flywheel.

A customer who discovers a brand online and then has an in-store experience has a fundamentally different lifetime value profile than a pure-digital buyer. The omnichannel customer spends more, churns less, and refers more.

We're watching this play out across every product category. Beauty. Apparel. Wellness. The brands that figured out physical presence early are seeing digital results that pure-play competitors can't match.

Omnichannel isn't a sign you've "made it." It's a growth lever.

The era of "we'll do retail when we're big enough" is ending for brands that want to compete on retention.

Save this for your next channel strategy conversation.
