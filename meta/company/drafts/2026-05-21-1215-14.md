## Post

The average e-commerce site converts 1.9% of visitors.

Top-performing stores convert 4-5%.

The gap between them isn't better ads or more traffic.

It's knowing who the visitor is before they bounce.

Average stores treat every visitor the same — same product page, same hero banner, same cart experience, whether it's a first-time browser or someone who's been back three times.

Top stores personalize. They act on behavioral signals. They follow up.

But you can only act on what you know.

If 97% of your visitors leave without identifying themselves, you're optimizing the 3% and ignoring the rest.

That's where the conversion gap lives.

We built KnownVisitors to close it — identifying anonymous visitors so the behavior signals you're already collecting can connect to a real person, a real contact, a real follow-up.

The traffic is already there. The visitors are already showing intent.

Most brands just can't see them.
