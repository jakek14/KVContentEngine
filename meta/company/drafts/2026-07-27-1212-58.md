## Post

D2C ecommerce just passed $239 billion in the US.

That's 19.2% of all retail.

And the brands that built this channel are now doing something unexpected -- they're opening wholesale accounts, signing with independent retailers, and building physical presence.

Shopify is reportedly in talks with Faire, the wholesale marketplace, for a potential combination. That one deal tells you exactly where D2C is heading.

The brands that went direct didn't do it to stay direct forever. They did it to own their customer data, prove their demand, and build brand recognition before going into distribution channels that would have taken all their margin before they had leverage.

Now they have leverage.

A brand with a proven LTV, repeat purchase data, and a real customer community walks into a wholesale conversation completely differently than a brand that's never sold direct. The retailer takes your call. You set the terms.

D2C was the launchpad. Omnichannel is the destination.

Save this for your next channel strategy conversation.
