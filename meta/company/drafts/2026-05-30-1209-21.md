## Post

Your website is the highest-intent media channel you own.

And most brands treat it like a brochure.

Someone landing on your pricing page is showing more purchase intent than anyone who saw your last Google ad.

Someone returning to your site three times in one week is more valuable than most of your remarketing list.

But if they don't fill out a form, they vanish.

98% of visitors leave without identifying themselves.

The brands getting this right aren't spending more on ads. They're capturing the intent signal already on their site -- identifying who visited, what they looked at, and when they came back.

KnownVisitors turns anonymous site traffic into a contact list of real, verified people who already showed interest.

The intent is already there. Most brands just aren't collecting it.

Save this the next time you're reviewing your traffic-to-lead conversion rate.
