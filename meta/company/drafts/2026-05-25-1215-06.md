## Post

Here's a number worth sitting with.

Warm audiences — people who already visited your site — convert 5 to 10 times better than cold traffic.

You paid to get them there. They showed up. They looked around.

And most of them left without leaving a trace you could act on.

The old retargeting playbook assumed you could follow them across the web after they left. Cookie deprecation ended that.

Today, the average advertiser can match maybe 10-30% of their site visitors to retargeting audiences.

Which means the most valuable segment of your traffic — people already familiar with your brand, already showing purchase intent — is mostly invisible.

The reframe:

The problem isn't getting more traffic.

It's knowing who's already there.

That's the gap KnownVisitors closes — identifying anonymous site visitors by name, email, and contact details, verified and compliance-ready.

Your highest-converting audience already visited.

You just need to know who they are.
