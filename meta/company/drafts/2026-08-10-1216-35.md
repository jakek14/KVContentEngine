## Post

For 20 years, Google was the largest advertising platform on earth.

This year, Meta is projected to surpass it for the first time.

Meta: $243 billion in global ad revenue. Google: $239 billion.

It's the first time in the history of digital advertising that Google hasn't held the top spot.

What drove it: Advantage+ AI automation, new ad inventory on WhatsApp and Threads, and a structural shift in how people discover products -- moving from search toward feeds, from intent-driven to recommendation-driven.

For marketers, the story isn't "move your budget from Google to Meta."

It's that both platforms are doing different jobs.

Google is still the intent channel. A buyer who searches for your category already knows they have a problem. Meta is the discovery channel -- buyers encounter you before they're actively looking, sometimes before they even know they need what you sell.

Those are different buyers at different moments. They shouldn't get the same message.

The brands winning in 2026 fund each channel based on the stage of the journey it serves -- not based on which platform is "winning."
