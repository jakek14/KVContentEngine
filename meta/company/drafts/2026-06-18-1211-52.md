## Post

Only 27% of marketers are using first-party data in their paid ad campaigns.

That means 73% are spending budget on audiences they don't own, built from data they didn't collect.

The gap between those two groups is widening as third-party data quality degrades and ad costs keep climbing.

Here's the framework for crossing over:

Step 1: Collect. Every form fill, every opt-in, every identified site visitor goes into a list you own. Don't let that data live only inside the ad platform's backend.

Step 2: Activate. Upload your customer list as a Custom Audience seed. Let the algorithm build lookalikes from actual buyers -- not anonymous cookies.

Step 3: Exclude. Suppress existing customers from acquisition campaigns. Stop spending budget to re-acquire people who already converted.

Step 4: Layer. Visitors who came to your site AND match your buyer profile are your highest-intent audience. Bid up on that intersection.

The brands with a first-party advantage aren't spending more. They're making the same budget work harder.

Save this for your next media planning session.
