## Post

Most website visitor tools tell you what company visited your site.

Not who at that company.

There's a significant gap between those two things.

Knowing "a mid-market SaaS company from Austin was on your pricing page" is better than nothing. But it doesn't tell you who to reach out to. It doesn't tell you what to say. It hands your sales team a company name and says good luck.

True person-level identification -- knowing the individual, their email, their role -- is what actually closes the gap between website traffic and pipeline.

In 2026, only a handful of tools deliver this consistently. Remote work made it harder: employees working from home resolve to consumer ISPs, not corporate networks. Company-level tools hit a wall. Person-level tools keep working.

That's what we built KnownVisitors to do.

Know who's on your site. Not just where they work.
