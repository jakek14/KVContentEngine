## Post

Threads just hit 400M monthly users.

App Ads launched globally there this month.

And most brands still haven't touched it.

That window -- a platform this size with ad inventory this uncrowded -- doesn't stay open long. It's the same dynamic that existed on Instagram Stories in 2017, or TikTok in 2019, before every brand and agency discovered it at once.

The brands that test now will have creative learnings, audience data, and CPM benchmarks before the market saturates. That's a real advantage.

If your brand is in B2B, Threads skews toward a professional and creator audience. Worth a test budget this quarter before the price catches up with the audience size.
