## Post

72% of Google searches end without a click now.

That number was 54% in 2022. Every year, Google answers more queries directly -- AI Overviews, featured snippets, knowledge panels -- and fewer people need to click through to a website.

For businesses that built their acquisition strategy around organic search, this is a structural shift.

It's not a traffic slump. It's a channel compression.

What the data says about what still works:

Email lists are immune to algorithm changes. B2B newsletters average 20-35% open rates and return $36-46 per dollar spent. If you have traffic right now, convert some of it into direct subscribers.

Brand recognition compounds. The companies people search for by name -- who type the URL directly or search "brand + category" -- don't lose ground when search results change. Building a brand that people know before they're ready to buy creates a floor that no AI Overview can take away.

Visitors who do click through have higher intent than ever. When an AI Overview already answered the surface question, the person who still clicked to your site wanted something more. That's a real signal.

Traffic strategy built on one channel is fragile. The companies winning in 2026 built redundancy before they needed it.

Save this if you're rethinking how you're getting found.
