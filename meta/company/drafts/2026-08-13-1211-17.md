## Post

Meta just announced an AI creative suite that learns from your existing ads.

It generates new creative that stays consistent with what already works for your brand -- without a designer in the loop for every variation.

41 percent of Meta advertisers are already using AI generative creative tools. That number is going to look low in six months.

For small B2B and ecommerce teams, this matters more than it does for large ones.

Big teams have always been able to produce and test creative at scale. Small teams get maybe a handful of new concepts per month.

AI creative collapses that constraint. Which means the competitive advantage shifts -- away from whoever can produce the most, toward whoever has the clearest sense of which message they're actually testing.

Knowing your audience is not a new skill.

It just got more important.
