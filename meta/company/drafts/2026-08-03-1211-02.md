## Post

Meta CPMs are up 34% year over year.

You're spending more to reach the same people. And when you stop spending, the reach disappears completely.

That's the fundamental difference between rented and owned.

Every paid ad buys reach for a moment. Traffic arrives, clicks around, and leaves. If you didn't capture something permanent -- a name, an email, a contact -- that visitor is gone.

Owned data works differently. An email list compounds over time. A database of identified visitors keeps growing. You can reach those people next month, next quarter, without paying Meta again.

The brands getting ahead of rising CPMs aren't just optimizing their creative. They're capturing first-party data from every visitor who shows up -- before the bounce, before the cookie expires, before they forget the site existed.
