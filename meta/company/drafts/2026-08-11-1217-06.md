## Post

Mobile gets 60% of your site traffic.

And it converts at less than half the rate of desktop.

Desktop conversion rate: 3.9%. Mobile: 1.8%.

That gap is not a device problem. It's a design problem. Slow load times. Tiny CTAs. Checkout flows that require three scrolls before you see a field. A buying experience built for a screen it was never actually tested on.

Mobile has dominated traffic for years. The conversion gap should be closed by now. It isn't.

The brands closing it in 2026 are finding growth their ad spend alone never could have unlocked. Not more traffic -- better results from the traffic they already paid for.

The cheapest acquisition is fixing what's already broken.

Save this if you're running paid traffic to a mobile experience that isn't built to close.
