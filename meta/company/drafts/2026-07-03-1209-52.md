## Post

Remote work broke B2B visitor tracking.

Not partially. Significantly.

IP-to-company matching works when someone is sitting inside a corporate network. The moment they're working from home, that IP resolves to their consumer internet provider -- and your tracking tool returns nothing useful.

The majority of B2B knowledge workers are now hybrid or fully remote. Which means a substantial portion of your qualified website traffic is invisible by default.

Person-level identification solves this. It doesn't guess from an IP. It resolves the individual directly -- name, role, company, email -- regardless of where they're working from.

That's what KnownVisitors does.

Save this for the next time someone asks why your site traffic doesn't match your pipeline.
