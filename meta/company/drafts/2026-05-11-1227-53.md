## Post

The average Shopify store converts at 3%.

Stores using AI-powered search convert at 12.3%.

Same traffic. 4x the outcome.

Most brands are obsessed with getting more visitors. The real leverage is in what happens to the visitors already there.

The brands growing in 2026 aren't just pouring more into top-of-funnel. They're extracting more value from every visit.

Your existing traffic is your most underutilized asset. Treat it like one.
