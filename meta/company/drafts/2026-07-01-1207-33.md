## Post

Meta changed how they count conversions this year.

Likes, saves, shares -- those no longer roll into your conversion attribution. Only real link clicks count now.

A lot of advertisers are watching their Meta numbers drop. Some are panicking.

Here's the other way to read it.

The old numbers were wrong. You were attributing conversions to people who liked a post and kept scrolling. Your actual conversion rate was always this number. The reporting just wasn't showing it to you.

Platforms build attribution systems that make their own numbers look good. That keeps ad budgets flowing. Meta just voluntarily made their numbers look worse -- which is unusual. And worth paying attention to.

Your real baseline is cleaner than you've had before. The campaigns that look good under accurate attribution are your actual winners.

Build from the real number.
