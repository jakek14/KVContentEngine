## Post

Meta changed how they count clicks.

Real link clicks only now.

Likes, saves, shares, and comments no longer count as click-through conversions -- they moved to a separate category called "engage-through attribution."

Your reported CTR probably dropped. Your reported CPA probably went up.

The traffic didn't change. The measurement did.

What to do: pull your Meta campaigns and compare click-through data from before and after the change. If your CPA looks worse than it did a few months ago, the attribution update may be the reason -- not your creative or your targeting.

Reset your benchmarks before you pull budget from campaigns that are actually working.

Save this for your next Meta ads review.
