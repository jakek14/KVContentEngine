## Post

Segmented email campaigns generate 760% more revenue than broadcast sends.

Not double. Not triple. More than 7x.

Broadcast treats every subscriber the same -- same message, same timing, regardless of where they are in buying journey or what they actually care about.

Segmentation changes the math. The right message to the right person at the right moment drives a fundamentally different result.

Most marketers know they should segment. The real barrier is data. You can't segment what you don't know about your subscribers.

What did they look at before subscribing? What problem brought them to your list? What do their behaviors say about where they are in the buying process?

Those are the answers segmentation runs on.
