## Post

80% of consumers are more likely to buy from a brand that personalizes their experience.

The problem: you can't personalize for someone you don't know.

The average ecommerce site converts 2-3% of visitors. The other 97% browse, compare, and leave without filling out a form or starting a chat.

Most personalization tools work well for logged-in users and repeat buyers. The anonymous majority? Same generic experience as every other visitor.

That's the gap.

KnownVisitors closes it. We identify who's on your site before they leave -- name, email, company -- so personalization can actually happen for the visitors who haven't converted yet.

Save this if you're rethinking your personalization strategy.
