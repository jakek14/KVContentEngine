## Post

75% of brands are phasing out third-party data by 2026.

Not because the technology failed. Because the laws got stricter, the browsers got smarter, and buyers started demanding transparency.

The third-party tracking window is closing for good.

What fills the gap is first-party data -- the signals brands collect directly from their own site visitors, customers, and existing audience.

The companies building this infrastructure now won't just survive the privacy shift. They'll have a data advantage that compounds while competitors scramble to catch up.

Save this if your team is still relying on third-party audiences for targeting.
