## Post

Moving from 2% to 3% conversion is 100 more leads per month. No ad budget increase.

Here's the CRO playbook most teams haven't actually run:

Stop testing the obvious first.

Button colors and headline copy are the last 1% of conversion lift. The first 90% is whether the right message is reaching the right visitor at the right moment.

Identify which pages signal serious intent.

Pricing page plus product feature page in the same session means evaluation mode. Return visit within 48 hours means still in consideration. Time on site over 4 minutes means genuine engagement. Optimize these pages obsessively before anything else.

Reduce friction specifically for high-intent visitors.

Most sites are built for the average visitor. High-intent visitors want less content, faster social proof, and direct answers to the objections already forming in their head. Build for them.

Treat returning visitors differently than first-time visitors.

A person back for the third time doesn't need your origin story. They need the comparison chart, the guarantee, and the fastest path to next step.

The teams adding 100 leads a month through CRO aren't smarter.

They're treating visitor behavior like the signal it is — not noise to average out.

Save this for your next site audit.
