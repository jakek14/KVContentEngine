## Post

Email flows generate 41% of email revenue.

That's the benchmark -- 41% of revenue from just 5.3% of total sends. Your abandoned cart sequence, win-back flow, post-purchase nurture. They work.

But they only work for people already in your list.

Most website visitors never give you their email. They browse your site, hit your pricing page, compare your products, and leave. You see them as a session count in your dashboard. You don't see a name or a contact.

At KnownVisitors, we identify those anonymous visitors before they disappear -- turning the traffic you already paid for into contacts you can actually reach.

Your flows perform well. The question is who they're performing for.
