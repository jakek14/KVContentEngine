## Post

Email returns $36 for every $1 spent.

Social media returns $2.80.

Most brands spend the majority of their paid effort on social and treat email like an afterthought. The numbers say that's backwards.

Email doesn't require bidding wars, algorithm updates, or ongoing ad spend. It runs on permission -- and once you have it, you own the channel.

Three things the brands with strong email programs do differently:

They segment. Segmented campaigns hit 14% higher open rates and double the click rates of broadcast sends. One list with no structure is a wasted asset.

They trigger. Welcome sequences, browse abandonment, post-purchase flows -- automated emails based on behavior outperform one-size-fits-all blasts every time. The best-performing emails aren't the ones you schedule. They're the ones that fire when someone does something.

They track the right metrics. Not just open rates. Revenue per email and click-to-open rate show what's actually working. Open rate is a vanity metric in 2026 -- conversion is the number that matters.

The channel is there. Most brands just haven't built the system around it.
