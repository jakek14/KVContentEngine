## Post

70% of B2B buyers complete their research before they contact a vendor.

Not 30%. Not half. 70%.

They found you on Google. Read your case studies. Checked your reviews. Came back to your pricing page twice. Compared you to alternatives on their own -- privately, with no one watching.

By the time they reach out, most of the decision is already made.

Most B2B companies build their entire go-to-market around the 30% phase: the demo, the proposal, the sales call. They're optimizing for buyers who've already decided to make contact.

The ones pulling ahead have figured out how to be present in the 70% -- visible and credible during the research window that happens before any hand is raised.

That's exactly what KnownVisitors does. You see who's in that research phase -- who's on your site, how often they're returning, what they're looking at -- before they decide whether to reach out.

The 30% is where deals close. The 70% is where they're won.

Save this for your next go-to-market planning session.
