## Post

B2B buyers complete over 70% of their research before they ever talk to sales.

That's not new information. But most marketing stacks are still built like it isn't true.

You optimize for form fills. You build nurture sequences for people who give you their email. You attribute revenue to the last touch.

All of that happens in the final 30% of the buying journey.

The first 70% -- the research phase, the comparison phase, the "is this even the right solution" phase -- is almost completely invisible to most marketing teams.

That's not a content problem. It's not a brand problem.

It's a data problem.

KnownVisitors identifies the visitors doing their research on your site -- name, email, phone, company -- before they fill out a form or move on.

Save this for your next marketing planning session.
