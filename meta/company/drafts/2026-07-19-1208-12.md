## Post

Company-level visitor identification matches 10 to 30% of B2B traffic on a good day.

Person-level identification: under 10%.

That means up to 90% of your B2B visitors leave with no name, no email, no company attached. Just an anonymous session in your analytics dashboard.

This isn't a tracking gap. It's a structural one. Most identification tools were built around corporate IP addresses and third-party cookies. Remote work scattered IP data. Cookies got deprecated.

The companies getting real pipeline from their website in 2026 use identity graph resolution -- matching against verified behavioral data across devices to resolve actual people, not just companies. Match rates are higher. Contact data is verified before it reaches your CRM.

Your site traffic is more valuable than your analytics suggests. Most of it just isn't visible yet.
