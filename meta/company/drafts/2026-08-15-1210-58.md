## Post

Most B2B companies know exactly how many visitors their site gets.

They have no idea who any of them are.

You might run solid paid traffic. Have good organic reach. See strong time-on-page and low bounce rates.

But when someone asks "who's ready to buy?"-- the answer is almost always "whoever filled out a form." That's less than 2% of your actual visitors.

The other 98% are showing real intent. They're reading your pricing page, your case studies, your comparison pages. They're coming back a second time. They're doing everything a buyer does except raise their hand.

Most companies just have no way to reach them.

That's the gap KnownVisitors closes. Identify the anonymous visitors on your site -- name, email, phone, company, location -- and reach them while the interest is still warm. No forms, no popups.

Save this for your next team planning session.
