## Post

The average landing page converts at 2.35%.

The top 10% convert at 11.45%.

That's not a traffic problem. That's a page problem.

Three things that separate the top performers:

Message match. The ad says one thing. The landing page delivers exactly that thing. Most pages lose visitors in the first 10 seconds because the ad made a promise the page doesn't immediately keep.

Single focus. High-converting pages have one goal. Not a nav menu, not a blog link, not three CTAs. One offer, clearly stated, one action to take.

Speed. A one-second delay in load time drops conversions by 7%. Mobile visitors leave first.

Most traffic problems are conversion problems in disguise.

Save this for your next site audit.
