## Post

Your content doesn't just need to be good.

It needs to be good in the first 60 minutes.

Meta makes most of its distribution decisions in the initial engagement window. High saves and shares early? The algorithm pushes the content further. Nothing happening? It stops amplifying.

This changes how you should think about posting:

Don't post when you have time. Post when your audience is most active.

Don't post and disappear. Show up in comments during that window — your own engagement extends the algorithm's read on the post.

The content winning on Meta right now isn't just well-written. It's written specifically for the people most likely to immediately save it and send it to someone.

One question worth asking before you hit publish: if someone DMs this to a coworker or a friend, what's the specific line in the caption that makes them want to?

If there isn't one, keep editing.

Save this as a reminder before your next post.
