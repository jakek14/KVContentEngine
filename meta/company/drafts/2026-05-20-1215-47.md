## Post

B2B buying committees now average 11.2 stakeholders.

Most B2B content is written for one.

That gap is the conversion problem most marketing teams have never named.

The champion who found you loves your product.

Their CFO wants ROI numbers.

Their IT team wants the security story.

Legal wants compliance documentation.

Procurement wants the vendor questionnaire.

Your content probably covers one of those people really well.

Here's the shift worth making:

Map your content to every seat at the table.

Champion: features, use cases, competitive differentiation.

CFO: payback period, ROI calculator, total cost of ownership.

IT: security, integrations, uptime, implementation timeline.

Legal/compliance: certifications, data handling, privacy policy.

Procurement: pricing transparency, contract flexibility, vendor references.

The companies closing complex B2B deals aren't selling harder.

They're removing every objection before it gets raised.

Save this the next time a deal goes quiet after you thought it was almost closed.
