## Post

Shopify's AI agents can now recover your abandoned carts automatically.

That's table stakes by end of 2026.

The moat isn't the automation. It's the data quality the automation runs on.

A recovery agent operating on thin audience data sends the wrong message to the wrong people with good timing.

If you don't know who your buyers are -- at the person level, not just the segment -- you're handing a sophisticated tool a vague brief and wondering why the results are mediocre.

Signal quality is the moat. Always was. AI just made it obvious.
