## Post

3 behavioral signals on your website that tell you someone is close to buying — before they ever fill out a form:

1. Pricing page visit in the same session as a product feature page. The combination means they're calculating, not just browsing.

2. Return visit within 48 hours. Someone who comes back that fast didn't forget. They're still thinking.

3. Time on site over 4 minutes on a first visit. Sustained engagement from a specific source signals intent, not curiosity.

These signals exist in your analytics right now.
