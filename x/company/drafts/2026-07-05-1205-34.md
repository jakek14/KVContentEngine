## Post

LinkedIn company pages now average 1.6% organic reach.

Not declining. 1.6%.

If you have 10,000 followers on your company page, the average post reaches 160 people. 

The same content from a personal profile? 6 to 8 times more reach.

This isn't a content quality problem. It's structural -- LinkedIn is deliberately deprioritizing company pages in favor of person-to-person content distribution.

What actually works right now:

Your people post. The company doesn't. Get employees, founders, and subject matter experts posting from their personal accounts about things they genuinely know. That's where the reach lives.

Native text outperforms everything. A well-structured text post is beating carousels by 28% on average this year. Less is more.

Conversation depth matters more than likes. LinkedIn's algorithm evaluates comment length and whether the original poster responds. Eight substantive replies outperform forty "great post!" reactions.

Zero-click content wins. Posts that contain all the value -- not a teaser with a link -- reach 60% more people than posts pointing offsite.

The B2B brands winning on LinkedIn in 2026 look more like thought leaders than companies.
