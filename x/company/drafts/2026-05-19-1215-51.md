## Post

Google is deleting your historical ad data.

Starting June 2026: hourly, daily, and weekly reporting in Google Ads gets capped at 37 months.

Everything before that window — attribution data, seasonal benchmarks, channel performance across prior years — gone.

For teams that built strategy around historical comparisons, this is a real change.

Here's the layer most are missing:

Every time a platform resets your data access, you start the testing cycle over.

Which is exactly what the platform wants. More experimentation spend, less institutional knowledge compounding in your favor.

Your first-party data — the visitors, the behavior signals, the patterns on your own site — doesn't have a retention window.

It's yours to keep.
