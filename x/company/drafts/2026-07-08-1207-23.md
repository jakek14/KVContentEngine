## Post

The average B2B website identifies maybe 10-20% of its visitors at the company level.

Person-level -- name, email, contact info? Under 10%.

So when your analytics says "1,200 sessions this week," you actually know who 120 of those people are. Maybe fewer.

The other 1,000+ came to your site, evaluated what you do, and left. Anonymous. You have no way to follow up.

That's not a traffic problem. That's a data problem.
