## Post

Google changed how Demand Gen campaigns bill. July 15.

If you're running Demand Gen ads on Discover with view-through conversion optimization, billing shifted from CPC to CPM. Automatically. No notification required.

CPC: you pay when someone clicks.
CPM: you pay per thousand impressions -- click or no click.

The change only affects campaigns with view-through conversion optimization enabled. But it applied automatically, which means a lot of advertisers are looking at invoices that changed without explanation.

What to do: pull your Demand Gen campaigns, check billing method on any Discover placements. To keep CPC, disable view-through conversion optimization in campaign settings.

And the visitors who do click through -- do you know who they are?

Because if you're paying CPM, you're paying for every impression. The ones that click and visit your site are worth knowing by name, not just by session count.
