## Post

Google processes over 5 billion searches a day.

93% of the ones using AI Mode end without a single click.

The people who DO click through read the AI summary and still wanted more. They're not browsing. They're already close to a decision.

Volume is down. Intent is up.

Most businesses see traffic decline, panic about the number, and miss what's actually happening underneath it.

Fewer visitors. Higher intent. Every one of them matters more than they did a year ago.

The traffic problem and the conversion problem are the same problem.
