## Post

This stat is worse than it looks.

When leadership pulls brand budget, marketing shifts entirely to performance. All-performance campaigns erode brand recognition over time. Brand erosion makes every paid impression work harder. CAC goes up. Leadership cuts more budget.

The cycle is self-defeating. But it takes 12-18 months to show up in the numbers -- long enough for the wrong lesson to stick.

The companies with the lowest CAC right now didn't get there by doubling performance spend. They built brand before they needed it to convert.
