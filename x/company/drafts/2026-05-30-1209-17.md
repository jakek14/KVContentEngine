## Post

Semrush is right about this.

The buyer's research journey just collapsed into a single AI interaction.

Here's what that means for B2B marketing teams:

Your content doesn't need to win a click from someone doing research anymore.

It needs to be what the AI cites when someone asks "what's the best solution for X?"

That's a completely different optimization target.

The B2B brands getting this right aren't publishing more. They're building content -- original research, clear frameworks, real product data -- that AI treats as authoritative.

Clicks are a lagging indicator.

Citations are the new share of voice.

## Context

Semrush posted that the marketing funnel is being redefined -- what used to be a multi-step research process (search, compare, decide) is now compressed into a single AI interaction. AI agents handle discovery, evaluation, and comparison; users show up as the final arbiter. The company extension: this changes the entire optimization target for B2B content. Old target = win clicks during a research journey. New target = be the source AI cites when forming the answer. The brands building authoritative, citable content (original research, frameworks, verified data) will accumulate AI citations while others optimize for clicks on a journey that's disappearing.
