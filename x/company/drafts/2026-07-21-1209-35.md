## Post

LinkedIn company pages get 5% of feed allocation now.

Personal profiles get 65%.

If your B2B brand's LinkedIn strategy is built around the company page, you're fighting over scraps of a platform that's actively routing around you.

The accounts building real pipeline on LinkedIn in 2026 are people, not logos. The company page amplifies them. It doesn't replace them.

## Context

LinkedIn's June 2026 algorithm update formally codified what had been building for two years: company pages are deprioritized relative to personal profiles by roughly 13:1 in feed allocation. Organic reach for company pages dropped 60-66% since 2024. For B2B companies relying on branded content, the playbook has to shift: executives and team members post as people, company page reshares.
