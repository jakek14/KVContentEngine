## Post

82% of Meta advertisers now use Advantage+.

The algorithm picks the audience. Builds the creative. Sets the bid. All of it.

Most brands are still trying to fight this. That's the wrong direction.

The brands winning on Advantage+ have figured out that you don't control targeting anymore. But you do control the inputs.

The seed audience you feed it. The creative variants in rotation. The conversion events you're optimizing toward. The customer data you bring in.

Advantage+ amplifies whatever you give it. Weak inputs -- anonymous traffic, stale lookalikes, one creative from last quarter -- the algorithm finds more of the same.

The competition is no longer who runs better ads. It's who builds better inputs.
