## Post

The Global Privacy Control is now mandatory in California, Colorado, Connecticut, and Oregon.

It's a browser signal. When a user turns it on, it tells every site they visit to opt them out of data collection and sharing. Automatically. Without filling out a form.

Regulators have already issued seven-figure settlements for ignoring it. Not warnings. Settlements.

If your ad stack is still reading third-party data from visitors who sent a GPC signal, you have a compliance exposure right now -- not a future risk.

The practical move: check whether your consent management platform recognizes and honors GPC. If you don't have one, that's your starting point.

More states are activating in the second half of 2026. This is the direction, not a detour.
