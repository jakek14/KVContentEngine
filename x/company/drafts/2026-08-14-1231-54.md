## Post

A lot of B2B visitor identification tools were built for 2018.

When everyone worked in an office, IP address mapped to employer. Clean data.

Now your prospect is on their home Wi-Fi. Their IP resolves to Comcast, AT&T, or Verizon. The company field comes back blank -- or worse, wrong. You think a telecom is interested in your product.

Remote work broke the core assumption most of these tools run on.

We built differently. Individual-level identification. No IP dependency. It doesn't matter where your visitor is logging in from.

## Reply

## Context

The shift to remote work created a real data quality problem for IP-based visitor identification tools, which were designed when office IP addresses cleanly mapped to employers. This is a fresh problem-awareness angle tied directly to a KV differentiator (people-level vs. company-level identification).
