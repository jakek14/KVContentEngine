## Post

Your July Meta invoice is higher than expected.

Location fees hit July 1:

UK: +2%
France, Italy, Spain: +3%
Austria, Turkey: +5%

Based on where your ads are delivered -- not where your business is registered.

Won't show in Ads Manager. Check your billing hub.

## Context

Meta's new location fees went live July 1, 2026, passing through Digital Services Tax costs in EU/UK markets. Any advertiser delivering ads to those regions -- including US-based brands -- pays the fee on top of their existing CPM. The fees only appear on the invoice, not in Ads Manager, so many advertisers will be caught off guard when reviewing end-of-month billing.
