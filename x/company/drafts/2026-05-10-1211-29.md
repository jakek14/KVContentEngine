## Post

True loyalty hit 29% in 2025.

77% of consumers are pulling their loyalty faster than three years ago.

The industry's response: more points programs, better rewards tiers, gamification.

Hot take: that's the wrong diagnosis.

Customers don't leave because they ran out of points. They leave because the brand never made them feel known.

You remembered their points balance.

You didn't know they came back to your site four times last week without buying.

The retention gap isn't in the incentive structure. It's in the signal you're not reading.

## Context

The 2026 State of Loyalty data shows True Loyalty (demonstrated repeat behavior, not claimed loyalty) declined for the first time in five years. The standard industry response is to build better points programs — but research shows emotional connection (306% higher LTV) matters far more than transactional incentives.
