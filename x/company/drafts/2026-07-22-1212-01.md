## Post

70% of B2B marketers are increasing their first-party data investment this year.

Not because they want another initiative. Because third-party data keeps shrinking.

Here's what first-party actually means for your ad campaigns:

CRM lists seeded into Meta and Google audiences. Email subscribers used to build lookalikes. Behavioral data from your own site powering suppression and retargeting segments.

None of that works if your underlying data is thin.

Which is why the real constraint isn't the advertising platform -- it's how well you know the people who've already interacted with your business. Better audience data produces better campaign outcomes. Every channel, every format.

The brands winning on programmatic in 2026 didn't build better creative first. They built better audience data first.
