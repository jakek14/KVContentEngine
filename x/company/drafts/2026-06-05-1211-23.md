## Post

73% of the B2B buying journey is complete before a buyer contacts your sales team.

That's not a soft metric. It's consistent across every major B2B buyer study in 2026.

By the time someone fills out your demo form, they've already compared you to competitors. They've read your pricing page. They've done the math. The decision is largely made before the hand raise.

Most B2B marketing teams think they have a lead generation problem.

They have a visibility problem.

The buyers are there. They're researching, evaluating, narrowing their list. They just haven't raised their hand yet -- and the moment they do, you're already at the end of the process, not the beginning.

The teams winning in B2B aren't just better at capturing leads.

They're visible earlier in the 73%.
