## Post

Meta removed the off-Meta activity opt-out this month.

That means every website custom audience grew automatically -- without any changes to your campaigns.

More people in your retargeting pool. More impressions available. Cheaper to reach the same funnel.

But here's what didn't change: you still can't tell who those people are.

Your pixel knows someone visited your pricing page at 2:14pm on a Wednesday. Meta can serve them an ad Thursday morning. Neither you nor Meta knows if that visitor was a $5,000-a-month prospect or someone who landed by accident.

Audience size is table stakes. Identity is the moat.
