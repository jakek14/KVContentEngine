## Post

Google Shopping ads now appear when someone photographs a product with Google Lens.

Take a photo. Match to an ad. Land on your site.

That's a new acquisition path that didn't exist two years ago.

And the visitors who arrive that way are already in purchase mode.

They didn't type a vague query. They photographed something they wanted to buy. That's as qualified as a prospect gets before they hit your page.

The problem is the same as every other channel:

Most of them won't fill out a form.

They'll compare prices, read your reviews, check your shipping policy — and leave without a trace.

High intent. No identity. No way to follow up.

That's the traffic problem that hasn't changed, no matter how many new discovery channels appear.
