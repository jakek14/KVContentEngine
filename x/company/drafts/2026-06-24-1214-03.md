## Post

If your Meta conversion numbers dropped in March -- they probably didn't actually drop.

Meta updated how it classifies conversions. Link clicks now tracked separately from likes, shares, saves, and comments. Conversions from non-link interactions moved to a category called "engage-through" -- 1-day window instead of 7.

Billing didn't change. Reach didn't change. The classification changed.

Brands paused budgets. Changed creative. Blamed agencies. Based on a reclassification, not a performance drop.

To check: pull your Ads Manager breakdown and look at link click conversions vs. engage-through conversions. The split shows you where the reported change came from.

The real signal is whether your downstream business outcomes changed -- sales, leads, signups. Not what a column says in Ads Manager.

Platform attribution is always a model. March was a reminder.
