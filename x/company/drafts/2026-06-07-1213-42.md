## Post

67% of B2B marketing teams still run on last-touch attribution.

The average B2B deal involves 27 touchpoints. Buying cycles run 6-12 months.

Last-touch credits one of those touchpoints. Ignores the other 26. Factor in dark funnel activity -- AI search summaries, peer recommendations, community mentions that never generate a click -- and it's ignoring north of 80% of the actual journey.

The model isn't just imprecise. It's actively misleading.

Here's what happens when you build strategy on it: your highest-performing channels look like they underperform because they operate early. Thought leadership, brand content, community presence -- these create the conditions for the final conversion. Last-touch buries them.

So you cut budget from what's building pipeline and redirect it to whatever shows last. Usually a retargeting ad. A branded search term. The kind of conversion that probably would have happened anyway.

The channels doing the real work stay underfunded.

AI search is making this worse. Buyers now do significant research inside ChatGPT and Perplexity before they ever visit your site. No click. No UTM. No session. A new attribution blind spot with nothing underneath it.

Most teams are measuring a smaller fraction of the buyer journey in 2026 than they were two years ago. The attribution report still shows everything's working.
