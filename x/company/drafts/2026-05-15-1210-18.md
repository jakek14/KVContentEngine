## Post

Organic CTR down 34–61% thanks to AI Overviews.

Paid CAC up 18–21%.

Both channels got harder at the same time.

The brands not scrambling already made a different bet: extraction over volume.

When fewer visitors arrive and each one costs more to acquire, making more of what shows up beats chasing more of what doesn't.

The reframe: when both your free channel and your paid channel are simultaneously shrinking or getting more expensive, the only real leverage is extracting more value from existing traffic.
