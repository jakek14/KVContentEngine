## Post

**Tweet 1**
Retargeting used to be automatic.

Third-party cookies tracked your visitors everywhere they went.

You could follow up. Run ads. Stay top of mind.

That infrastructure is gone.

**Tweet 2**
Chrome is deprecating third-party cookies. Safari and Firefox already block them.

Meta's pixel still works — but iOS privacy changes cut signal accuracy by 30–40%.

Google's cross-site tracking has been restricted in Chrome since 2025.

Retargeting audiences are quietly shrinking.

**Tweet 3**
The brands still running clean retargeting campaigns in 2026 all have one thing in common.

They built their own audiences.

First-party data. CRM lists. Email subscribers with explicit consent.

You can't rent your way to a durable audience anymore.

**Tweet 4**
The math:

CRM list retargeting: 70–80% match rates.

Cookie-based retargeting pools: shrinking 20–30% per year as browser restrictions compound.

One asset grows. One decays.

Build the one that compounds.

**Tweet 5**
Your retargeting strategy is only as good as your owned data.

If you don't know who your visitors are, you can't follow up when they leave.

That's not a platform problem.

It's a data problem.
