## Post

Google is removing first-click, linear, time-decay, and position-based attribution from Google Ads this month.

All four. Gone.

Replaced with data-driven attribution -- where Google's machine learning decides which touchpoints get credit for a conversion.

If your team benchmarked performance against those models, that comparison breaks. The historical baseline disappears. And you're now relying on Google's math to tell you what worked.

That's not a neutral upgrade. It's Google becoming the referee in a game you're also playing.

The teams that won't feel this: the ones already measuring revenue, pipeline, and cost-per-acquisition against first-party signals.

The ones who built their entire reporting stack around platform attribution are about to have a rough quarter.

Build your measurement foundation around what you own.

## Context

Google announced mid-July removal of legacy attribution models from Google Ads. This is a significant change that affects every marketer using those models for benchmarking. Strong hot take for the company page -- it's happening in real time and has immediate implications for KV's audience (marketers, SaaS teams, DTC brands running paid).
