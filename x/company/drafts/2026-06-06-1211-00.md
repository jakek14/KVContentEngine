## Post

Most teams that set up visitor identification stop at the report.

They see the list. Company names, job titles, visit counts. Then nothing.

The top teams use it differently.

They don't treat it as a lead list. They treat it as a timing layer. A company that visits your pricing page three times in a week isn't cold outreach -- that's a warm conversation waiting to happen. The visit data tells you when to reach out, not just who.

They also feed it back into content. Which companies are hitting your blog? Your help docs? Your feature pages? That pattern tells you what problems your best-fit accounts are actually trying to solve.

Most teams are still optimizing for the 1.4% who fill out a form. The smarter move is building a system for the other 98.6%.

That's how we built KnownVisitors to work.
