## Post

**Tweet 1**
83% of B2B buyers complete 70% of their research before they ever contact sales.

By the time someone fills out your form, the decision is almost made.

The real research already happened.

It happened on your site.

**Tweet 2**
They read your pricing page.

Compared your features against a competitor in another tab.

Came back twice in 48 hours.

Spent 8 minutes on your case study.

None of that left a trace you could act on.

**Tweet 3**
The highest-intent moment a B2B buyer has isn't the demo.

It's not the sales call.

It's the anonymous research phase before they raise their hand.

That's when the decision is actually forming.

**Tweet 4**
Most marketing teams optimize for the form fill.

The form fill is a lagging indicator.

The real signal is what visitors do before they convert — or before they leave without converting.

That data exists. Most teams can't act on it.

**Tweet 5**
If 83% of the buying decision happens in the research phase, then 83% of your opportunity is in understanding that research.

Most companies only start paying attention at 100%.

The window is earlier than you think.
