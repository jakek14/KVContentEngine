## Post

Cart abandonment is treated as an ecommerce problem.

It's actually a lead gen problem.

The average site loses 70% of cart sessions before checkout. A fraction of those visitors get abandoned cart emails -- but only the ones who were logged in or had already given you their email address.

The anonymous visitor who spent 12 minutes browsing, built a cart, and left? Gone. You never knew their name. You can't email them. You'll pay to reach them again with retargeting ads -- maybe.

Most of your highest-intent visitors are invisible to your re-engagement workflows.

We built KnownVisitors for exactly this. Identify who came to your site before they leave -- name, email, company, phone -- so your abandoned cart isn't just a sequence. It's a list of real people you can actually reach.
