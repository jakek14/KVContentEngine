## Post

June 30 is a real deadline.

Meta Conversions API. Amazon Ad Tag. UK and EEA traffic. Verified consent signals required before then.

After that date, attribution breaks for anyone who hasn't set this up.

Most B2B teams assume this is a consumer ad problem.

The regulators don't make that distinction.
