## Post

Your website probably gets more visits in a day than your sales team has conversations in a month.

Most B2B companies track it like a billboard.

Sessions. Bounce rate. Time on page. Numbers that tell you something happened but not who it happened to.

The gap between "someone visited our pricing page" and knowing their name, company, and role -- that's pipeline.

Most teams are running full-spend campaigns on the front end of this equation. Paid search, SEO, content, outbound -- all to drive visits. The back end, turning those visits into identifiable contacts, gets treated like a nice-to-have.

The math doesn't hold. Traffic you can't identify is just a cost center.
