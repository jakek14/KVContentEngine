## Post

ARR growth hides a lot.

Two B2B companies, both growing 30% year over year. One is replacing every churned account with new logos. The other is growing existing accounts faster than it loses any.

Same headline number. Two completely different businesses.

NRR above 110% means existing customers are spending more than you're losing. That compounds. The company isn't filling a leaky bucket -- it's building on top of a full one.

Above 120%, the base starts to grow itself. Acquisition becomes optional, not existential.

Most teams optimize for new logos and track NRR as an afterthought. The ones scaling efficiently have it backwards.
