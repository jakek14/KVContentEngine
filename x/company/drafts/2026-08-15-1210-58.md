## Post

One study of a billion marketing emails: only 44% were actually delivered.

The other 56% were blocked by spam filters before anyone saw them.

Most teams spend weeks optimizing subject lines, copy, and sequences. Almost none audit whether emails are landing in the inbox first.

Three things that move the needle most:

Authenticate your domain. SPF, DKIM, DMARC -- it's not optional anymore. Domains without proper authentication see inbox placement around 44%. With full authentication, it's closer to 89%. That gap is your entire deliverability gap.

Keep your list clean. Every hard bounce and spam complaint drags your sender score down. Clean the list regularly or you'll pay for it in placement.

Send from a subdomain. If deliverability takes a hit, it's isolated. Your main domain stays clean.

Most email problems aren't copy problems. They're infrastructure problems.
