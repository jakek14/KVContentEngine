## Post

Google is pulling first-click, linear, time-decay, and position-based attribution from Ads this month.

Data-driven is the default from here.

If top-of-funnel content has been getting credit in your model, the story is about to change.

Worth auditing your setup before Google resets it for you.

## Context

Google is removing four legacy attribution models from all Google Ads accounts in mid-July 2026 -- first-click, linear, time-decay, and position-based. Only last-click and data-driven attribution remain. This is significant for B2B companies that used those models to credit awareness and brand content at the top of funnel.
