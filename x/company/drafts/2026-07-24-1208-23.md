## Post

Meta killed "reach" and "impressions" on June 15.

The replacements are Media Views and Media Viewers.

If your reporting hasn't been updated, you're measuring metrics that no longer exist.

Worth auditing before your next client call or board deck.

## Context

Meta's June 15 deprecation of legacy reach and impression metrics is causing widespread confusion among advertisers. Many teams are still reporting on the old metrics without realizing the definitions changed underneath them. This is a sharp, timely short that provides immediate actionable value for any B2B or e-commerce company running Meta ads.
