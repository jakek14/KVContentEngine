## Post

AI SaaS tools under $50/mo: 23% gross revenue retention.

Established SaaS: 85%+.

Before signing up for any new software this year — one question worth asking:

Are other teams still using it at month 6?

## Context

2026 SaaS benchmark data shows a dramatic retention split between cheap AI tools and established products. The "AI tourist" effect: companies spin up throwaway AI tools quickly, evaluate briefly, and abandon. 23% GRR means most customers are gone within a year. For B2B buyers in 2026, month-6 retention data is the most honest signal about whether a tool delivers real value vs. novelty.
