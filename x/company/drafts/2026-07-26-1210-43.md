## Post

Leads contacted within 5 minutes of form submission convert exponentially higher than leads reached an hour later.

Most B2B companies have a process where the rep finishes what they're doing first.

The CRM looks full. The close rate is quiet about why.

Response speed is a distribution advantage. Barely anyone treats it like one.
