## Post

Google expanded Smart Bidding Exploration globally this week.

18% more unique converting query categories. 19% more conversions on average, per their data.

The algorithm gets better at finding demand you're not capturing. That part is real.

But Smart Bidding is a signal machine. It optimizes toward audiences that look like the ones you've already converted. Feed it good data and it compounds. Feed it weak data and it finds more people who look like visitors who never converted.

Most accounts are seeded with anonymous traffic -- people who browsed, looked around, and left without ever identifying themselves. The algorithm learned from that pool. The output is more of the same.

The brands pulling away on Performance Max right now can tell the algorithm: here's who actually converted. Here's what they looked like before they bought. Find more people like that.

That requires knowing who was on your site before they converted.

That's what we built KnownVisitors for.
