## Post

New CCPA regulations took effect January 1, 2026.

Most B2B marketing teams haven't read them yet.

The changes that matter to anyone running lead gen: mandatory privacy risk assessments before deploying new marketing technology, new data-sharing requirements for cookies and pixels, and expanded opt-out rights for data broker data.

The practical translation: using third-party data tools without documented consent processes isn't a privacy gray area anymore. It's an active compliance exposure.
