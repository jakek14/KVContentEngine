## Post

The EU AI Act hit full enforcement yesterday.

Most B2B operators are treating it like a compliance headline. It's not.

If your marketing stack uses AI to score leads, auto-personalize content, or profile EU visitors behaviorally -- full enforcement means regulators can investigate and fine. Not threaten to. Actually do.

High-risk AI systems now need formal documentation and registration. Forbidden practices -- social scoring, biometric surveillance -- are banned without exceptions. Transparency requirements mean users can demand explanations for automated decisions about them.

The companies that built compliant data practices before yesterday already have the advantage.
