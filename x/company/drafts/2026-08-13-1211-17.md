## Post

Meta Q2 2026: average price per ad up 12% year over year.

More advertisers. Same inventory. The auction is getting expensive.

But two advertisers in the same auction can pay very different effective CPMs -- and it's mostly creative that separates them.

Meta prices impressions on predicted engagement. When your ad earns higher relevance scores, the algorithm discounts your cost compared to the next buyer in the queue.

Two things that move this consistently:

Open with a pattern interrupt in the first two seconds. Most people scroll past ads that look like ads.

Run early tests against narrow audiences. Specificity improves predicted action rates. The algorithm needs wins to learn from, and a broad initial audience slows that down.

The CPM floor is rising. Creative quality determines how far above it you land.
