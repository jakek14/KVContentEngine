## Post

The list you bought got you to the right company.

It doesn't tell you if that company is actively looking.

Most B2B outbound fails not because the target is wrong -- but because the timing is. A prospect who matches your ICP perfectly but isn't in evaluation mode is just a cold contact. Hit them six months later, right after they expanded the team or had a new hire land in the exact role you serve? Different conversation entirely.

The gap between "decent outbound" and "outbound that actually converts" in 2026 is almost entirely timing.

The teams closing fastest aren't sending better emails. They're sending at the right moment -- triggered by a funding round, a job posting, a leadership change, a competitor mention in a press release.

Firmographics gets you to the right account. Signal gets you there when it matters.

Most companies have the first. Almost none have the second.
