## Post

Meta changed two things in July that B2B teams should pay attention to.

First: Lead Ads now book appointments directly. Prospect submits the form, picks a time, lands on the calendar. The gap between "interested" and "first conversation" just got a lot shorter.

Second: Conversion API setup is now one click. No engineer. No code. Zero technical knowledge required. The last excuse for running Meta ads without proper server-side tracking is gone.

Both changes go after the same problem -- speed and accuracy. How fast does a qualified lead turn into a real conversation, and does your tracking actually capture what's driving it.

B2B teams still running Meta without Conversion API are working with incomplete conversion data. That's now a fixable problem in about 60 seconds.

Worth updating your setup this week.
