## Post

Meta's location fee is now live.

If you're serving ads in the UK or EU, you're paying it -- on top of your campaign budget. Austria and Turkey at 5%, France, Italy, and Spain at 3%, UK at 2%.

At $50k/month in affected markets, that's an extra $1,000 to $2,500 per month. Before you've reached a single additional customer.

The thing we see most ad managers getting wrong right now: Meta's budget optimization doesn't factor in location fees. Your target spend levels are off. Manually adjust campaign budgets for each affected country or your ROAS reporting will be wrong for Q3.

Google did this in 2020. Amazon in 2024. Meta is just the last major platform to catch up.

The fee is manageable. Not knowing about it is not.
