## Post

Meta just launched Brand Memory -- an AI layer that studies your historical ad creative and applies that brand identity to everything it generates going forward.

This changes the creative production equation.

The floor is rising. Every advertiser using the suite gets better baseline creative automatically. That used to be a differentiator for brands with big creative teams. It won't be for long.

What actually separates advertisers now: how clean and consistent your brand signal is in your historical creative. The AI trains on what you've already made. If your creative has been scattered, the model inherits that.

The brands with a clear point of view and consistent creative history will pull ahead. The ones who've been throwing variations at the wall are going to get average output at scale.

Your historical ads are training data now.

Act like it.
