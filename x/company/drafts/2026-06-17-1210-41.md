## Post

Amazon: $60B in ad revenue this year.

Not from better ads. From owning the data.

Amazon knows who searched, who clicked, who bought, and what they bought next. That closed-loop visibility is what brands are spending billions to access.

They're not buying ads. They're buying data clarity they never built for themselves.
