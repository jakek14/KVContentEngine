## Post

Meta changed how it counts clicks in March 2026.

If your reported conversions dropped around that time, this is probably why.

Click-through attribution now requires an actual link click -- the kind that sends someone to a destination. Likes, saves, shares? Those moved to a new bucket called "engage-through," with a one-day window instead of seven.

Here's the part that catches people: under the old model, if someone saved your ad on day two and converted on day six, Meta counted that as a click-through conversion. Under the new model, it doesn't qualify for either bucket. It disappears from your reporting entirely.

Your total sales probably didn't drop. Your reported conversions did.

Check your engage-through column before cutting campaigns that look like they're underperforming.
