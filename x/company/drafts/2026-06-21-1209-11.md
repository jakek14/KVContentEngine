## Post

The average D2C brand knows their CAC to the dollar.

Ask them their 90-day retention by cohort. Different story.

Ecommerce investors in 2026 require cohort retention proof before writing a check. Not because it's a nice metric. Because CAC tells you what you spent. Retention tells you what you actually built.

Most brands can't show that proof because they can't trace a customer back to their pre-purchase site behavior. They know someone bought. They don't know what that person was doing on their site for the two weeks before they bought -- how many times they came back, which pages they hit, what finally closed them.

That gap between visitor and customer is exactly where the retention analysis lives.

How are you closing it?
