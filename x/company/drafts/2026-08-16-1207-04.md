## Post

Your site hit 10,000 visitors last month.

More than 5,000 of them were bots.

Bots don't buy. They don't fill out forms. They skew your conversion rate, inflate your session counts, and make your funnel look bigger than it is.

The humans in your traffic are rarer and more valuable than your analytics dashboard suggests.

## Context

Research from 2026 confirmed that bot traffic has tipped past 50% of all internet traffic for the first time, with AI agents, crawlers, and automated tools driving the shift. This is a sharp reframe for any company that reports on web traffic -- the number is a lot noisier than it looks.
