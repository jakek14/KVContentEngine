## Post

Google updated its Ads Terms of Service on July 1st for the first time in 8 years.

Auto-applied to every account. No login prompt. No opt-in required.

The new terms give Google explicit legal authority to use AI to generate your targets, ads, and destinations.

You're still responsible for everything it creates.

Most advertisers have no idea this happened.
