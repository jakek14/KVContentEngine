## Post

68% of Google searches now end without a click to any website.

Not a rumor. SparkToro tracked over 100 million queries across the first four months of 2026. The number held.

Organic traffic is shrinking structurally -- not because your SEO broke. Because the search engine stopped sending it.

The visits that still come through are higher intent than ever. Someone who clicked in a 68%-zero-click environment made a real choice.

They still land as an anonymous session.

Traffic is getting more selective and less visible at the same time. That's the problem worth solving.
