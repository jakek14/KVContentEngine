## Post

ICONIQ surveyed 150+ B2B software leaders.

The companies pulling ahead in 2026 aren't outspending their competitors.

They're outoperating them.

The pattern: running both top-down and bottom-up GTM at the same time. Rethinking pricing instead of defending it. Embedding AI in the funnel instead of adding it as a feature.

The companies staying flat are still waiting for ad spend to recover or for a macro tailwind to bail them out.

It won't.

The advantage in 2026 is operational. The tools are there. The question is whether you're using them.
