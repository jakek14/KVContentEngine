## Post

Most B2B companies pay $40-80k a year for intent data.

Here's what doesn't get mentioned in the sales call: every competitor in your space bought the same list.

The account that "showed intent" for your category this week also received outreach from six other vendors this week. Same signal. Same timing. By the time you follow up, you're the fifth email in their inbox about a decision they haven't made yet.

Intent data tells you who's researching your category. It doesn't tell you who came to your website.

The difference between "someone is researching this space" and "someone actually visited your pricing page" is the entire ballgame.

The first signal is sold to everyone. The second one is only yours.
