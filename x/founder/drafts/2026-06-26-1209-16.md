## Post

Cursor hit $2B ARR in 3 years.

That number matters less than what it proves.

Three years ago, building serious software meant engineers. Good ones. And those engineers needed other engineers. A team of 8 before your first customer.

That math is gone.

The bottleneck for building used to be "can you write code." Now it's "do you understand the problem better than anyone else in the room." That's a different kind of advantage -- one that was always there in founding teams, but never drove the ship this directly.

I built KnownVisitors because I understood what anonymous website traffic actually costs businesses. Not because I had a 10-person engineering team. The AI filled the build gap. The insight was always the asset.

The era where "I'm not technical" was a real excuse ended a while back.
