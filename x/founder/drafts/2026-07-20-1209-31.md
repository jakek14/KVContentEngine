## Post

"Nobody does this. Everybody knows this."

That last line is the whole thing.

Charging on day one terrifies most founders not because they don't believe the advice -- but because charging requires committing to a specific problem. And most early products are still too broad to price.

When you can't name a price, the real problem isn't courage. It's specificity.

"Marketing software" doesn't have a price. "Automated retargeting for Shopify stores doing $50k-$500k per month" has one. If you can't answer "who exactly is this for and what does it replace" -- you can't charge on day one because you don't have a day one yet.
