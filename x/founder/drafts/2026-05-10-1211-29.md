## Post

Meta just opened its AI Business Assistant to every advertiser on Facebook and Instagram.

Give it your URL and your budget. It handles the creative, the targeting, the bids, the placements. All of it.

No campaign setup. No audience research. No creative testing. Automated.

Here's what I keep thinking about:

If every brand is running the same AI, on the same platform, with the same optimization logic — where does the edge actually come from?

Execution used to be the moat. You had to know how to structure campaigns, test creative, read attribution, build audience segments. That took real skill.

Now the AI does all of that by default.

I don't think this kills differentiation. I think it relocates it.

The brands with clear positioning, a real understanding of their customer, and a specific problem they actually solve — the AI can take that story and optimize it very effectively.

The brands that are vague? No amount of automation solves a positioning problem.

Genuine question: if you're running paid social, has the shift toward AI automation changed how you think about strategy vs. execution?
