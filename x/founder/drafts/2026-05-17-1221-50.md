## Post

The VC math on SaaS just got brutally clear.

AI startup deals: $51M average.
Non-AI startup deals: $4.7M average.

Same year. Same market. A 10x gap.

TK is right that SaaS is back — the business fundamentals didn't change. But the funding market bifurcated sharply.

Most founders not building AI-first are staring at that gap and wondering what they did wrong. Nothing. They just opted out of a different lottery.

The real implication: if you need a VC's attention to validate your direction, you're building for the wrong audience. The founders getting $4.7M checks built something the market wanted before they went fundraising.

Build revenue first. Then decide if capital is a lever worth pulling.

That's the SaaS playbook that still works.
