## Post

7-day free trials convert at 40%.

60-day free trials convert at 30%.

Founders keep extending trials to "give people more time." Every extra day removes urgency. Urgency is what drives action.

The product doesn't need more time. The onboarding does.

## Context

Product-led growth data shows a clear counterintuitive result on trial length: shorter trials with higher-urgency onboarding dramatically outperform generous long trials. Most founders get this backwards -- they optimize trial length when they should be optimizing time-to-first-value. Hot-take pillar, short format.
