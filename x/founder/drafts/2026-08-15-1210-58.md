## Post

Google just told every advertiser: opt out before September 1 or we're converting your campaigns to AI Max.

Not a suggestion. An email. Already sent.

Specifically: any Search campaign using automatically created assets or campaign-level broad match gets migrated automatically.

If you want to stay in control of the timeline, here's what to do now:

-- Go into your Google Ads account and check for campaigns with "automatically created assets" or "campaign-level broad match" enabled

-- If you want manual control: turn those features off before September 1

-- If you want to test AI Max on your terms: flip it on yourself now so you're not learning what changed while spend is running

The migration itself isn't necessarily bad. AI Max finds more intent signals than standard broad match. The accounts that get hurt are the ones who get auto-upgraded with no plan and spend a month figuring out why their numbers moved.

Run it on your own schedule.

## Context

Google announced in early August 2026 that broad match and ACA campaigns would be auto-upgraded to AI Max on September 1, 2026 -- affecting all advertisers who haven't already opted in. Dynamic Search Ads got an extension to February 2027 after pushback. This is actionable free-game content with a hard deadline.
