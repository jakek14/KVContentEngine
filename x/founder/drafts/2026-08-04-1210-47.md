## Post

Giving up being first was the right call.

Karri built one of the best product companies of the last decade. The deliberate approach he describes -- understand the actual workflows before shipping the AI -- is what separates products that compound from ones that get deprecated in six months.

The pressure to move fast with AI is real. But most urgency around "we need an AI feature now" is distribution anxiety, not customer need. It's the fear of watching a competitor announce something before you do.

Founders who shipped deliberate and timed it right all started from the same place: a clear view of the actual problem, not a technology-first instinct.

The best AI product decisions come from that clarity. Not from the race to announce.
