## Post

The "seat" used to be the natural unit of SaaS value.

More users = more revenue. Renewals were straightforward. Expansion meant adding headcount.

That logic worked when humans were the ones doing the work.

Keith's right -- AI agents don't buy seats. They replace them. A single agent running 24/7 might do the work of a dozen users who each paid $150/month. You can't price that with per-user math.

The question every SaaS founder needs to answer right now: does your pricing model capture the value you're actually delivering -- or is it still pricing the old version of who does the work?

Per-seat has a limited shelf life when the seat doesn't have to be a person.
