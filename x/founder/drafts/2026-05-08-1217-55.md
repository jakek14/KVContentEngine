## Post

The "everyone's an AI founder" era of 2025 did something useful.

It forced every founder to answer a harder question.

Do you have a real business — or just a story that works in a partner meeting?

The founders who stayed disciplined during the hype: same product, same customers, same focus.

The ones chasing the label: new deck, new positioning, same fundamentals they'd always avoided building.

Now that "AI first" isn't a magic pass, you can see clearly who was building and who was storytelling.

The best founders I know don't lead with an AI story. They lead with a customer story.

AI is just how they build it faster.
