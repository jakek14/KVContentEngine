## Post

This is exactly right. And the first one is the hardest.

"Go where your users already complain" sounds obvious. It's not. Most founders survey users or read competitor reviews. That's fine. But the real signal is in the conversations happening before anyone knows there's a solution.

Before we built KnownVisitors, we spent a long time in the rooms where marketers were frustrated. Not about tools. About the feeling of running paid traffic, watching people browse their site, and having no idea who any of them were.

No tool existed they were complaining about. The pain existed. Massive gap.

Every item on this list is a customer-discovery principle dressed up as a go-to-market principle.

They're the same thing.
