## Post

TK's right, and the gap between $1M and $3M is where most companies quietly stall.

Getting to $1M means you found something that works.

Getting to $3M means you built a system that finds customers without you personally closing every deal.

Those require completely different skills.

At $1M, you're selling with founder energy. You know every customer. Every objection. You win because you care more than the competition and move faster.

At $3M, that stops scaling. You need a repeatable GTM motion, a team that can execute without your presence on every call, and a CAC/payback model that can absorb the cost of a real sales process.

Most founders try to run the $3M playbook with the $1M skill set.

Then they wonder why growth flattens.

The transition isn't about working harder. It's about stepping back from the thing that got you here and building the machine that gets you there.
