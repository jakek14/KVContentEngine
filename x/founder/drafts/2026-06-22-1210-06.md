## Post

The numbers are real. The sequence is the thing.

Rob hit $61K MRR in 53 days on his second SaaS -- same system as the first. Content to email to webinar. Three steps, same order every time.

Most founders see that outcome and try to speed-run it. They grab the content angle. Skip building the list. Jump straight to the close.

The math doesn't work that way. Each step is filtering for the next one. Content surfaces people who care. Email moves the interested ones closer. The webinar converts whoever showed up after both steps. Pull out the middle and you're presenting to a cold room.

That's why the same playbook produces wildly different results depending on who runs it. The tactics are available to everyone. The sequencing discipline isn't.
