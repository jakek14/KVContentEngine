## Post

ChatGPT referral traffic converts at 7.1%.

For context -- paid search is 7.8%. Email is around 4%. Social is closer to 2%.

AI search isn't an experimental channel. It's performing like paid search. Without the ad spend.

But here's the gap most founders aren't thinking about: SEO and AEO are completely different games.

SEO puts you on a results page. You're one of ten blue links. The user decides whether to click.

AEO puts you in the answer. The AI responds with information, cites a source or two, and the question is resolved. Either you're the brand that gets cited, or you aren't. There's no page 2.

37% of product discovery queries now start in AI chatbots. Buyers are asking "what's the best tool for X" directly into ChatGPT before they ever touch a search bar.

What determines whether AI cites you?

Consistent, structured, extractable content across multiple surfaces -- your own site, LinkedIn, Reddit, review platforms, third-party coverage. LLMs form "preferences" based on patterns across everything they've been trained on. If your brand appears consistently alongside the right category terms and customer problems, the model starts treating you as a relevant answer.

The founders who figured this out in 2024 are the ones AI is recommending now.

The founders who don't figure it out in 2026 will wonder why they're invisible to a third of all research sessions.
