## Post

Third-party cookies didn't die. Google reversed course.

But that's not actually good news.

Safari blocks them by default. Chrome's new consent prompts mean a growing slice of your traffic never sends them at all. Reversing the deprecation timeline doesn't fix any of that.

The first-party data imperative didn't go away. It just lost its countdown clock.

The brands figuring this out aren't waiting for better cookie policy. They're building first-party data directly -- capturing intent signals from visitors who actually showed up and looked around.

People who come to your site, browse, and leave without filling out a form are the warmest first-party signal you have. You just need to identify them before they go.

That's exactly what I built KnownVisitors to do.
