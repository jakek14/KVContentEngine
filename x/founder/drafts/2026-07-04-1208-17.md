## Post

The companies accelerating in B2B right now have something in common: they know more about their leads than their leads expect them to.

MQL-to-SQL conversion fell from 13.1% to 9.8% last year. A lot of teams are treating that as a sales problem. It's not.

It's a signal problem. Volume at the top of funnel went up. The ability to identify who's actually in-market -- before the handoff -- went down.

The teams still posting strong numbers fixed the signal problem first. Everything else flowed from that.
