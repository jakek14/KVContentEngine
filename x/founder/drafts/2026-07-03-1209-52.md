## Post

Most bootstrapped founders get this backwards.

They build the product. Then they go find the audience.

The ones who win did the opposite. They built the audience first -- or found where their audience already lived -- then built the product for that specific group.

The difference isn't luck. It's distribution solved before launch instead of after.

Hard truth: an audience of 500 people who actually trust you is worth more than a product that 50,000 people have never heard of.

Build the relationship first. The product fills the demand you already created.

## Context

H2 2026 is underway. The bootstrapping research shows a clear pattern among winners -- audience-first strategy precedes product launch. This is practical free game that founders can act on immediately. Breaks from the "build it and they will come" default that most early-stage founders fall into.
