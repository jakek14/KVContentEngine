## Post

New logo CAC just crossed $2.00 per dollar of ARR.

You spend $2 in sales and marketing to land $1 of new revenue.

The number most people aren't talking about: blended CAC ratio dropped to $1.40. Down from $1.61 last year.

The gap between $2.00 and $1.40 is expansion revenue.

Companies with strong NRR are subsidizing their new logo costs with upsells, cross-sells, and expansion from existing customers. That's what's keeping the unit economics workable in 2026.

If your net revenue retention is below 100%, you're on a treadmill. Every dollar you acquire has to compensate for what's walking out. More new logos don't fix that -- they just slow the drain.

The companies with sustainable unit economics this year aren't the most efficient acquirers.

They're the ones whose customers grow.

That's a product problem. An onboarding problem. A customer success problem. Dressed up as a marketing problem.
