## Post

63% of people building with vibe coding tools aren't developers.

Think about what that means.

For two decades we told non-technical founders they needed a co-founder, a developer, or funding to hire one. The technical requirement was a real filter. It shaped who got to start what.

That filter is dissolving.

The vibe coding market hit $4.7B and is growing 38% a year. 25% of YC's last batch shipped products where 95% of the code was AI-generated. One solo founder documented going from $0 to $10K MRR in under a year with AI tools handling most of the production work.

The moat is shifting.

Execution used to mean technical execution. Now it means knowing the customer well enough to build the right thing -- and moving faster than everyone else once you have that answer.

Business judgment doesn't compress the way code does. That's where the actual leverage is.

I don't think most of the startup world has caught up to this yet.
