## Post

The fastest adopters of vibe coding aren't engineers.

It's founders who never wrote a line of code. Nothing to unlearn. No opinions about architecture or the right way to structure a project. They describe what they want, iterate until it works, and ship.

That's actually an edge.

Engineers come in with habits and strong preferences about HOW to build. Non-technical founders only care about the output. They're closer to the spec -- they ARE the spec.

41% of global code is now AI-generated. The tooling was built for developers. But the people extracting the most leverage from it might not be developers at all.

## Context

LogRocket AI dev tool power rankings for June 2026 show Claude Opus 4.7 at the top among models. The most interesting trend isn't which model is winning -- it's that non-technical users (founders, operators, marketers) are adopting these tools faster than many engineering teams. They have no prior workflow to protect. Scientific American and Stormy.ai both noted this pattern in recent coverage of Claude Code reaching non-developers.
