## Post

B2B buyers are shortlisting tools in ChatGPT and Perplexity before they ever open Google.

That shift means the question isn't "how do I rank for [category] software" anymore. It's "does the AI know my product exists -- and think it's worth recommending?"

The same fundamentals that built SEO authority still work: content, citations, being written about in credible places. The destination changed. That content now needs to reach LLM context, not just Google's index.

The founders paying attention are building for both.
