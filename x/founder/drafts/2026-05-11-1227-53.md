## Post

Growth rate is no longer the headline SaaS metric.

The new gates:
CAC payback under 12 months.
LTV:CAC minimum 4:1.
Monthly churn under 2%.

Most founders learn these numbers from an investor mid-process.

Don't let that be you.

## Context
