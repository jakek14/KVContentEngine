Google CPC: up 18% year-over-year.

The typical reaction is to optimize which platform you buy on.

That's missing the real problem.

Both platforms have the same fundamental issue — you're renting traffic. And rent goes up every year on every platform without exception.

The answer isn't rotating budget. It's extracting more from the traffic you're already paying for.
