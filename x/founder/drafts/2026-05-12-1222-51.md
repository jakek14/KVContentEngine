## Post

The Tibo story keeps getting framed as an "AI founder success story."

That's right. But it undersells the harder part.

Plenty of founders have the same AI tools. Very few have $1M/month.

The tools removed one bottleneck. They didn't install the judgment about what to build, who it's for, or whether anyone will pay.

What actually explains that outcome: ship fast, charge on day one, kill things that don't work, build distribution over time.

AI made the shipping faster. It didn't make the judgment sharper.

Solo founders winning right now share the same actual edge: ruthless about validation before investment. AI just means validation is cheaper and faster than ever before.

The bottleneck isn't building anymore.

It's knowing what's worth building.

That clarity is still entirely on you.
