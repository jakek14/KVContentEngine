## Post

The martech landscape has 15,505 products.

It barely grew this year. 0.79%. First time in 15 years the number held nearly flat.

And underneath that flat headline: content marketing tools just had their biggest outflow ever. 176 products cut from the category in a single year.

This is what AI consolidation actually looks like. It doesn't expand a category -- it eats the commodity layers of it. Any tool doing a task AI now handles natively? Gone or going.

The ones surviving have one of two things: defensible data the AI needs to function, or workflow lock-in so deep that switching costs make replacement painful.

If your SaaS doesn't have either of those, the next few years are going to get uncomfortable.
