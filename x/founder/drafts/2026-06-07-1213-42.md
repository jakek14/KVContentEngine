## Post

**Tweet 1**
The median B2B SaaS company now takes 18 months to recover its customer acquisition cost. In 2021 it was 11.

**Tweet 2**
That's not just a number getting worse. It's the business model getting harder to defend.

**Tweet 3**
When payback was 11 months, you could grow fast and the math still worked. At 18 months, every dollar you spend on acquisition is tied up for a year and a half before you break even. Burn-funded land grabs don't survive this.

**Tweet 4**
The gap between median and top quartile tells the story. Companies at 6-8 months payback aren't necessarily getting cheaper traffic. They're extracting more value from what they already have -- better activation, faster time-to-value, expansion revenue kicking in earlier.

**Tweet 5**
Top-quartile SaaS now gets 40-50% of new ARR from expanding existing accounts. Not new logos. Existing customers who got enough value to buy more.

**Tweet 6**
Founders who figure this out stop asking 'how do we get more leads' and start asking 'what happens to the leads we already have.' That shift is worth more than any new channel.
