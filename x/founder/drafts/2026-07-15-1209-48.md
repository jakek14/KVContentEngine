## Post

"Stop guessing at marketing. Start engineering demand."

The hard part: most founders think publishing content = engineered demand. It's not.

Engineered demand is a closed loop. You create something valuable, it reaches the right people, and then you know who responded. Not "someone visited." Who, specifically.

Content without identification is still guessing. You published something. Someone came. You still don't know who.

The loop closes when you can say: this brought in 43 qualified visitors. Here are the 17 companies. Here are the 8 people.

That's engineering. Everything else is broadcasting.
