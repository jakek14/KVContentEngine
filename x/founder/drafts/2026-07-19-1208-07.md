## Post

58% of SaaS companies run some form of PLG.

Only 34% track activation.

Activation is the only metric that actually predicts whether a free user ever pays. Not sign-ups. Not trial starts. Whether they hit the moment where the product clicked for them.

Without that number, you're running product-led growth blind. You can optimize onboarding, improve the trial UX, add in-app tooltips -- and have no idea if any of it moves the needle. Because you never defined what the needle is.

The companies growing most efficiently know their activation moment cold. They know which specific actions, in which sequence, correlate with conversion. Everything else in the funnel points back to that.

If you can't answer "what does an activated user look like in our product" -- you're not running PLG. You're running a free trial.

## Context

2026 PLG benchmarks show a sharp gap: most SaaS companies have adopted some kind of product-led motion, but only a third track activation.
