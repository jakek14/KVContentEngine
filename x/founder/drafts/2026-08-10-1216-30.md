## Post

The EU AI Act's transparency rules went live August 2.

Most founders filed it under "not my problem" and moved on.

Worth reading before your legal team has to.

Article 50 doesn't just cover the headline high-risk systems. It applies to any AI-generated or AI-altered content. Chatbots have to disclose they're AI. Marketing emails and ads created with generative tools need machine-readable disclosure markers. Deepfakes and altered images require explicit labels.

If you have EU customers -- and most B2B SaaS companies do -- this is live now.

Fine for non-compliance: up to €15M or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.

Your legal team needs to know. And you need to know your marketing team is using AI to produce content at scale.
