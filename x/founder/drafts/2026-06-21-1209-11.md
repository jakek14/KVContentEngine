## Post

"SaaS is back" is the 2026 headline.

But the companies worth looking at never went anywhere.

They didn't rebrand to "AI company" because they didn't have to. NRR above 110%. Expansion from existing accounts. Customers who stayed and spent more year over year.

The "AI company" label in 2025 was, in many cases, a fundraising play. Dress the business up in the vocabulary investors were rewarding that quarter. The underlying model -- recurring revenue, high gross margins, retention -- was already doing or not doing what it needed to do.

TK is right. SaaS is back. But the more accurate way to say it: the efficient business is back in fashion.

It never stopped working.
