## Post

Raising VC money too early is mistake #2 on John's list of 99.

He's right. But the WHY deserves a longer look.

It's not just that early capital "shifts focus." It changes the definition of success. Now success is hitting the metric that unlocks the next check. You start building for demonstrations, not for value.

The customers who can tell you if you've built something real get deprioritized. The investors who need you moving fast stay central.

By the time you figure it out, you've got cap table complexity, runway pressure, and a product built around what fundraising required -- not what customers needed.

The founders who raise at the right moment usually waited until "I want to raise" flipped to "I don't need to raise -- but now I can choose to."

That's the bar.
