## Post

Karri nailed something most founders won't admit.

Being deliberate -- waiting until you understand where AI actually fits -- is harder than just shipping something with "AI" in the name.

The temptation is real. Add the feature when the hype peaks. Move fast. Don't miss the window.

But the companies ending up ahead aren't the ones who moved fastest. They're the ones who had the most clarity about which specific problems were actually theirs to solve.

That clarity shows up in product, in pricing, in how you talk about what you built. It compounds. Shipping for the headline doesn't.
