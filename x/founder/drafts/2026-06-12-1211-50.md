## Post

Karri's right.

Everyone wants to ship AI features first. Race to the category. First-mover advantage.

But first-mover in AI features has a shelf life of months -- not years. Models improve. Competitors get access to the same foundation. Whatever you shipped first, someone copies in a quarter.

The thing that actually compounds isn't who shipped first.

It's who understood the workflow well enough to know WHERE AI makes it better -- and where it gets in the way. That requires genuinely knowing your users. Not usage dashboards. The part of their job that's painful vs. the part they'd fight to keep doing themselves.

Most AI bloat I see is teams shipping features because they CAN, not because users need them.

Deliberate takes longer. It usually wins.
