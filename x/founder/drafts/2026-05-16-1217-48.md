## Post

Most founders treat churn as a product problem.

Build better features. Fix the onboarding. Improve the experience.

All real levers. Here's what gets skipped:

20–40% of all SaaS churn is involuntary.

The customer didn't decide to leave.

Their card expired. The payment failed. Nobody followed up.

That's not a retention problem. That's a billing ops problem.

And it's recoverable — companies with smart dunning sequences recover 1 in 3 customers who would have otherwise left without a single product change.

Before you build another onboarding flow, check what your involuntary churn rate actually is.

Most founders don't know the number. Most are leaving recoverable ARR on the table because they never looked.
