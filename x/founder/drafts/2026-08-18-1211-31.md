## Post

**Tweet 1**
Bootstrapped companies reach $1M ARR about 4 months behind VC-backed peers.

And they arrive there owning 100% of the outcome.

In 2026, that gap is compressing.

**Tweet 2**
Here's what changed:

AI collapsed build costs. You ship at a pace that used to need 3 engineers.

No-code and cheap infra mean overhead is near-zero early on.

Distribution is free -- SEO, LinkedIn, X, build in public. Channels that cost time, not money.

**Tweet 3**
And the VC window has gotten narrow.

Funding is clustering around AI mega-rounds and elite technical teams. For the other 90% of founders, that window is closing.

The honest path for most people in 2026: build revenue first.

**Tweet 4**

Fundraise from strength, or don't fundraise at all. Both are real options now.

The easiest way to get there: build like you never will.
