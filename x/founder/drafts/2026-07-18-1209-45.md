## Post

"Landing pages on Google are the equivalent of creatives on Meta."

This is exactly right. And it got measurably more true this month.

Google just removed first click, linear, time decay, and position-based attribution models. The two that survived -- data-driven and last click -- both optimize heavily around what happens after the click.

Your landing page is the only thing the algorithm can actually learn from now.

If your copy is vague, your offer is unclear, or your page buries the value -- the model reads that as low intent and starts routing spend elsewhere. It doesn't know why the conversion didn't happen. It just knows it didn't.

The landing page was always the real lever on Google. The attribution change just made it impossible to ignore.
