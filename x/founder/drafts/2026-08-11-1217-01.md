## Post

SaaS didn't die. The pitch deck terminology changed.

The business model -- recurring revenue for solving a problem that doesn't go away -- is exactly as durable as it's ever been.

What actually got exposed this cycle was the AI wrapper. Take an existing product, add a chatbot layer, call it an AI company. That playbook ran out of runway.

The founders who came out ahead are the ones who used AI to build something better. Not to rename something existing.

The label never mattered. The problem you solve always did.
