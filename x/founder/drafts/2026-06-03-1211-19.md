## Post

Google just replaced the static B2B lead form with an AI chat agent.

That's not a minor update. It's the end of "click here, fill out this form, we'll follow up in 48 hours" as a B2B acquisition strategy.

The new Business Agent for Leads -- announced at Google Marketing Live 2026 -- embeds a Gemini-powered chat agent directly inside the ad. The prospect clicks. The AI asks what they need. The conversation happens in real time. A qualified lead passes to sales with context the rep can actually use.

No redirect. No landing page. No autoresponder sequence kicking off two days later.

Here's what this actually changes for founders:

The AI can handle the conversation. It can qualify intent, answer product questions, collect information, and hand off a warm lead. It's genuinely good at this.

What it can't do: show up with something worth saying.

The brands that will extract the most from this aren't the ones with the best prompts. They're the ones whose prospects already showed up with a reason to engage. They read something useful. They heard the name from a peer. They saw content that made them curious before they ever clicked an ad.

AI handles the conversation. Positioning is still your job.

Most teams will add this feature and keep the same weak offer: "request a demo." An AI wrapper on a weak value prop is still a weak value prop. Prospects will just have a more polished experience saying no.

The ones who win will rebuild the entry point -- what does a prospect actually need to know before they're ready to talk? Build toward that answer. Not the form fields.

The media buyer job is changing. The positioning job isn't.
