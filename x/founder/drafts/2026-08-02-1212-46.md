## Post

SaaS isn't dead. It just got harder to shortcut.

TK nails it -- the AI Founder rebrand was mostly narrative positioning. The SaaS model is as sound as it always was. Predictable software revenue, real retention, solved workflows. None of that changed.

What changed is the distribution layer.

Research now starts in ChatGPT. Recommendations come from agents. The founders winning in that environment are the ones who built direct audience relationships before the AI layer arrived.

Agent-native is the right evolution. But the companies doing it well didn't get there by renaming their product. They got there because customers already trusted them when the shift happened.

The relationship mattered before. It matters more now.
