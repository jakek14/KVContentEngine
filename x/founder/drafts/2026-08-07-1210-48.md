## Post

Most founders think their Google performance problem is a bid problem or a keyword problem.

It's usually a landing page problem.

Creatives on Meta get iterated constantly -- multiple angles, multiple hooks, weekly tests. The exact same logic applies to Google. The click gets you there. The page is where the conversion actually lives.

If you'd test five versions of an ad, test five versions of the landing page it sends traffic to. Different copy for the informational search. Different CTA for the comparison search. Different headline for the brand keyword.

Jackson's right on this. Most teams just haven't made the mental shift to treating landing pages the same way they treat ad creative.

## Context

Jackson Blackledge shared notes from a Google Ads talk his team gave to ecom operators, where the main point was that landing pages are the Google equivalent of Meta creatives -- the most underrated performance variable in 2026 paid search. Good angle for founders running their own growth.
