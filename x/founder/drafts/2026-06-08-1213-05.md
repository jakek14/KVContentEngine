## Post

Ranking #1 on Google used to mean traffic.

60% of searches end without a single click now. In AI Mode it's 93%.

Google's AI Overviews answer the question before the reader ever sees your result. You can hold the top spot and still get passed over completely. And the overlap between ranking in the top 10 and getting cited in an AI Overview has collapsed -- from 75% a year ago down to somewhere between 17 and 38% today.

This isn't a glitch. It's working exactly as designed. Google doesn't need you to get clicked to monetize the search.

Your content trained the model. Your traffic was the toll.

The founders I've seen adapt aren't doubling down on SEO spend. They're building audiences they own -- email lists, communities, direct distribution -- and treating the website as a destination worth visiting, not just a funnel to rank into.

You can't rank your way to an audience anymore.
