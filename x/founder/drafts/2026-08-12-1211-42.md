## Post

Google AI Overviews now appear on 48% of searches.

Organic CTR for position-one on those queries dropped from around 30% down to 8-12%.

The content arms race ran for twenty years. Publish more. Rank for more. Capture more traffic. Every agency sold the same playbook because it was the right playbook.

Google ended it by deciding to answer the question itself.

Companies that built genuine authority -- not just high-ranking content, but the kind of writing that gets cited as a source in an AI answer -- they're fine. Better than fine.

Companies that built content factories? That model just became a very expensive way to rank for traffic that no longer clicks through.

The shift isn't temporary. It's structural.

## Context

Google AI Overviews are now appearing on roughly half of all US searches. Organic CTR for top-ranking pages on those queries has collapsed from ~30% to 8-12%. Major publishers like HubSpot have reported losing 70-80% of organic traffic. The hot-take angle: the SEO content arms race was always a bet that Google would keep sending traffic.
