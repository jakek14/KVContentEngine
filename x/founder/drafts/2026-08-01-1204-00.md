## Post

The share of startups with solo founders and no outside capital went from 22% in 2015 to 38% in 2024.

The fundraising-first model is losing its grip.

Old script: build fast, raise early, hire ahead of revenue. The round was the milestone.

New math: AI tools cut the team size you need to ship a real product. Infrastructure is cheap. Validation is faster. The case for diluting before you understand your own business gets harder every year.

Top quartile bootstrapped companies reach $1M ARR only 4 months behind their VC-backed peers -- and keep 100% of the equity.

Profitability used to be what you promised investors later. Now it's what bootstrapped founders have from day one.

The leverage in every fundraising conversation just shifted.
