## Post

Bootstrapped teams used to lose the knowledge war.

Funded competitors had SDR teams, intent data subscriptions, and enterprise enrichment tools. They knew who was on their site. Who was coming back. Who visited the pricing page three times before ghosting.

Small teams just guessed.

AI narrowed the execution gap. A 2-person team can now ship and market at a pace that used to require 20 people.

But the intelligence gap didn't disappear with it. You still need to know who's showing up and who's evaluating you right now -- before they ever fill out a form.

That signal matters more when you can't out-spend on acquisition. When every qualified visitor counts, losing them to a form they won't fill out is a real cost.

That's why I built KnownVisitors. Not because enterprise teams need it. Because lean teams need it most.

When budget is limited, wasting visitor intelligence isn't a small miss. It's the whole ballgame.
