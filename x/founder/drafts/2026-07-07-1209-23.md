## Post

**Tweet 1**
$510B raised in the first half of 2026.

Every headline called it a record.

Here's the part most people skipped.

**Tweet 2**
Two companies -- OpenAI and Anthropic -- captured roughly $217B of it.

That's 43% of all VC capital deployed in the first six months of the year.

To two companies.

**Tweet 3**
The other 57% was split across thousands of deals, every sector, every stage from pre-seed to late growth.

That's the market most founders are actually living and competing in.

**Tweet 4**
Meanwhile, median SaaS valuation multiples dropped from 7x ARR to 3.8x in roughly 15 months.

Record capital flowing into AI didn't lift every boat. It just concentrated at the top.

**Tweet 5**
So two things are both true:

AI investment is at an all-time high.

And the market for founders building outside that circle -- real products, real customers, real revenue -- is leaner and harder to raise in than it was three years ago.

**Tweet 6**
The funding environment you're actually in isn't the one making headlines.

It's the one where you build something people pay for and figure out unit economics before running out of runway.

Most of us live there. Build for that.
