## Post

The "SaaS is dead" crowd has been wrong three times already.

Email was going to kill it. Then no-code. Then product-led growth was going to eat everything. Each time, the model survived. The bar just got higher.

That's what's happening now.

AI didn't kill SaaS. It collapsed the execution gap. Features that took 6 months now take a weekend. Which means if your only advantage was "we'll ship it before they do" -- that part is gone. Not the business. The advantage.

SaaStr nailed the framing. The question isn't whether SaaS is dead. It's whether your company has something that doesn't compress.

Customer relationships don't compress. Distribution doesn't compress. Trust doesn't compress. The brand you've built over years doesn't compress.

If that's your edge, build faster now that you can.

If execution speed was your only moat -- that's the part that's actually dead.
