## Post

US AI ad spend hit $32 billion this year.

If you've been following the "AI is killing paid search" conversation, that number probably surprises you.

Here's the detail nobody mentions: more than 80% of that $32 billion flows through traditional paid search formats running alongside AI-generated results -- not inside chatbots. Not inside Perplexity. Not inside ChatGPT.

And Alphabet's Q2 search revenue grew 17% year over year. With a full year of AI Overviews running.

The death of paid search is being announced by people who haven't looked at the numbers.

What IS changing: where buyers start their research. AI assistants are becoming the top of the funnel. But buyers still click through. They still visit websites. They still convert in the traditional places.

The media narrative is real. The financial numbers tell a different story.

The smart move isn't to abandon paid search. It's to understand that intent still works -- it's just being shaped by new inputs before it reaches your ad.
