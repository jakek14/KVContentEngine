## Post

Google's Business Agent for Leads just changed something most founders haven't caught yet.

When a buyer clicks your ad in AI Mode, Gemini reads your website -- live -- and answers their questions inside the ad before a lead form ever appears.

Your website content is your first sales conversation now. Not your pitch deck. Not your sales team. The copy sitting on your site right now.

If your site is thin and generic, Gemini gives thin and generic answers. The lead doesn't qualify. The form doesn't get filled.

The companies that win this aren't the ones with the best sales teams. They're the ones whose websites already communicate with clarity: who this is for, what it does, what changes after someone buys.

That was always true. It just became measurable in a new way.

## Context

Google announced the Business Agent for Leads at Google Marketing Live 2026. The format lives inside AI Max and Performance Max campaigns -- Gemini reads the advertiser's website content in real time to answer buyer questions, then passes a pre-filled lead form. The insight: thin or vague website copy now directly determines lead quality in the new AI ad format. Currently in pilot for Education, Automotive, and Real Estate.
