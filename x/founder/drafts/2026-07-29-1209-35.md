## Post

LinkedIn company pages now reach 1.6% of their followers.

Down 60-66% from two years ago.

Personal profiles generate 561% more reach than company pages. Sometimes the same content, posted one day apart, performs completely differently depending on who hits publish.

There's a lesson here that most SaaS founders are learning slowly.

The company logo is not a distribution channel. The algorithm stopped treating it like one and the data is unambiguous about when that happened.

The founders building real LinkedIn audiences are doing it as themselves. Posting the decisions they're making. The things that didn't work. The surprises from the last quarter. The stuff no press release would ever say.

That's what gets read. Not the company announcement with the stock photo.

If your only LinkedIn presence is a company page, you're building into silence. The channel exists. The audience isn't there.

Personal brand is the only LinkedIn distribution that still works.
