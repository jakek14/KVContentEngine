## Post

Email still returns $42 for every dollar spent.

Highest ROI in marketing. It's not close.

Most brands know this and still treat their list like a broadcast channel.

Same promo, blasted to everyone, once a month.

The brands compounding on email do three things differently:

Send based on behavior, not calendars. Someone visits a pricing page twice — they don't get the same email as someone who only read a blog post. The signal tells you where they are in the decision.

Build trust before the pitch. Not every email is an offer. Some are just useful. That's what makes the offer land when it comes.

Treat email as the start of a conversation, not the transaction. Opens and clicks are early signals — not the outcome.

Paid traffic rents attention. Email owns it.

That's the moat that compounds while every other channel gets more expensive.

## Context

Email marketing ROI benchmarks for 2026 confirm email continues to outperform every other marketing channel at $42 ROI per dollar. The insight most brands are missing: email is being treated as a broadcast tool when it should be behavioral and relationship-driven.
