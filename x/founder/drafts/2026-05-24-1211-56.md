## Post

Marketing automation will handle 36% of all marketing work by 2028.

That's double what it handles today.

Here's the take nobody's adding to that number:

Every automated workflow in marketing runs on visitors who already identified themselves. Email sequences, AI lead scoring, behavioral triggers — all of it fires on the ~3% of site visitors who filled out a form or gave an email.

The other 97% browsed, showed intent, and left.

No automation touched them. None.

So when teams celebrate their AI marketing stack, what they're actually celebrating is a more efficient operation on 3% of the opportunity.

The upstream gap — knowing who visited before they converted — is the layer that doesn't exist yet for most companies.
