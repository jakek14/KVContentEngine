## Post

Chamath is right. The Great SaaS Meltdown is real.

But the interesting question isn't who's going under.

It's what survives.

Not the fastest growers. Not the most impressive ARR charts. The ones that survive are the ones whose product kept working after the novelty wore off. Customers who stayed and expanded -- not because of sales pressure, because the thing actually delivered.

NRR is the truth serum. It separates "we signed them" from "we earned them."

The meltdown isn't a disaster. It's markets getting honest about which companies built something real.

Know who your best customers are. Know who visits, who converts, who stays -- those signals tell you more about your product's durability than any growth chart. It's exactly why I built KnownVisitors.

Build the thing that earns the renewal.
