## Post

Your CRM is a record of who said yes.

Every contact in it raised their hand. Filled out a form. Booked a demo. Bought something.

That's useful. But it tells you nothing about the people who researched your product for three weeks and bought from a competitor. Or the ones in an active evaluation right now. Or the buyer who hit your pricing page twice this month and hasn't reached out.

The pipeline problem most companies have isn't in their CRM. It's in everything that happened before the CRM record was created.

The decision is usually made before the first touchpoint with sales.
