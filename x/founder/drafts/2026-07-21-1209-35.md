## Post

Google's new ToS went live July 1st.

Short version: Google can now use your campaign inputs -- creative, targeting, URLs, anything you enter into their AI tools -- to generate assets and select placements automatically, across any features they want.

You approved nothing specific. You still own the legal exposure.

That asymmetry is worth sitting with. Every time Performance Max generates an asset you didn't review, or Smart Bidding does something you didn't sign off on, you're the entity that agreed to it. The platform got the authority. You kept the accountability.

Not a reason to stop running Google Ads. But it is a reason to audit what you've handed the algorithm -- and build a habit of reviewing what it generates.

If you're spending serious budget on Google and haven't read the July update, spend 10 minutes today.

## Context

Google Ads' revised Terms of Service took effect July 1, 2026. The update explicitly grants Google authority to use advertiser inputs across automated systems, generate campaign elements, and change serving logic -- while preserving advertiser legal liability for the output. Most advertisers running Performance Max or Smart campaigns were already giving the algorithm this level of control in practice. The ToS made the contractual structure explicit. This is free-game content -- founders and marketers need to understand what they've agreed to.
