## Post

Neil is right — and the data behind why is the part most people aren't tracking.

AI Overviews now appear in 48% of all Google searches.

When one shows up, 80-83% of the time the user never clicks anything.

Your organic traffic didn't disappear. It just started going somewhere you can't see.

Here's the counter-intuitive part:

Brands that get CITED inside the AI Overview earn 35% more organic clicks than competitors who rank #1 but aren't cited.

The game shifted from "rank #1" to "be the source the AI quotes."

Three things that actually move the needle:

1. Write content that directly answers the exact question — not content optimized for keywords
2. Publish original data and frameworks your category refers back to. AI cites sources.
3. Consistent structured data across every surface an AI can read: product pages, reviews, third-party coverage

You can't optimize your way to a citation that doesn't exist. Build something worth citing.
