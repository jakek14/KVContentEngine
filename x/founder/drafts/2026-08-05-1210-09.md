## Post

$510 billion in venture funding in H1 2026. A new record.

$217 billion of it went to two companies.

If you're not OpenAI or Anthropic, the market you're actually competing in is tighter than it looks.

Act accordingly.

## Context

SaaS Intelligence Substack published analysis showing the $510B H1 2026 funding record is misleading -- $217B (43%) flowed to OpenAI and Anthropic alone. The remaining $293B was distributed across 50,000+ other startups. For traditional SaaS founders, the real market is more constrained than the headline suggests.
