## Post

The build-side of starting a SaaS just got commoditized.

Cursor crossed 1M monthly active users. 34% of new micro-SaaS products launched in Q1 were shipped by founders with zero prior coding experience.

The full tool stack -- Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf -- runs about $55 a month. A working product is a weekend project now.

Which means "I built it" is no longer the hard part.

Pieter Levels shipped Photo AI v2 in 3 days with Cursor and Claude Code, added $20K MRR in week one. Everyone talks about the build speed. Nobody mentions the 500K+ Twitter followers he already had when he pushed to production.

That's the part that doesn't get easier.

Distribution, positioning, knowing exactly who you're building for -- those didn't speed up when the IDE got smarter. I keep coming back to this: the founders winning with AI tooling are the ones who already had distribution clarity. The tools just let them execute faster.

The bottleneck didn't move. It just got easier to ignore.
