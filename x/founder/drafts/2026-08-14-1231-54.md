## Post

Intent is still the best signal in marketing.

What changed is where it starts.

In 2021, high-intent traffic meant Google search. In 2026, it's Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini -- and someone coming from an AI answer has already read a synthesized take on your category before they land on your site.

More informed visitor. Different journey. Still anonymous.

The attribution stack most companies built doesn't handle this. You can see the visit. You can't see where it really came from or who's behind it.

The organic playbook didn't get smaller. It got more complicated.
