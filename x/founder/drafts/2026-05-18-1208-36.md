## Post

TK is right. SaaS is back.

But the founders who made it through didn't just reclaim the label.

They upgraded the motion.

2025 was PLG vs SLG. Everyone had an opinion.
The winners landed on the same answer: neither.

Product-led sales.

Product drives initial adoption.
Sales comes in when there's real signal — usage, expansion, engagement.

Hybrid PLS companies run 2x more profitable than pure PLG or pure SLG peers.

Not because they found a middle ground.
Because they stopped leaving money on the table at both ends of the funnel.
