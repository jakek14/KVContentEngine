## Post

Lovable is in talks to raise at $13.2 billion. Three years old. $500M ARR in June.

Cursor just got acquired by SpaceX for $60 billion.

Two companies whose entire job is making building easier are now worth $73 billion combined.

There's a thing that happens when a constraint disappears fast enough -- the value that lived inside the constraint moves downstream to the next one.

Build used to be the constraint. Getting a product out required engineers, time, money, a real team. You had a head start on anyone who couldn't afford those things.

That constraint is nearly gone.

Which means the value moved. To distribution. To brand. To whether enough people know you exist.

The tool companies are going to be worth a trillion dollars. The products built on top compete on reach.

If you're building right now, the question isn't whether you can ship anymore.

It's whether enough people know you did.
