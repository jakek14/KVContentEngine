## Post

**Tweet 1**
Google is changing Smart Bidding on August 17.

If you run budget-limited campaigns with Target CPA or Target ROAS, your costs are probably about to change.

Short thread on what's happening and what to do before then.

**Tweet 2**
Right now, if your Target CPA is $10 but you've been converting at $5, Google lets it ride.

After August 17, it won't.

Budget-limited campaigns will be pushed to perform closer to their stated target -- not wherever they've naturally drifted.

**Tweet 3**
Translation: if you've been beating your CPA target, expect costs to move up.

If your target matches your actual performance, you won't notice much.

The ones who get hit are the ones who set a target once and never touched it.

**Tweet 4**
What to do before August 17:

1. Find campaigns marked "limited by budget"
2. Compare stated CPA target vs actual CPA over the last 30 days
3. If you're beating your target, lower it to match actual performance
4. Otherwise Google recalibrates for you -- at higher cost

**Tweet 5**
Google keeps giving you more "AI control" while quietly raising the floor on what your ads cost.

This one is worth an hour before it hits.

## Context
Google Ads August 17, 2026 Smart Bidding update: campaigns limited by budget using target-based bid strategies will now perform more consistently toward their stated bid target. Advertisers who've been beating their CPA targets could see real cost increases. Source: Optmyzr, TechWyse.
