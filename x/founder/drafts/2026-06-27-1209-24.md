## Post

CAC has jumped 40-60% since 2023.

When paid acquisition gets that expensive, the whole playbook inverts.

Three things that actually matter more now:

Retention. Losing a customer costs you what you paid to get them, plus the revenue they won't generate. A 5% retention improvement beats a 5% conversion improvement in most unit economics.

Referrals. Your best customers can do acquisition work you can't afford to buy. Most founders have zero referral infrastructure -- they assume it happens naturally. It mostly doesn't.

First-party data. Knowing who's already engaged with you -- who visited pricing, who opened emails, who bought once -- beats any cold list you can buy.

The companies still running the 2021 playbook are bleeding out on paid spend right now.

## Context

B2B SaaS CAC has risen 40-60% since 2023, driven by stricter privacy rules, saturated paid channels, and rising digital ad costs.
