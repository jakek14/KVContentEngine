## Post

Over 60% of organic search journeys now end without a click to an external site.

Not 60% of total traffic. 60% of the people who searched, found an answer inside Google's AI Mode, and left.

I watched founder friends spend years on SEO. Rankings, backlinks, blog volume. The whole playbook. And the math worked -- until the search interface changed.

The mechanic is different now. Google synthesizes the answer directly. Your site gets cited somewhere below the fold, if at all. The click never comes.

What the founders I respect most are doing differently: they built audience before they needed it. Not a content strategy. An actual audience. People who subscribe, follow, come back because they want to -- not because an algorithm sent them.

That's not a SEO substitute. It's the thing SEO was supposed to do, done without intermediaries.

Direct distribution is the only channel you actually own.
