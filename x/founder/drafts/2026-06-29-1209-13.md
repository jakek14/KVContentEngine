## Post

Google's AI Overviews just cut organic CTR at position one by 58%.

For ten years, "write good content and rank" was a real distribution strategy. It still works -- but the traffic it delivers is a fraction of what it was.

93% of searches in Google's AI Mode end without a click at all.

Founders who built their top-of-funnel on organic search are watching it quietly deflate. Not because they wrote bad content. Because the answer now lives in the search result itself.

This is a forcing function.

The founders who will feel it least built owned channels -- email lists, communities, word of mouth, brand that doesn't depend on a SERP position. The ones who will feel it most treated SEO traffic as the plan, not a tactic.

Traffic you don't own was always fragile. This is just the clearest it's ever been.
