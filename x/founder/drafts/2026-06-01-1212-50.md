## Post

A new ad platform with 500 million monthly users just opened to any business with a budget.

No $50,000 minimum. No enterprise pitch required.

The instinct is to ask: should I run ChatGPT ads?

The question I'm more interested in: does ChatGPT already mention us when someone asks about our category?

Founders who'll get the most from this platform built brand first. They're already showing up in the organic citations -- paid just amplifies what's already there.

The ones who go straight to ad spend without the brand foundation are paying for distribution in a context where the AI's existing citations carry more trust than the ad does.

New platforms don't change the order of operations.

Brand before ads. Still.
