## Post

10 years. No VC. Now his project name is on 50+ buses in San Francisco.

That's not a shortcut story. That's what patient compounding looks like.

Most people treat bootstrapping as a constraint you graduate out of when the funding arrives. But some of the most durable companies never took the deal. Every point of equity they didn't give away in a funding round is equity that's still there when the buses roll by.

The people who said "come back when you have traction" weren't always wrong about the timeline. They were wrong about the destination.
