## Post

AI search just created a blind spot most marketers haven't dealt with yet.

Buyers are researching vendors in ChatGPT and Perplexity now. Getting summaries. Comparing options. Often never clicking through to any of the sites they're evaluating.

A bigger slice of the buying process is happening before anyone lands on your website.

But here's the flip side: the visitors who DO show up are further into the process. They're not browsing -- they're evaluating. Traffic from AI search referrals converts 22% better than traditional organic. The intent is higher.

This changes what I focus on. More effort into structuring content so AI tools surface it accurately. And more attention to WHO is actually landing -- because each visitor carries more signal than they used to.

That's exactly why we use KnownVisitors on the KV site. When someone lands, they've probably already done their homework. Knowing who they are lets us act on that before it goes cold.
