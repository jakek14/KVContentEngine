## Post

Marketers building their own tools is one of the most underrated shifts happening right now.

Not because the tools are always impressive -- but because building forces you to think clearly about what you actually need to measure.

I've been thinking about this since I saw Sandra Djajic post about building her own ad tracking software. She was manually checking three ad libraries every week to track competitors. The act of building her own tracker probably clarified exactly what she needed to know, and when.

That's the part most people skip. AI lowers the cost of building. But the thinking -- what do I actually need, in what format, how often -- that's still a marketing problem. Not a coding problem.

## Context

Sandra Djajic (marketing lead at Chatbase) posted about building her own ad tracking tool with Lovable instead of patching together three manual library checks.
