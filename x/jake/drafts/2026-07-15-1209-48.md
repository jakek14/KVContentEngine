## Post

Meta's Brand Memory AI learns your brand voice from your ad history.

That sounds great until you think about what most ad accounts actually look like.

A year or two of experiments. Different visual styles every quarter. Copy that shifted whenever ROAS dipped. Messaging that never really committed to a position.

The AI learns that.

Inconsistency in, inconsistency out.

The brands that'll get the most from AI creative tools are the ones who did the boring work first -- clear positioning, consistent visual language, a message that held up across campaigns.

Brand consistency was always a compounding investment. Now it also trains your AI.
