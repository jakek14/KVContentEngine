## Post

Preston nailed the real problem here.

The word "brand" has become a liability in budget conversations. Not because brand building doesn't work -- because marketers let it get synonymous with "we're spending money and you'll have to trust us."

The fix isn't to stop building brand. It's to stop calling it brand when you're pitching a CFO.

Call it "lower CAC over time." Call it "warmer inbound." Call it "buyers already knowing who you are when the sales rep calls." Those are the outcomes brand creates -- and they're all measurable with a lag.

The language shift Preston describes is the only way marketing leaders survive the next round of budget cuts.
