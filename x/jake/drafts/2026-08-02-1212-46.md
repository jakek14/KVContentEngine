## Post

60% higher revenue growth for marketers who lead in AI.

That stat matches what I'm seeing in practice -- not because the tools are magic, but because the right applications save real time on the tasks that used to eat most of the day.

The ones getting the most out of it aren't using AI for everything. They're using it specifically for creative testing, campaign analysis, and audience research -- tasks where pattern recognition across a lot of data actually helps.

AI-generated copy is fine. AI that helps you figure out which messages are landing with which audience segments is where the actual payoff is.

Tool adoption isn't the differentiator. Knowing what to point the tool at is.
