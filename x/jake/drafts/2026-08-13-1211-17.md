## Post

This is right -- and it goes deeper than ecommerce.

The "throw it at the wall" approach to creative testing isn't random. It's actually a choice to skip the hardest part: figuring out which message resonates before you put money behind it.

Message-first. What does your customer actually care about? Then build creative around the one or two answers that keep coming up.

When CAC is up 40 to 60 percent across the board since 2023, the cost of getting the message wrong is higher than it used to be. Every wasted test eats more budget.

Small teams can't out-test big teams. But they can out-think them.
