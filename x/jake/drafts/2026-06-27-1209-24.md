## Post

AI content tools don't replace marketing strategy. They execute it.

The teams actually seeing results built the system first: a clear brief, a defined voice, content archetypes by goal.

Without the strategy layer, you're just automating mediocrity.

## Context

The most common mistake marketers make with AI content tools: skipping the strategy layer and expecting the tool to fill in the gaps. The teams getting real results have built upstream systems -- briefing frameworks, voice guides, content archetypes -- that give AI clear inputs.
