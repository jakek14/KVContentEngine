## Post

Everyone talks about cutting CAC.

Almost nobody talks about improving LTV.

CAC is a bidding war. Costs go up every quarter whether you win or lose.

LTV is yours to build. Better onboarding, smarter retention, tighter loops. That one compounds without competition.

If the unit economics are broken, the problem is usually not acquisition. It's what happens after.
