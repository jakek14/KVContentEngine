## Post

The F tier for paid B2B ads without PMF is the thing most teams learn by burning through a budget.

Paid ads don't find product-market fit. They scale it once you already have it.

In B2B, the person on the other end of your ad doesn't know you, hasn't heard of you, and hasn't been warmed up by anything. The click is expensive. The conversion is rare.

Content and outbound build the foundation that makes paid work later. You can't spend your way past the trust gap.

1,000 B2B customers in under a year. The tier list speaks for itself.
