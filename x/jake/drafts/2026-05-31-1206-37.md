## Post

Ad creative drives 75% of effectiveness.

Not audience targeting. Not bid strategy. Not budget size.

The creative.

Most teams spend most of their time on the other 25% -- audiences, bids, budgets. That stuff feels controllable. Easier to point to in a report.

But the data is pretty clear: you move performance numbers more by fixing a weak creative than by any amount of audience refinement.

Doesn't mean targeting doesn't matter. It does. It's just not where the leverage is.

The creative is also the hardest to optimize. No algorithm tells you why a specific hook flopped. No dashboard gives you the insight -- you have to build the instinct yourself.

Which is probably why most teams spend more time on the easy 25%.
