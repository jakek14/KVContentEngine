## Post

Marketing mix modeling used to cost six figures and take months. Only enterprise had it.

Now Hershey is running it near real-time with AI.

For most small marketing teams, MMM was theoretical. You knew it would give you better channel attribution than last-click. You knew your spreadsheet model was missing things. But the resource requirement was completely out of reach.

That's changing.

The gap between what an enterprise marketing team can measure and what a two-person team can measure is closing faster than most people realize.

That's more than speed. That's access to capabilities that used to require a team of analysts and a six-figure budget.

What else is on that list? That's the question I keep thinking about.
