## Post

Cold calling fell 7.5% last year across B2B channels.

Intent-based lead gen grew 11%.

That gap is not closing.

The shift makes sense when you think about how B2B buyers actually work now. They research independently. They come to their own conclusions before talking to anyone. By the time they reach out, they're already 70% decided. Cold outreach interrupts a process they've already started on their own terms.

So the question isn't how to get better at cold. It's what signals tell you who's already warm.

Working on KnownVisitors has genuinely changed how I think about this. The people who matter most to a business aren't always the ones who haven't heard of you. Sometimes they're the ones already on your site -- checking pricing, reading your blog, coming back for the third time without converting.

Those visitors are the warmest signal most companies completely ignore because they don't know who they are.

The future of lead gen runs on warm signals. Most teams are still optimizing the cold side.
