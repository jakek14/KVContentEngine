## Post

Creative drives 75% of ad performance.

Not targeting. Not budget. Not your bidding strategy.

The copy, the hook, the image.

Most marketing teams spend most of their time optimizing the other 25%.

Where AI changed my approach: speed of iteration on creative. Going from one draft to ten variations in the same session means I'm finding real patterns instead of guessing which direction to test.

But here's the catch: AI makes the volume easy. It doesn't make the judgment call for you.

If you know what a strong hook looks like, AI makes you fast. If you don't, you just get more mediocre output faster.

The constraint shifted from bandwidth to taste.
