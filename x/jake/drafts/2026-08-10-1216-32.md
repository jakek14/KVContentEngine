## Post

All of this is marketing before it's product.

"Go where users complain" -- that's customer insight work. A marketing skill.

"Reply like a human, not a brand" -- that's voice and tone. Also marketing.

"Charge money on day one" is really about positioning. Who is this for and what's it worth to them?

The stuff that makes early SaaS actually grow is mostly those decisions. Who you're building for, how you talk to them, why they'd pick you over everything else.

We treat it as something to figure out after the product exists. Then wonder why traffic isn't converting.
