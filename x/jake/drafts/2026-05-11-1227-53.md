## Post

This conversation is closer to right than most CMOs will admit.

"Brand marketing" as it's been sold for years — unaccountable, emotional, just trust the vibes — deserves to die.

But performance-only is a trap too.

Every channel I've seen work long-term is built on trust first. The ads get cheaper when people already know who you are. The conversion rate goes up when they've seen your name before.

Brand is what makes performance more efficient over time. The real work is figuring out how to build that trust, not abandoning it.
