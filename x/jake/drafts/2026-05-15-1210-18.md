## Post

Working at KV changed how I think about marketing metrics.

Most of what I used to track was lagging.

Traffic. Impressions. Followers. Reach. They all tell you what already happened — not what's about to happen.

The signals that actually predict pipeline are behavioral. Who came back to the site a second time. Which pages they hit. Whether they checked pricing.

That's a leading indicator. It's happening before anyone fills out a form or books a call.

Most marketing teams are optimizing based on yesterday's data. The leading signals are sitting on the site right now, unread.

That's the core idea behind KnownVisitors — surface those signals before they go cold.
