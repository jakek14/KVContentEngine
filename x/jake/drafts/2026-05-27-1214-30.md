## Post

Semrush is describing something I've been trying to put words to for months.

The B2B funnel used to have a shape: awareness, research, comparison, shortlist, decision. Six to eight touchpoints across weeks or months. Content and campaigns were built to work that sequence.

That shape is collapsing.

The discovery, comparison, and research phases are now happening inside a single AI conversation. By the time someone lands on your site, they may already have a shortlist.

From a campaign-building perspective, this changes the priorities completely.

Less about: "how do we get someone into the top of the funnel and nurture them through 8 emails."

More about: "are we the obvious answer when an AI agent looks up our category?"

Brand authority is doing more work than ever. Not brand awareness for its own sake -- but being the name that shows up, that gets cited, that people recognize when the AI mentions it.

That's a different kind of marketing brief to write.
