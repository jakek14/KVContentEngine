## Post

Agree with this -- and from a marketing side, the same shift is happening.

The last couple years the play was: run AI-targeted ads, automate your outreach, let the algorithm sort it out.

Now CAC is up 40-60% across B2B SaaS. Open rates keep sliding. Ad spend stretched thin across every channel.

The marketers cutting through went back to basics. Know exactly who you're targeting. Know who's on your site. Follow up with the ones who showed intent.

That precision used to be impossible at scale. Now it's just the job.

That's what we're working on at KnownVisitors -- not adding more AI hype to the stack, just knowing our traffic better than anyone else does.
