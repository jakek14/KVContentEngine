## Post

Brand accounts are hitting a credibility ceiling.

Not because the content is bad. Because it can't be real. A brand can't be uncertain about where things are heading. Can't share the thing that didn't work. Can't post something unpolished on a Tuesday because that's just what came out.

A person can.

The accounts growing fastest on X and LinkedIn right now aren't polished brand pages. They're founders, marketers, and people doing the actual work -- sharing what's happening in the moment, without the approval cycle.

For small teams, this is the opening.

You can't outspend the company with a $10M marketing budget. You can't out-produce a content team of 20 people. But you're actually in it -- building, learning, figuring things out as you go. That's the thing a brand account can't replicate no matter how good their copywriter is.

The only thing between you and an audience is showing up with something honest.

Big companies are hiring creators to fake the thing that small teams have by default.
