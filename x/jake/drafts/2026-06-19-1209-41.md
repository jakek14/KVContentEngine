## Post

The hardest part of marketing for a small SaaS isn't figuring out what to do.

It's resisting the urge to do everything and picking the two things that actually matter this week.

That filter gets easier the longer you do it. But it's still a filter.

How do you make that call?

## Context

Jake's genuine experience doing marketing for a small bootstrapped team. At a 3-person company, every marketing hour carries a real opportunity cost -- the discipline of knowing what NOT to do is more valuable than knowing the full list of what's possible. Writing from the real experience of working on KV with limited resources.
