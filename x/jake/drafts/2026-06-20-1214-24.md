## Post

80% of B2B marketing teams still aren't integrating brand and demand.

At a company with separate brand and demand teams, that's a coordination failure. Different mandates, different metrics, messages that diverge between the team that builds the story and the team that runs the targeting.

At a bootstrapped company, there's no brand team and demand team. Just one or two people doing both. And that's actually an advantage most people don't talk about.

When you're the same person making the brand call and the demand call, there's no alignment meeting. No brief to write. No handoff where the message shifts.

The positioning you're building is the positioning you're bidding on. The story in your content is the same story in your ads.

Most large-org brand-demand integration failures are coordination failures. You're paying people to talk to each other about something a small team keeps in sync automatically.

The real challenge at small scale isn't integration. It's having a clear enough point of view that "brand" and "demand" are obviously the same decision.
