## Post

Marketing on a small team forces something bigger teams avoid:

You actually talk to the customers.

Not through NPS surveys. Not through Salesforce notes two weeks later. You're in the conversations from day one.

That changes how you market. You know exactly why someone chose you, why they stayed, why they almost left. Copy gets written from what real people said — not what you assumed they felt.

The disadvantage is obvious. Less budget. Fewer people. Less to test with.

The advantage is knowing your customer in a way that takes big teams years of research to approximate.

That's a lot of what makes KV's marketing work. We know who our users are and what they were dealing with before. Because we asked.

Small team means you can't hide behind dashboards.

Turns out that's a feature.
