## Post

95% of your market won't buy this quarter.

That's not a failure of targeting. It's just timing.

Most paid campaigns only talk to the 5% who are actively in their buying window right now. Which makes sense -- those are the conversions you can measure, the ones that show up in dashboards and justify spend.

But the 95% who aren't ready yet are your customers next year. And by the time they enter their buying window, they've already built a short list in their head. That list was built from what they saw while they were still in the 95%.

Consistent content, showing up in the right places, building brand familiarity before someone needs you -- that's not soft work. It's pipeline for next year.

Short-term performance and long-term brand aren't competing with each other. They're just working on different clocks.

## Context

The 95:5 rule (popularized by Professor John Dawes at the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute) is a foundational B2B marketing concept that most performance marketers underappreciate. Good angle for Jake as a marketer sharing what shapes his thinking on campaign strategy and brand investment.
