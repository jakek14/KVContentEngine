## Post

The part that doesn't get said enough: outbound only works when inbound is already working.

You can build the best outbound sequence in the world -- personalized, well-researched, great timing. But if the prospect googles your company name and finds nothing that builds trust, the sequence dies there.

The marketing work that makes outbound convert is invisible. Nobody sees the blog posts, the social proof, the positioning clarity. They just see the cold email.

But that background work is the reason the cold email gets a reply instead of an archive.
