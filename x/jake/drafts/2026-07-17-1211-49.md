## Post

The line between a marketing plan and a marketing wish list is pretty thin.

A real plan has distribution built in before the content is created. It answers "how does this reach the exact person we're trying to reach?" at every step.

A wish list has a target number at the top and the assumption that what worked before will work again.

I've seen both. The gap usually shows up in Q3.
