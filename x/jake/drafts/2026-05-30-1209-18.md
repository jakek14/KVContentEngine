## Post

79% of B2B marketers don't trust the data behind their budget decisions.

And the fix isn't a better attribution tool.

The real problem: 38-51% of B2B pipeline comes from peer referrals, private communities, word of mouth -- channels attribution software will never fully capture.

No dashboard closes that gap.

What works is accepting it. Running half your budget on measurable channels you can optimize. Running the other half on building the kind of brand presence that generates referrals and mentions in places your tracking pixel will never reach.

You can't attribute it. You can still design for it.

Brand shows up in private Slack channels. Gets mentioned at conferences. Earns word of mouth in communities you're not in.

That doesn't show in last-click. It still builds pipeline.

The dark funnel isn't a problem to solve. It's a reality to build strategy around.
