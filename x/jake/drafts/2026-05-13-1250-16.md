## Post

"One problem, one promise, one price."

That's the product advice.

But it's the marketing advice too.

You can't run a campaign, write an ad, or build a funnel until you can answer those three things clearly: who has the problem, exactly how we solve it, and why the price is fair for that specific person.

Most teams skip the clarity and jump straight to the channel. Campaign fails. They blame the channel.

Working at a bootstrapped company forces the answer faster than almost anything. There's no budget to paper over a positioning problem.

The product and the marketing collapse into the same question: do you actually know who you're for?
