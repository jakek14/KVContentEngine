## Post

Gartner just told every enterprise CMO they need to build hybrid human-AI marketing teams.

This is new information to a lot of large marketing departments.

It's not new to anyone who's been marketing a bootstrapped startup for the last two years.

When the team is small, you don't get to assign one person per channel. You figure out which tasks AI can handle and which ones need human judgment, and you build the workflow around that split. Not because you're an innovator. Because you don't have the headcount to run it any other way.

What I've learned from doing it under that constraint: the discipline it forces is actually better.

You can't hide behind activity. "We sent 12,000 emails" doesn't mean anything if none of them drove pipeline. When you're a small team using AI to scale output, every tool has to prove it earns the time investment. You cut fast. You rebuild. You test again.

Enterprise marketing teams are about to learn what bootstrap marketers already know -- having fewer people doesn't mean doing less. It means building tighter systems.

The gap is closing. Some of us got the head start on accident.
