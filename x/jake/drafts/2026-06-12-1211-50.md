## Post

**Tweet 1**
ChatGPT just opened its ad platform to every US business.

No minimum spend. CPC bidding. Available to small teams now.

First genuinely new ad channel in years -- and it's still early.

**Tweet 2**
Some context on why this matters.

Until May, the minimum to test ChatGPT ads was $50,000. That locked out basically every small company.

The platform hit $100M in revenue in its first 6 weeks. Moving fast.

**Tweet 3**
What makes it different from Meta or Google:

Targeting is conversational. You're not matching keywords -- you're reaching people mid-conversation about a topic.

The intent is different from a keyword search or a scroll.

**Tweet 4**
New channels reward early movers way more than established ones.

When targeting is still being calibrated, costs are low, and the algorithm isn't biased toward big budgets -- small teams can punch above their weight.

Meta 2012. TikTok 2019. ChatGPT ads 2026.

**Tweet 5**
I don't know if this becomes a top channel long-term.

But cost-per-click is low, competition is thin, and the intent signals are high right now.

Testing a new channel when it's cheap and uncrowded beats waiting until everyone else already figured it out.
