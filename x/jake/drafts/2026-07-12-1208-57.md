## Post

Email open rates don't tell you much anymore.

Apple's Mail Privacy Protection pre-loads tracking pixels for most iOS users. Open rates look fine in your dashboard. A lot of those are inflated.

The number worth paying attention to: click-to-open rate. People who opened AND clicked. That's your real engaged list.

I've been segmenting based on this instead of raw opens. The people clicking are signaling intent. Messaging that segment differently changes what you see from your campaigns.

Most email tools pull this easily. Most email strategies still aren't built around it.

## Context
