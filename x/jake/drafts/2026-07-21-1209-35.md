## Post

Generic cold email in B2B gets a 3.1% reply rate on average.

Signal-based outreach -- same list, same tool, but sent when something just triggered at the company -- gets 5 to 18%.

That gap is not about copywriting.

It's about timing. A well-written email to someone who just got promoted, hired a new CMO, or closed a funding round lands in a completely different context than the same email sent on a random Tuesday.

The problem is most outreach sequences are built around list size, not buyer context. You load a CSV, set a cadence, and let it run. The contacts who reply are the ones who happened to be in-market when your email showed up. Everyone else ignores it.

Signal-based is harder to build. You need data feeds that surface the triggers -- job changes, expansions, intent signals, tech installs. It takes longer to set up than a CSV import.

But "when" is doing more work than "what" in cold outreach right now. The best-written email in the world doesn't beat good timing.
