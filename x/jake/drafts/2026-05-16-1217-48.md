## Post

LinkedIn just told every small marketing team something important.

Company pages: ~5% of feed allocation.
Personal profiles: ~65%.

Your company page with 10,000 followers gets less reach than a team member who posts consistently as a person.

This isn't a bug. LinkedIn is rewarding authentic human voices over brand broadcasts.

The practical shift for a small team: the highest-leverage LinkedIn play isn't building the company page.

It's making it easy for the actual people at your company to post.

One real person beats five brand posts. Every time.
