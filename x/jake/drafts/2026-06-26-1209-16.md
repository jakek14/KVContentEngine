## Post

**Tweet 1**
Small team marketing teaches you things you can't learn with a big budget.

Biggest one: waste is invisible until it hurts.

**Tweet 2**
On a bigger team, a bad campaign just underperforms. You move on.

On a small team, every bad bet has a real cost. You feel the waste immediately. That feedback loop teaches you faster than any playbook.

**Tweet 3**
You stop asking "what should we try?" and start asking "what do we actually know?"

One generates activity. The other generates insight.

**Tweet 4**
It also kills the fear of "small" campaigns.

A campaign that works with $500 in spend is a win. Nobody grades you on scale here. Results are the only metric that matters.

**Tweet 5**
Three years at a bootstrapped company will teach you more about marketing efficiency than any course or big-team budget.

You learn fast when you can't afford not to.
