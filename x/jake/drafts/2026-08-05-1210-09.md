## Post

Marketing attribution in B2B is mostly fiction.

Your dashboard shows email, paid, and organic. But the buyer who just signed up saw a post you wrote six months ago, heard your name in a Slack community, Googled you once, and then clicked an ad three weeks later.

The ad gets 100% of the credit.

What I've found working at a small SaaS: the channels that feel hard to measure are usually the ones doing the most work. Content and community build something slow -- familiarity, trust, the "oh yeah I know them" moment that makes the eventual conversion make sense.

The paid channel often closes what the unmeasurable stuff opened.

So I run paid and I invest in the stuff that doesn't fit neatly into a dashboard. Both matter. They just look different in reports.
