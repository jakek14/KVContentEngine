## Post

The best ad creative insight doesn't come from brainstorming.

Nick's right. You can sit in front of a blank doc and convince yourself of all kinds of things. Most of them don't work.

What actually produces ideas: watch what brands are actively spending money on. If an ad's been running for 30+ days, it's working. Figure out WHY it's working. What emotion is it hitting? What problem is it naming that the audience already feels?

Then bring that understanding to what you're selling.

Creative research is the part most marketers skip entirely. We go straight to making things and wonder why nothing lands.
