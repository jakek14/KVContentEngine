## Post

15.3% of CMO budgets going to AI. Only 30% ready to actually use it.

The gap between buying AI tools and knowing how to use them is real. And it's wide.

Here's what I've noticed though -- the teams actually getting results from AI marketing tools aren't the ones with the biggest AI budgets. They're smaller teams where one person just... learned the tools. Rolled up their sleeves. Figured out what worked through trial and error over months.

You can't buy that readiness. The survey is basically measuring how many companies paid for something they aren't using well.

The edge right now isn't access to the tools. It's the reps.
