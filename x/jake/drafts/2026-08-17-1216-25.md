## Post

AI-generated dynamic CTAs convert 267% higher than static ones.

Not 10% better. Not 25% better. 267%.

The difference isn't the copy. A static CTA says "Start Free Trial" to everyone. A dynamic one knows you've been to the pricing page twice, you work at a SaaS company, you opened the case study email but didn't click. It says something different.

The high-leverage move in email marketing right now isn't better subject lines or fancier templates. It's the signal feeding the message.

Most marketing teams spend 80% of their optimization time on the message and 20% on the segmentation logic. The 267% stat suggests that ratio should probably be flipped.
