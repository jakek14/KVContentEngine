## Post

This is the most underrated shift in marketing right now.

Every AI tool I use makes it easier to produce content.

Blog posts, ad copy, social, emails — the production bottleneck is basically gone.

Which means the bottleneck moved.

Now it's judgment. Deciding what to make, who it's for, what problem it's actually solving, whether it's going to do anything real.

Before AI, you couldn't produce faster than you could think. Now you can — and that gap exposes every assumption you've been holding about what's worth making.

The teams getting the most out of AI aren't producing more.

They're deciding better.
