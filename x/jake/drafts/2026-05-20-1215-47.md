## Post

73% of B2B buyers now actively avoid gated content.

I think that's the right call. Ungating your content creates better-educated prospects and less friction in the funnel.

But it creates a real problem nobody talks about:

The form fill was annoying.

It was also the only reason you knew someone was interested.

When buyers self-educate through ungated content, they're on your site. They're reading. They're coming back. Checking pricing.

You just can't see them.

The most qualified visitors you have may be completely invisible to your team.

How are you handling visibility into ungated site behavior?
