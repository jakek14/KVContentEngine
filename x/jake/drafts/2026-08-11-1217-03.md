## Post

Everyone's talking about how AI lets you move 75% faster.

That's real. But the part people skip over is what faster actually buys you.

Before: you could test 2 campaign concepts in a quarter. You picked your best guess and made it as polished as possible.

Now: you can test 10.

The one that works? It's almost never the one you would have led with. It's the fourth or seventh concept. The one you would have never gotten to without the bandwidth.

That's the real unlock -- not doing the same work faster. Running more experiments than you could before. The insights were always there. You just didn't have the cycles to reach them.

Small teams compete against big ones this way. You can't outspend them. But you can out-iterate them.

Recent data shows marketing teams with AI automation go to market 75% faster. The angle here isn't about AI being magic -- it's about iteration velocity being the strategic advantage for small teams.
