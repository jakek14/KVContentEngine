## Post

The chance of converting a lead drops 80% if you wait more than 5 minutes to respond.

Not 5 days. Not 5 hours.

5 minutes.

Speed isn't a sales culture problem. It's a marketing system design problem.

Build the system, and it doesn't matter how fast your reps hustle.

## Context

Lead gen research consistently shows the dramatic drop-off in conversion probability after 5 minutes — prospects have short attention windows and move on quickly. The framing here is important: most companies treat this as a sales urgency issue, but the real solution is building marketing automation that triggers immediate, relevant follow-up the moment intent is shown.
