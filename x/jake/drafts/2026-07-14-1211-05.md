## Post

"Reply like a human, not a brand" is the one most companies never actually do.

At a small bootstrapped company you don't have a choice. No comms team. No approval chain. When someone asks a question, you just answer it.

What we've found at KnownVisitors: that directness is the whole point. Buyers can immediately tell when they're talking to a person who built the thing vs. a marketing layer.

The bigger the brand, the harder this is. The smaller the team, the more it becomes a real advantage.
