## Post

CMOs are putting 15.3% of their marketing budgets into AI.

Only 30% say they're actually ready to scale it.

That gap tracks with what I see. Buying the subscription is easy. Knowing what to feed it is the hard part.

Most AI marketing failures aren't tool failures. They're brief failures. Positioning failures. "We don't actually know our customer well enough to give the AI useful instructions" failures.

The teams getting results aren't the ones with the most AI tools. They're the ones who did the clarity work first -- tight ICP, defined message, real data on what converts. The AI just runs the plays faster.

Garbage brief in, garbage output out. That's always been true. AI just makes the feedback loop faster.
