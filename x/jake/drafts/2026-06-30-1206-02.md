## Post

The first point is the most underused one.

"Go where your users already complain" is one of the highest-ROI things a marketer can do -- and most teams skip it entirely because it doesn't feel like marketing.

Reddit threads. Facebook groups. Slack communities. 1-star reviews on G2. The LinkedIn comment section under competitor posts.

That's where buyers describe their problems in their own words. Before any sales cycle. Before any survey you send them. Real language, real frustration, real context.

The campaigns I've seen land best are almost always ones where the copy came from listening to those spaces -- not from a briefing room guessing at messaging.

You can automate the listening now too. AI can crawl communities and surface patterns at a scale no one could do manually. The signal was always there. The bottleneck was bandwidth.

Go read where they complain first.
