## Post

This is the right thing to be paying attention to.

When the platform handles the image, video, text, and targeting -- the human job becomes the brief. What problem does this solve? Who exactly is it for? What does the brand stand for?

That's always been the hard part. Execution was just the translation layer.

A lot of marketers have spent years getting good at execution. Good at the translation. That's the skill going away.

The clarity layer -- knowing what you're actually trying to say, to who, and why it matters -- that stays. And honestly, most teams haven't invested nearly enough in it.
