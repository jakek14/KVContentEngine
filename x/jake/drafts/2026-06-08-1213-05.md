## Post

Most people treat churn like a late-stage problem.

It's not. 60-70% of SaaS churn happens in the first 90 days -- that window is on marketing as much as product.

The promise you made in the ad, the landing page, the email sequence -- that's what the customer shows up expecting. If day one doesn't deliver on that expectation, they're gone before anyone realizes they're at risk.

Churn isn't just a customer success failure. A lot of the time it starts in the funnel.
