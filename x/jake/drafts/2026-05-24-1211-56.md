## Post

Gartner surveyed CMOs: 65% expect AI to significantly disrupt their role.

Only 32% say they need significant new skills to deal with it.

That's a 33-point gap.

I've been trying to understand it. I think it comes down to this: acknowledging disruption is easy. It requires no action. Actually changing how you work requires real risk — committing to new workflows before you know if they pay off.

The marketers I see actually moving are the ones treating AI like a tool to figure out, not a trend to monitor from a distance.

"AI is coming for marketing" is a belief almost everyone holds now.

Knowing what that means for how you run campaigns next month is still rare.
