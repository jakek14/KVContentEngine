## Post

Everyone's talking about what AI can build.

The more useful conversation is what it frees you from.

Marketing used to involve a lot of mechanical work. Pulling lists. Building out campaign sheets. Writing copy variations and picking one based on gut. Rebuilding the same kind of sequence for a new audience you ran last month.

That work didn't need judgment. It needed time.

AI handles most of it now.

What's left is the thinking. What message actually lands with this audience? Which segment are we really going after? Is this campaign even solving the right problem for the right person at the right stage?

The teams winning with AI aren't moving faster at the same work. They're doing different work entirely.
