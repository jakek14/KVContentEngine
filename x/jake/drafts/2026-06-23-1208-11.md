## Post

Karri's point applies to marketing work too.

There's a tool-of-the-day trap when you're using AI for marketing. New model drops, you add it to your stack. A few months later you've got five different AI tools for content tasks, none of them actually changing what you're producing.

The most useful shift wasn't using more tools. It was getting specific about the two or three workflows where AI actually changes the result -- brief writing, creative iteration, campaign research -- and going deep on those.

Understanding which workflows mattered was the right question. Most people are still asking what tool to try next.
