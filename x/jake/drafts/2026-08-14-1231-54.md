## Post

**Tweet 1**
D2C is having its reckoning.

The brands built on cheap Facebook ads and growth-at-all-costs are struggling.

What actually survived?

**Tweet 2**
The survivors built real brands.

Not products with better packaging. Brands customers actually love and come back to.

Retention rate is the proof of the work.

**Tweet 3**
CAC went up 60%+ in five years.

At that price, acquisition-only math doesn't work anymore. You need customers to buy again, refer friends, stick around.

Brand is the hedge.

**Tweet 4**
The other thing separating winners: first-party data.

Brands that own their customer relationships are pulling away from brands that still rent their audience from Meta.

One algorithm change and you lose everything you built.

**Tweet 5**
The 2021 D2C playbook: find a product, run ads, scale fast.

The 2026 version: find a product, build a brand, own your customer.

Two very different businesses. One of them works long-term.

## Context

D2C ecommerce research in August 2026 shows major divergence in performance between brands that built loyal audiences and first-party data vs. those relying purely on paid acquisition. CAC increases of 60%+ over five years make the pure acquisition model increasingly unworkable. This is core marketing insight relevant to Jake's audience of marketers and founders.
