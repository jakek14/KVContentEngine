## Post

Shopify merchants can now connect their catalogs to ChatGPT.

Buyers can discover products and purchase directly inside the conversation -- no redirect, no new tab, no checkout page.

That changes something for marketers that I don't think people are talking about enough yet.

Your product descriptions aren't just website copy anymore. They're the input an AI uses to decide whether to recommend your product in a conversation. The buyer may never see your website at all.

The marketing asset most teams have treated as an afterthought -- actual product copy -- just became the most important creative in your stack.
