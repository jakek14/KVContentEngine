## Post

Copywriting made this list for a reason.

But what I've learned from actually working on campaigns: the skill isn't really about the words. It's about the diagnosis.

The best performing copy I've seen leads with the exact problem the buyer is already thinking about. Not a feature. Not even a benefit. The thing they woke up thinking about this morning.

Most marketing copy leads with the solution. The stuff that converts leads with recognition -- "this is exactly my situation" -- before it explains what the product does.

You can teach anyone to write clearly. The harder skill is knowing which problem to lead with in the first place. That's market insight disguised as copywriting.
