## Post

This framing applies in B2B too.

The channels that don't feel like "marketing" are usually the ones with the lowest true CAC.

A newsletter with an actual editorial voice. A founder posting consistently about the problem they're solving. Customers who evangelize because the product genuinely changed how they work.

None of this looks like marketing on a Tuesday morning.

But look at the data twelve months out.

The math doesn't lie — it just takes longer to show up. Most teams optimize for what they can measure in 30 days. The channels that compound over 12 months stay neglected because they're hard to attribute.

That's the gap. And it's a real one.
