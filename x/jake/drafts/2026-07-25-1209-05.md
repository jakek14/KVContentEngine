## Post

68% of Google searches now end without a click.

That number completely changes how I think about content strategy.

The old play was: rank for high-volume keywords, drive traffic, convert. More volume meant more pipeline. But if most searches are being answered inside the results page itself, the traffic that actually reaches your site isn't random anymore.

The visitors who make it through are the ones who wanted more than a snippet. They're not just browsing -- they're evaluating. Comparing. Researching in depth.

That means generic top-of-funnel content designed to capture volume is a worse bet than it was two years ago. Deep, specific, genuinely useful content -- the kind that earns a click because the snippet isn't enough -- is a better one.

The bar went up. The volume went down. The intent of the people who do click is higher.

That's not a content problem. It's actually an argument for doing content right.
