## Post

MQL to SQL conversion dropped from 13.1% to 9.8% over the last two years.

25% decline. No single obvious cause.

The instinct is to fix the MQL criteria -- tighten the lead score, realign definitions between marketing and sales.

But I keep coming back to something more basic: we're calling things "qualified" based on actions that don't actually signal purchase intent. Downloaded a PDF. Hit a threshold in a lead scoring model. Attended a webinar.

Those predict engagement. Not buying.

The better question isn't "how do we improve MQL quality." It's "which visitors are already showing high intent -- and how do we actually see them."
