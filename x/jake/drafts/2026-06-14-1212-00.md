## Post

The checkout is one of the most underinvested parts of the funnel.

Traffic teams own the click. Creative teams own the ad. CRO is usually the landing page.

But by the time someone gets to checkout, the hard marketing work is done. They've said yes. The only job left is to not lose them.

Shopify's Summer 2026 Editions data shows Checkout Blocks lifting conversion 8-22% for Plus merchants.

That's not a dev problem. That's a marketing win nobody's been taking credit for.
