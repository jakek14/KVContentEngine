## Post

The easy thing to miss here: AI can automate execution, but it can't automate strategy.

Meta's automation handles all the downstream stuff -- copy variants, creative, targeting weights, bid pacing.

What it can't do is tell you what makes your product different, who cares about it most, and why they should buy it now instead of next quarter.

Those inputs still need a human. And if your strategy is fuzzy going in, you're just running fuzzy logic at machine speed.

The marketers who are going to do well in a fully automated ad environment are the ones who get sharp on positioning before the automation takes over. Brand strategy becomes more valuable as execution becomes cheaper.

Feed the machine something worth amplifying.
