## Post

AI will handle 36% of marketing work by 2028.

Up from 16% today. Gartner published this yesterday.

The reaction I keep seeing: marketers worried about getting replaced.

But that's the wrong number to focus on.

The 64% that AI still won't handle — that's where it gets interesting.

The stuff AI genuinely can't do: figure out why a campaign that looked good on paper didn't convert. Recognize that your audience has shifted before the data catches up. Know which customer insight should actually change the message.

That's not production work. That's the judgment that makes the production worth anything.

The marketers I want to learn from aren't the ones automating the most. They're the ones using AI to clear execution off their plate so they can spend more time on the part that actually moves the needle.

AI handles the output.

You own the insight.

That's a better deal than what we had before.
