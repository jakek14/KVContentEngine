## Post

SaaS per-seat pricing is quietly collapsing.

February 2026: $285 billion in SaaS market cap evaporated in what analysts called the SaaSocalypse. The companies hit hardest were the ones charging per seat.

The reason: AI agents do real work now. They don't take up a seat. They don't need a license.

If your tool's whole value proposition is replacing headcount, and your pricing is based on headcount, the model doesn't survive the agent era.

For small marketing teams, this actually matters in both directions.

Some of the tools we pay for monthly will start charging by usage, not by seat. Budget gets harder to predict.

But the other side: the tools that actually deliver outcomes — campaigns run, leads generated, content produced — get a lot easier to justify at any price.

The per-seat era rewarded getting more people onto the platform.

The next era rewards demonstrating actual outcomes.

That's a better deal for the buyer.
