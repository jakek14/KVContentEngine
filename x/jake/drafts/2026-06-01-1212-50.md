## Post

The marketing advantage that's hardest to copy isn't budget.

It's how fast you can read a signal and act on it.

You can run the same campaign as a competitor with twice your spend. If they review performance in weekly standups and you're cutting underperformers in 48 hours, you're going to win.

The creative that actually converts doesn't come from inspiration. It comes from iteration.

I've seen this pattern over and over: teams think they have a creative problem when they actually have a process problem.

Speed of learning is a moat. Almost nobody builds it on purpose.
