## Post

When the platform writes the ad, what's your job?

Meta's full automation plan -- AI generates the image, video, copy, and targeting -- lands by late 2026.

Most marketers are worried about this. I get it.

But the way I think about it: if execution gets automated, the thing that stays hard is knowing what to say and who to say it to.

That's audience understanding. That's brand clarity. That's knowing whether an AI-generated ad actually captures what makes your product different -- or just sounds like every other ad in the category.

The AI creates the ad. A marketer who actually knows their customer decides if it's right.

That skill matters more when execution is automated, not less.
