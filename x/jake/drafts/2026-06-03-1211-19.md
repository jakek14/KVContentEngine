## Post

This is the tension every marketing team is dealing with right now.

CEO wants ROI on brand spend. CMO knows brand matters but can't put a number on it. Neither is wrong, and they're talking past each other.

The issue isn't that brand can't be measured. It's that we keep trying to measure it with performance tools, then wondering why it looks inefficient.

Brand doesn't show up in last-click attribution. It shows up in close rate, payback period, referral volume, repeat purchase rate. Metrics that trail the spend by 12 to 18 months.

Working in marketing at a bootstrapped company taught me something: you don't get to run brand and performance as separate programs. The budget doesn't allow it.

Every campaign has to build trust and drive conversion at the same time.

The constraint turns out to be good discipline. You stop running "awareness plays" with no anchor to action. You build things that earn attention and move something.

That's not a compromise between brand and performance.

It's just what good marketing is.
