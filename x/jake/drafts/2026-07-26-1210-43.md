## Post

Ad creative shifted first.

AI tools made it easy to test 50 variants instead of 5. Teams that treated creative as a volume game -- more tests, faster iteration, tighter feedback loops -- pulled way ahead on Meta.

Landing pages are next.

Jackson's point about Google is the same pattern playing out one level down the funnel. Having a single great landing page isn't the goal anymore. You need a system that generates, tests, and iterates on pages the way good media buyers treat creative.

The old playbook: build a page, design it well, ship it. Revise once a quarter if you're disciplined.

What works now: launch fast, build variants, let the conversion data tell you what's working.

AI makes every step faster -- writing copy, building variants, analyzing results. The bottleneck isn't tools. It's marketers still thinking about landing pages as a destination instead of a hypothesis.
