## Post

Meta just surpassed Google in global ad revenue. First time ever.

$243 billion versus $239 billion. A platform built on photos and status updates is now the biggest advertising business on the planet.

As a marketer, the important part isn't the number. It's the signal.

Google wins on intent. Search = buyer in motion. You pay for access to people who already know they want what you have.

Meta wins on behavioral reach. The algorithm finds buyers before they know they're in the market. If your creative tells it who it's for and what problem it solves, it handles the targeting.

For years, the B2B playbook was: Google for conversion, Meta for awareness. That split is harder to defend now.

Budget follows attention. Attention has been moving to Meta for a decade. The revenue numbers just confirmed it.
