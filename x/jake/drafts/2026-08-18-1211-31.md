## Post

One of the biggest advantages of marketing at a small company: you can't hide.

At a big company, you can run a campaign that doesn't work and point to attribution complexity. You can say the brand awareness play is working even if pipeline is flat. Spread accountability across three agencies and a VP. Lots of cover.

At a small company, the revenue either goes up or it doesn't. And it's mostly on you.

That used to intimidate me. Now I see it differently.

When you can't delegate judgment, you develop it faster. You do your own customer research because there's nobody else to do it. You figure out which message actually lands because you're the one writing and testing the copy. You learn which channels drive real buyers and which ones just look good in a monthly report -- because you're the one reading that report and making the call.

Corporate marketing teams get good at running big systems.

Small team marketers get good at the actual craft.

I'd rather have the craft.

The constraint is the education.
