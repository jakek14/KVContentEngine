## Post

This is the metric shift I had to make as a marketer.

I tracked leads for a long time. Then MQLs. The dashboard always looked good.

Meanwhile, nobody in finance could connect those numbers to actual money coming back.

CAC payback period is what the business actually cares about.

Not how many leads. How fast does the spend return as revenue?

Most marketing teams know their CPL. Know their CAC. But the time dimension — how long until you're in the black — stays vague.

Optimizing for payback changes what you optimize for. You stop chasing cheap leads and start chasing the right ones.
