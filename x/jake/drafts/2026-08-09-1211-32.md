## Post

100%.

At a small bootstrapped company, there's no CMO. Marketing is just... everyone's job in different ways.

The question isn't "who's running marketing?" It's "how does a tiny team actually do this well?"

AI tools changed that calculation. Not because they replace strategy -- they don't -- but because they close the production gap. A 2-person marketing team can now produce what used to require a full department.

The classic CMO model makes sense at a certain scale. For companies like ours, a different playbook was always necessary. The gap now is whether you're using the tools that actually make it possible.
