## Post

In marketing the K is already happening.

The marketers adding AI to their workflow are running campaigns, writing copy, analyzing data, and managing more channels than before -- and doing it faster.

The ones who aren't are facing the same output expectations with the same tools from three years ago.

The gap isn't closing. It's widening.

And the thing I've noticed: it's not really about which tools you use. It's whether you figure out what you're trying to accomplish first. AI doesn't cover for fuzzy strategy. It just executes it faster.

The top of the K isn't about working with AI. It's about thinking clearly enough that AI actually helps.
