## Post

Kaleigh is onto something here.

For a long time, "thought leadership" at a B2B company meant the founder tweeted and everyone else stayed quiet.

But buyers don't trust logos. They trust people. The more people from a company they can follow and see doing real work, the more real the company feels.

We're a small team at KV. No dedicated content department. No PR agency shaping a polished narrative. Just people showing their actual work.

That version works. Not the brand account with a strategy deck behind it -- the person who actually does the thing and talks about doing it.

Hard to manufacture that. Which might be the whole point.
