## Post

This is where AI belongs in a marketing workflow.

Not replacing the creative brief. Not skipping the part where you figure out what the ad is actually trying to say.

Replacing the production bottleneck.

The strategy still needs a human. The insight, the angle, the understanding of who you're talking to -- that doesn't change. But once you have direction, cranking out 40 variations used to eat a week. Now it doesn't.

The creative director role isn't going anywhere. The production grind is.
