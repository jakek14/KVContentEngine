## Post

AI-driven traffic to Shopify stores was 8x higher year-over-year in Q1.

Orders from AI-powered search: up 13x.

That's not a test. That's a channel shift.

Buyers are now starting their discovery journey inside AI conversations — asking ChatGPT for recommendations, getting a shortlist, clicking through to a site.

Which means the brands getting found are the ones with the clearest, most specific positioning.

Not the ones with the biggest ad budgets.

When someone asks an AI "what's the best tool for X," the answer isn't built from a bidding auction. It's built from how clearly your product occupies a defined space.

Specificity was always the right marketing answer.

Now there's a 13x multiplier attached to it.
