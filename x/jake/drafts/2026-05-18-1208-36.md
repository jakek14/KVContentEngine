## Post

"I just want someone to handle this for me."

Saurav heard this on nearly every sales call for five years.

But the landing page said "powerful platform for X."

That's the gap most SaaS marketing teams never close.

Customers describe their problem in outcomes.
Marketing writes about the product.

The copy that converts sounds like it came from a customer, not an engineer.

If your homepage doesn't use the exact words your best customers use — that's your conversion problem.
