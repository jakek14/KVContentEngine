## Post

Most B2B visitor identification tools built their matching logic around office IP addresses.

In 2019, that worked. Employees were at a fixed location. The IP resolved to the company. You could match visitor to account with reasonable confidence.

Remote and hybrid work changed the math.

When an employee works from home, their IP resolves to Comcast, AT&T, or Spectrum -- not their employer. Traditional IP-to-company matching doesn't see them. They show up as unidentified consumer traffic.

This is a larger problem than most marketing teams realize.

Your ICP is probably on your site right now. Decision-makers at the exact companies you're targeting for outbound. They just look like anonymous traffic because their laptop is at a kitchen table instead of an office desk.

The tools built for a 2019 browsing environment are producing 2019-era accuracy in a 2026 browsing environment.

Visitor identification that works now has to account for where people actually work -- which is increasingly not the company's office.

IP-to-company matching was never the whole answer. In 2026, it's an even smaller part of it.
