## Post

Interactive content generates 2x more conversions and 5x more pageviews than static content.

Most B2B websites are still brochures.

A wall of text. A few case studies. A contact form. Nothing that requires a visitor's input or gives them something personalized in return for their attention.

Interactive content changes the dynamic. A ROI calculator that requires inputs before showing results. A diagnostic tool that generates a benchmark based on their data. A quiz that surfaces the right product for their use case.

Those things get engagement because the visitor gets something back.

Three places to start if you're a B2B team with limited bandwidth:

Add a ROI or savings calculator to your pricing page. Tie the inputs directly to outcomes your ICP actually cares about -- not generic "save X hours" but specific to their workflow and their numbers.

Build a benchmark tool using data you already have. Customer metrics, survey results, industry averages. Buyers want to know how they compare. Giving them a number they can't get elsewhere is a genuine reason to stay on the page.

Replace a lead magnet download with an interactive version. Same content, different experience. Engagement goes up because completion requires investment -- and investment creates more memory of what they learned.

The floor of B2B website performance is rising. Static content is getting outcompeted.
