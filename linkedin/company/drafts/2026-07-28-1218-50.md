## Post

The average B2B SaaS sales cycle is now 134 days.

Up from 107 days in 2022. That's 27 more days of evaluation, more stakeholders involved, more trips back to your site to check something, reconsider, compare.

Most companies can see when a deal is in their pipeline. They can't see who's researching them before the pipeline starts -- or who's quietly reconsidering during a 134-day evaluation.

Buyers don't announce themselves. They read your pricing page four times over three months. They compare you against alternatives using search. They come back after a call with your team to read the case studies more carefully.

All of that is invisible if you can't identify your website visitors.

The companies closing faster aren't always running better campaigns. A lot of them just have better visibility into who's already in-market.

That's what visitor identification changes. Not just lead generation -- pipeline intelligence. When you know who's on your site and what they're looking at, you're not guessing about intent. You're watching it happen.

That's what we built KnownVisitors to do.
