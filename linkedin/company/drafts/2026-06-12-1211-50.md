## Post

Shopify just opened its B2B features to every merchant on Basic, Grow, and Advanced plans.

For years, wholesale catalog management, custom buyer pricing, and company accounts were locked behind Shopify Plus -- a plan priced significantly higher than standard tiers.

Now those features are included at no extra cost for all merchants.

What's newly available to everyone:

Company profiles with custom pricing for wholesale buyers
Volume discounts and quantity rules
Vaulted payment methods for repeat B2B accounts
A unified admin for both DTC and wholesale without managing two separate systems

Shopify's own data: merchants using these B2B features saw up to 33% more self-serve orders and 20% higher reorder frequency within six months.

This matters for a specific reason. Shopify kept B2B as a deliberately small portion of its GMV because the tools were behind a paywall most merchants couldn't justify. That changes quickly when the tools are free.

If you're a merchant selling both DTC and to resellers -- or managing wholesale in spreadsheets or a separate system -- the economics of centralizing on Shopify just shifted.

Worth building on if you've been waiting.

If you're managing DTC and wholesale today, how are you handling inventory and pricing parity across both?
