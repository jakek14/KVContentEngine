## Post

AI-driven traffic to Shopify stores grew 8x this year.

Orders from AI-powered searches are up 13x since January 2025.

Shopify and Google co-developed an open standard that lets AI agents complete a checkout on your behalf. Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Stripe, Salesforce, Etsy, Target, and Wayfair all signed on to it.

This is live infrastructure with industry-wide backing -- not a future scenario.

What this means for B2B brands:

Your buyer's journey now starts inside a conversation with an AI assistant. Not a Google search. Not an ad. A chat. "What's the best solution for X?" The AI answers. The buyer acts on it.

That changes two things.

Your brand's presence across AI surfaces matters more than it ever has. LLMs build "preferences" based on patterns -- which brands show up consistently alongside which problems, across your own content, third-party coverage, review platforms, and community discussions. That's the new SEO.

And when that buyer clicks through and lands on your site, the visit is higher-intent than it's ever been. They didn't arrive casually. They came after an AI conversation that pre-qualified them.

The channel changed. The opportunity on the other end of it got bigger.
