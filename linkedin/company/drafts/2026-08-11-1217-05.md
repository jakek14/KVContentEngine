## Post

The website visitor identification market was $2.85 billion in 2025.

By 2035, it's projected to reach $7.81 billion.

That's a 10.62% annual growth rate, sustained for a decade.

The reason isn't complicated. Most B2B sites convert 2-3% of visitors through forms. The other 97% leave without a trace. And every year, the cost of paid traffic goes up. Every year, the gap between traffic spend and pipeline grows wider.

Visitor identification closes that gap. That's why the market is scaling.

The market is telling you this problem is real. The only question is when you start solving it.
