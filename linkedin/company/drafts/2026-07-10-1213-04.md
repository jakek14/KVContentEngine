## Post

The average B2B customer journey takes 211 days. 76 distinct touches before a purchase decision.

When someone visits your website, they're not browsing casually. They've been in-market for months. They've already compared options, read reviews, asked colleagues. Your site shows up somewhere in the middle of a process that started long before they found you.

Which means the people on your site are more valuable than your analytics suggest.

You can see the session. You know someone came.

You don't know who.

That's the gap most B2B marketing stacks don't close. Budget goes to driving traffic. The identity of who showed up goes uncaptured. The visit happens, the prospect moves on, and the moment is gone.

When someone is 60 touches into a buying journey and lands on your site, that's not a cold lead. That's a warm prospect who decided to look closer.

KnownVisitors identifies the person behind the session -- name, email, company -- so you can follow up with the visitors who matter, not just count them.

The window is shorter than you think. They're already deep in the process.
