## Post

Visitors from LLM tools -- ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude -- convert at 4.4x the rate of traditional search visitors.

Not because AI audiences are smarter buyers.

Because AI has already done the research for them.

By the time someone clicks through from an AI-generated answer, they've been through the education layer. The LLM answered their basic questions. They know the category. They understand the problem it solves. They arrive pre-qualified.

This changes what the content job actually is.

For classic SEO, the goal was to get found by an index. For AI search, the goal is to become the source that AI tools cite when someone asks about your category.

That means publishing authoritative, specific content that answers real questions with real precision -- not keyword-optimized filler, but the kind of writing that gives an AI model something concrete to cite.

The visitors you get as a result arrive past the "what is this?" stage. They're asking "which one is right for us?"

That shift in intent is exactly what the 4.4x conversion difference reflects.
