## Post

Buyers receive 120+ cold emails a week now. Generic reply rates have dropped to 3-4%.

The teams hitting 15-25% aren't sending more. They're sending to the right people at the right moment.

Here's what separates the two in 2026:

Start with in-market signal, not your ICP list. The best time to reach a prospect is when they're actively researching. Repeat website visits, pricing page views, multiple sessions in a short window -- these are the signals that separate a warm prospect from a cold one.

Match your message to what they've shown interest in. Someone who spent time on your integration docs needs a different opener than someone reading case studies. Signal-based personalization consistently outperforms generic by 4-5x.

Move fast. Leads reached within hours of showing intent convert far better than leads contacted the next day. Most B2B teams are responding in 24-48 hours -- well outside the window where signal is still hot.

The common thread: act on data that already exists. Your site visitors are signaling intent without filling out a form. The teams who see those signals win the deal before competitors even start their outreach sequence.
