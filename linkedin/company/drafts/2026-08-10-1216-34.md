## Post

The average B2B buyer is more than halfway through their decision before they ever talk to a sales rep.

Not 10%. Not 20%. More than halfway.

That means the comparison, the shortlisting, the mental math on whether your product actually solves their problem -- most of that happens without you in the room.

If you're not influencing that research phase, you're entering conversations where the outcome is already leaning somewhere.

Most B2B teams think about top-of-funnel as content that gets seen. Blog posts, LinkedIn articles, white papers -- things that build awareness over time.

But during this research phase, buyers are also visiting your website. Checking your pricing page. Reading case studies. Watching product demos. Comparing your features against a competitor's.

Those aren't passive interactions. That's the decision being made.

The teams that understand this invest in two things at once: the content that builds trust before intent shows up, and the ability to know when intent actually arrives.

Both matter. Most companies only have one.
