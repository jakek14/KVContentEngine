## Post

Every CMO in 2026 is being asked to choose between brand and performance.

That's the wrong question.

The brands outperforming right now aren't choosing.

They're building a full-funnel engine where each side makes the other more efficient.

Here's how it actually works:

Brand creates the conditions for performance to land.

When someone already knows who you are — your conversion rate goes up, your cost-per-click goes down, your payback period shrinks.

That's not a theory. It's the arithmetic of why some companies can run profitable ads that others can't, even bidding on the same keywords.

Performance, in turn, funds brand.

When your bottom-of-funnel converts efficiently, you have the margin to invest in content, reputation, and category authority.

The companies running brand-only without performance accountability are flying blind.

The companies running performance-only without brand are on a treadmill — every gain requires proportionally more spend.

Both failure modes are real. Both are common.

The question B2B marketers should be asking isn't "brand or performance."

It's: what does our brand need to do so performance gets cheaper over time?

And what does performance need to do so brand investment is defensible to the board?

Those are the same question from opposite ends.

The data backs this up: 80% of marketers say creative quality is the most important driver of marketing success. Creative quality drives 47% of a brand's sales lift from advertising.

Creative is what connects brand to performance. Not channel strategy. Not budget allocation. The quality of the idea — and how consistently that idea shows up everywhere.

The best-performing marketing organizations have figured this out.

The gap between them and everyone else is widening.
