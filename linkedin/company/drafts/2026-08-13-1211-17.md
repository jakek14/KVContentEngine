## Post

The demand gen playbook worked when three things were true.

Ad targeting was precise -- you could find your exact buyer with narrow demographic signals on any major platform.

Buyers filled out forms -- inbound content converted at rates that made the whole machine worth running.

Acquisition costs were predictable -- you could build a model, set a CAC target, and hold to it quarter after quarter.

All three are broken.

Privacy changes have degraded targeting across every platform. Less than 2 percent of B2B website visitors complete a lead form. And CAC has risen 40 to 60 percent since 2023.

The best B2B marketing leaders right now aren't doubling down on the same machine at higher cost. They're making a harder shift -- from lead generation to market creation. Brand. Category positioning. Buyer trust. Measuring success over a longer horizon than the quarter.

That's a harder argument to make to a board that wants this month's pipeline number.

But the companies running the old playbook harder and harder are spending more to get less. That's not a budget problem.

It's a strategy problem.
