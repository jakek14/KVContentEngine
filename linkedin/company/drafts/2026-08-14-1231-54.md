## Post

First-party data isn't a privacy workaround. It's the new competitive moat.

Most marketing teams treat data as a reporting layer -- something you look at after a campaign runs.

The companies pulling ahead treat it as infrastructure. Something you build on.

The difference shows up over time. When you own your customer data, every campaign gets more accurate. CAC trends down. ROAS trends up. You're not starting from scratch each quarter.

Brands renting their audience from Meta and Google are one algorithm change away from losing everything they built.

That's the core reason we built KnownVisitors the way we did. You get your data. You own it. You take it wherever you want. No lock-in. No hostage situation with a vendor who controls your list.

In 2026, the question isn't whether to build a first-party data strategy.

It's whether your team has started yet.
