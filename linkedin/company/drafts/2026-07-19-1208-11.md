## Post

AI search referral traffic converts at 3.49%.

Traditional organic: 2.6%. Paid search: 1.5%.

Buyers arriving from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews are more ready to buy than buyers from traditional search. They've already gotten an answer. Your brand was in it. They clicked through because they want to verify -- not discover.

That changes what your landing pages need to do.

A buyer from traditional search is still in research mode. They need the full story -- category education, product explanation, how you compare to alternatives. Long, detailed pages work here.

A buyer from AI search already did that work. They want specifics. Pricing. What the onboarding looks like. What happens after they sign up. Concise, direct pages work here.

Most B2B websites were built for the research-mode buyer. One page structure for every traffic source -- optimized for the visitor who needs everything explained, and losing the visitor who already decided.

The practical adjustment: segment your landing page strategy by traffic source. Same product, different entry points, different pages.

The buyers showing up from AI channels are further along. The page should reflect that.
