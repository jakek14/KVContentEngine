## Post

70% of B2B companies now use buyer intent data.

If you're in the 30% who haven't, here are the signals actually worth tracking -- and what to do when they fire.

**Pricing page visits.** A company that matches your ICP spent four minutes on your pricing page and didn't convert. That's not a bounce. That's a buyer who isn't ready yet. Follow up within 24 hours, personalized to the fact that they were evaluating.

**Job postings.** A company just posted for a demand gen manager, a marketing ops lead, or a sales development rep. Those roles build and buy the tech stack. Their evaluation window just opened.

**Competitor review traffic.** G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot traffic from specific accounts tells you they're in evaluation mode. If you're not in the conversation, you might not make the shortlist.

**Funding announcements.** A Series A or B means fresh capital and new priorities. Companies with new funding buy software in the first 90 days at a much higher rate than at any other point in their lifecycle.

**Job changes.** A new VP of Sales or CMO at a company in your ICP means a blank slate. New leaders build new stacks.

The pattern: buyers signal intent long before they fill out a form.

Intent data closes the gap between when the buying journey starts and when your team finds out about it.
