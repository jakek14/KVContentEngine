## Post

Median CAC hit $1,200 per customer in 2026.

Google Ads costs are up 164% since 2019. LinkedIn Ads up 89% over the same period.

This isn't a short-term cycle. It's what a saturated auction-based ad market looks like after a decade of compounding competition. Every quarter, more advertisers are bidding on the same audiences, the same keywords, the same intent signals.

The response most marketing teams reach for: tighten targeting, improve creative, optimize landing pages. All valid. None of them moves the underlying number by more than 10-15%.

The companies actually bending their CAC curve are doing it differently. They're narrowing who they pay to acquire -- not through demographic filters, but through intent signals. Behavioral data. Site engagement. Active research indicators.

Running paid programs at buyers who are already researching you costs less and closes faster. The unit economics look different when the audience already knows what they want.

$1,200 is the median. The distribution is wide. The companies at the low end of that distribution figured out something about their buyers that the rest of the market hasn't.
