## Post

The B2B lead form had a good run.

Google's Business Agent for Leads -- announced at Google Marketing Live 2026 -- embeds a Gemini AI chat agent directly inside the ad.

Prospect clicks. AI engages in real time. Qualified lead passes to sales with actual context about what they asked and what they need.

No landing page. No 5-field form. No 48-hour follow-up sequence.

Here's the take: the lead form wasn't failing because of poor design. It was failing because buyers didn't want to start a sales process before they were ready.

An AI agent that answers real questions without scheduling a call is genuinely better for the buyer. And better for the buyer means better conversion -- the data will confirm this over the next 12 months.

Most B2B teams will add this feature and keep their existing offer. "Request a demo" with a chatbot instead of a form. They'll miss the point entirely.

The teams that win will rebuild the entry point. What does a buyer actually need before they're ready to talk? What question is behind the click? Build the agent around answering that -- not collecting fields.

The form was a gate in front of the actual conversation.

Now the conversation is the entry point.

That shifts everything -- not just lead capture, but how we should be thinking about offer design, messaging, and what buyers need before they're ready to close.
