## Post

41% of email revenue comes from 5.3% of email sends.

That stat from Klaviyo's 2026 benchmarks has been consistent for years -- and the gap keeps widening.

It's not a creative win. It's a timing win.

Automated flows trigger on behavior. Someone browses a product twice. Returns to a category page. Starts checkout and stops. The flow catches that intent signal at the right moment. A standard campaign goes to everyone on a schedule, regardless of what they just did on your site.

The revenue per recipient gap between flows and campaigns is roughly 26x.

Same email list. Same brand. Same offer. Different logic about when to send.

Here's what most brands miss: flows only work for the contacts already in the list.

Industry average opt-in rates hover around 2-3% of site traffic. Which means if you're running solid flows, they're running on a fraction of the visitors who already showed enough interest to land on your site.

The behavioral signals are there. The contact isn't -- because that visitor left before subscribing.

That's the problem we built KnownVisitors to solve. Identifying site visitors before they leave, so the traffic you're already paying to attract can actually enter your email flows and retargeting sequences.

The best flow in your stack does nothing for the visitor who bounced before opting in.
