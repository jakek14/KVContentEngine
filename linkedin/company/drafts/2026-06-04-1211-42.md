## Post

Most B2B marketing teams assume their visitor data is accurate.

Here's a quick audit that will tell you if it actually is.

Step 1: Open your visitor identification tool and look at the top companies in your recent traffic.

See any ISPs? Comcast, Cox, Verizon, AT&T, Charter? Those aren't companies. Those are home internet providers -- visitors who are working remotely and showing up as a residential IP address.

IP-to-company matching was built for an office-first world. Remote work broke that assumption, quietly.

Step 2: Run a controlled test.

Have someone from a known company visit your site. Check what your tool reports. If it shows their employer accurately, your identification layer is working. If it returns their ISP or nothing, you have a data gap affecting every audience segment you're building.

Step 3: Cross-reference with CRM data.

Pull your last 10 closed deals. Find the dates they visited your site before converting. Check whether their company appears in your visitor logs during that window. If it doesn't, your highest-intent traffic is invisible to your marketing stack.

These three checks take 20 minutes.

The gap they reveal can change how you think about every retargeting campaign you're running.
