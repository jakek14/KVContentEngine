## Post

Google just updated its advertiser terms to explicitly authorize AI to generate your ad targets, copy, and destinations on your behalf.

That's in the new AI Max campaign agreement. Phased rollout through September.

A lot of B2B marketing teams haven't caught this yet.

The practical shift: the levers advertisers used to control directly -- which keywords triggered the ad, which landing pages traffic went to, what the ad actually said -- are increasingly being decided by an automated system reading your brand and website.

That changes what the job of a B2B marketer actually is.

The skill that used to matter most was bid management and campaign architecture. The skill that matters now is brand clarity. Clear positioning. Landing pages that convert cold traffic. Messaging that's specific enough for an AI to run with.

If your brand foundation is vague, AI automation will amplify that vagueness at scale.

The advertisers who are going to win in this environment aren't the ones who resist the automation. They're the ones who give the AI something real to work with.
