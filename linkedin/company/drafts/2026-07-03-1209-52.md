## Post

The average startup team size is now 4.3 people.

That's not a reaction to overhiring. That's AI tools collapsing how many people you need to operate at a high level.

Small teams ship faster. They make decisions without six weeks of approval cycles. And they buy software very differently.

The old B2B SaaS sales playbook -- long demo cycles, champion programs, stakeholder maps, procurement reviews -- doesn't fit a team of four.

They evaluate a tool in a day. They decide in a week. They cancel in 30 minutes if it doesn't deliver.

This is the buyer profile that's growing fastest right now. And they're allergic to friction.

If your onboarding, pricing, and product were built for a 200-person enterprise team, you're actively working against the buyers expanding fastest in 2026.

What works for this segment: clear value in the first session, no forced demo to get access, pricing that works at small scale, and a product that respects their time.

That's not a startup-friendly feature. That's just where the market went.
