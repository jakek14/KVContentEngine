## Post

High-intent B2B buyers are researching your category in ChatGPT before they ever reach your website.

Not all buyers. But the ones who know exactly what problem they have and what kind of tool they're looking for -- they're typing it into an AI interface, not a search bar.

What comes back is a synthesized answer that either mentions your company or doesn't.

If it doesn't, those buyers may never encounter your brand at all.

This is already happening at scale. Google AI Mode now accounts for over 60% of search journeys that end without a click to any external site. Perplexity and Claude are handling a growing share of B2B research queries.

Getting cited in AI answers isn't accidental. A few things that help:

Content that directly answers the specific questions your ICP searches for -- not just "best [category] tools" but the problem-level questions they're actually asking

Consistent presence in third-party publications, industry directories, and credible sources that AI systems pull from

Pages that clearly explain what your product does, who it's for, and what problem it solves -- with no jargon, no vague positioning

The brands building for AI search visibility now will have outsized awareness in 12 months. The ones optimizing purely for keyword rankings are losing buyers they never meet.
