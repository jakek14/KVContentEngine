## Post

Meta is building a system to fully automate ad creation by late 2026.

Image. Video. Copy. Targeting. All generated automatically.

Meta cut 8,000 employees and reassigned 7,000 to AI projects. This isn't a product experiment -- it's a strategic bet on where advertising is going.

The marketer's job on Meta is shifting. Less making the ad. More managing the signal you feed the machine.

Audience quality, first-party data, and funnel clarity become the competitive edge when creative production is automated.

The input matters more when the machine builds the output.
