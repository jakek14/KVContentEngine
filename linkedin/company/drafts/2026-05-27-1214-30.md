## Post

Most "alignment" between sales and marketing is both teams agreeing to measure different things.

Marketing tracks leads, impressions, MQLs.

Sales tracks demos, pipeline, closes.

The research phase -- where a B2B buyer is actively evaluating but hasn't raised their hand -- gets measured by neither.

And that's where most of the buying decision actually forms.

A B2B buyer completes 70-80% of their purchase journey before they ever talk to sales. That research happens on your site, in search results, in private Slack conversations, in AI-assisted comparisons. It leaves traces. But most teams aren't reading them.

The result: sales calls a lead "not ready" and marketing marks it as a nurture. Meanwhile the buyer already made their decision somewhere else -- somewhere neither team could see.

Real alignment isn't about the same dashboard. It's about both teams operating on the same signal from the research phase -- before the form fill, before the demo request, before anyone officially raised their hand.

That's what we built KnownVisitors to surface.

The research phase isn't invisible. It just requires a different kind of data.
