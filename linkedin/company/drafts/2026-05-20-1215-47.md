## Post

Shopify just opened its B2B features to every merchant — not just Plus subscribers.

For the first time, a brand can manage wholesale and DTC from a single platform without patchwork plugins or multiple tools.

The headline: Shopify made B2B easier.

The real story: the DTC-only brand is becoming a competitive disadvantage.

---

Merchants using Shopify B2B see up to a 4.1x increase in reorder frequency compared to DTC-only orders.

Reorder frequency is the metric that determines whether a brand builds lasting revenue or runs an acquisition treadmill.

DTC captures the first purchase.

B2B captures the repeating business.

The brands running both channels don't need to acquire new customers at the same pace to maintain their revenue curve.

---

There's a bigger shift underneath this:

B2B buyers and DTC buyers increasingly want the same things — frictionless checkout, self-serve access, transparent pricing, no sales call required.

The brands building for both are discovering the B2B buyer isn't always a different person from the DTC customer.

They're often the same company buying for themselves and for their business.

The playbook is converging.

The platforms just caught up.
