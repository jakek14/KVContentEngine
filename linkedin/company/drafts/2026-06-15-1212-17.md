## Post

97% of CMOs are expanding creator marketing budgets in 2026.

That's not a budget trend. It's a trust problem being solved in public.

B2B buyers don't trust brand ads the way they used to. They trust people who work in their space, share real experience, and talk about problems they recognize. Creator content fills a gap polished marketing can't -- not because it looks more polished, but because it sounds like someone who's actually been there.

For B2B brands, the most effective creators aren't celebrities or lifestyle influencers. They're practitioners. Operators. People who've lived the exact problem your product solves.

A few things worth knowing before you build a creator program:

Platform specificity beats audience size. A creator with 8,000 engaged LinkedIn followers in your vertical will consistently outperform one with 200,000 generalist followers. Match the creator's audience to your ICP, not just their reach numbers.

The creative brief needs to be loose. Creators who sound like brand copy convert less. Give them the key facts and the hook angle -- then let them tell it in their own voice. That's the whole point.

Authenticity shows up in the data fast. Sponsored content that performs has one consistent trait: the creator actually believes in the category or has used the product. Audience trust is fragile and prices in almost immediately.

In 2026, the B2B brands winning trust aren't the ones with the biggest media budgets. They're the ones with the most credible voices in the room.
