## Post

Something structural is shifting in how B2B buyers research.

They're not just using Google anymore. A growing segment of buyers start their vendor evaluation inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini. They describe their problem. They get a summary of options. They compare vendors -- sometimes deeply -- without ever visiting a single company's website.

The traffic that used to come from early-stage research is being absorbed by AI tools.

What that means for your website: the visitors who do show up are further into the process than they used to be. Traffic from AI search referrals converts 22% better than traditional organic, and the direction of that gap is growing. The buyers who click through to your site from an AI summary have already filtered themselves. They've seen the comparison. They chose to learn more about you specifically.

Your website is no longer a discovery tool for many buyers. It's the validation step.

That changes what the site needs to do.

The home page copy that explains what you are and who you're for -- still necessary, but it's not doing as much heavy lifting for this segment. What matters more: can a buyer who already knows roughly what they need quickly confirm you're the right fit? Case studies, specific outcomes, proof over claims.

It also changes what intelligence you need. If every visitor is further into their decision than they were two years ago, knowing who those visitors are carries more weight. Not for volume -- you're getting fewer of them through the top. For quality -- the ones showing up are worth more.

The brands that adapt fastest won't be the ones who drive the most traffic. They'll be the ones who extract the most signal from the traffic they get.
