## Post

Most B2B visitor tracking tools will tell you which company visited your website.

That's not the same as knowing who to call.

There's a real gap between "Acme Corp was on your pricing page" and "Sarah Chen, VP of Marketing at Acme Corp, spent 8 minutes on your pricing page yesterday."

Company-level data points you toward an org.

Person-level data tells you exactly who to reach, when, and with what context.

The follow-up conversation changes completely.

"We see your company has been checking us out" is generic.

"Hi Sarah, noticed you were reviewing our pricing -- happy to answer any questions" is relevant.

Company IP matching was the first generation of visitor identification. It was a meaningful start. It's not the finish line.

The market is moving toward person-level data -- not because it's a nice-to-have, but because company-level data still leaves you guessing which person inside that company actually cares.

That's what KnownVisitors is built to solve. Not "which company is on your site" -- but "exactly who."

Name. Email. Phone. No form required.
