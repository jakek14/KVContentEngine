## Post

The bar for B2B SaaS in 2026 isn't what it was in 2023.

Investors aren't asking about your growth rate first.

They're asking: do your customers stay, expand, and tell others?

---

The benchmarks have shifted.

Sub-12 month CAC payback. 80%+ gross retention. Expansion revenue that grows without a proportional sales cost increase.

That's not a stretch goal. That's now the baseline for a credible Series A conversation.

The signal investors are chasing is "proof of repeated customer behavior" — evidence that real people chose to keep paying, not just that someone persuaded them to start.

---

What this means in practice:

The companies struggling to raise right now often look fine on the surface. Solid ARR, decent growth, reasonable burn.

But when you look at the underlying metrics — net revenue retention, expansion rates, logo churn — the story gets complicated.

The ones winning are almost always the companies that chose a narrow, painful problem with a defined customer, and then relentlessly kept that customer happy.

---

The funding market isn't broken.

It's just honest.

It's separating the companies that acquired customers from the ones that earned them.
