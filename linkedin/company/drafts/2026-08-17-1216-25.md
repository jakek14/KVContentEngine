## Post

Google Performance Max got two updates this summer that most advertisers haven't fully explored yet.

First: structured A/B testing for asset groups. You can now compare entire creative sets or isolate individual assets in a controlled split. Before this, PMax creative testing was a black box. Now you can find out what's actually working and why.

Second: retention lifecycle bidding. PMax now lets you set a retention goal -- bidding more aggressively to win back lapsed customers, not just to find new ones. If reactivation is a real problem in your business, this is a new lever.

Here's where most B2B teams will hit a wall on retention bidding.

It works through customer match. You upload a list, and PMax uses it to identify and bid on those people inside Google's ecosystem.

Most B2B teams upload their CRM list -- a few hundred or a few thousand names from customers who actually converted. But what about the visitors who came to your pricing page multiple times, never converted, and then went quiet? They're not in your CRM. They're not in your customer match list.

That's where KnownVisitors fits. We identify anonymous visitors to your site -- name, email, company -- so your customer match list includes high-intent non-converters, not just people who already bought.

Retention bidding is only as powerful as the list you feed it.
