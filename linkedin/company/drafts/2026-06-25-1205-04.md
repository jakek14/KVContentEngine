## Post

Google just rolled out three bidding and budgeting updates. Here's what actually changed and what to do with it.

**Promotion Mode (beta)**

New feature that lets you schedule temporary boosts to your ROAS tolerance and daily budget during peak windows -- product launches, seasonal events, flash sales. You define the window, Smart Bidding adjusts, then resets automatically when it ends.

This is different from Seasonality Adjustments, which only signal an expected change in conversion rate. Promotion Mode actually lets the algorithm bid more aggressively and spend more during the window you define.

To access it: contact your Google Ads account team. Still in beta and not available to everyone yet.

**Smart Bidding Exploration -- now rolling out globally**

Now available in all Performance Max campaigns. Designed to find converting queries you're not currently capturing. Google's reported data: 18% more unique converting query categories, 19% more conversions on average.

It's opt-in. Worth turning on if you haven't yet.

**Bidding target optimization**

Google will now auto-recommend CPA or ROAS target adjustments when your current targets are too conservative -- limiting volume without improving efficiency. You can review and accept or dismiss each recommendation.

Treat these as data points, not mandates. Google's goal is more spend. Your goal is more profit. Those overlap, but not perfectly.

The bigger pattern here: Google is building toward a model where advertisers provide inputs -- budget, targets, creative, product feed -- and the algorithm handles the rest. The quality of your inputs matters more than ever.

If you haven't reviewed your Performance Max settings this month, worth doing this week.
