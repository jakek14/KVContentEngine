## Post

B2B cost-per-lead hit $214 in Q1 2026.

That's an 11% jump year-over-year. Driven by paid channel saturation, stricter privacy enforcement, and the accelerating shift away from third-party cookies.

The companies keeping their CPL flat -- or improving it -- have one thing in common.

They stopped depending on data they rent and started building data they own.

First-party data used to be a "nice to have." It's the moat now.

Your website is one of the richest first-party data sources you have. Every page view, every pricing visit, every return session -- that's intent signal. Most B2B companies aren't capturing it, not because the signal doesn't exist, but because their visitors aren't filling out forms.

That's exactly the gap KnownVisitors closes. When someone visits your site, we identify who they are -- name, email, company -- without a form required.

The CPL problem isn't going away. The companies building owned signals now will be in a different position in 12 months than the ones still bidding for rented audiences.
