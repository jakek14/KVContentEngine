## Post

Conversion drops 80% if you wait more than 5 minutes to follow up with a new lead.

Five minutes.

Most B2B sequences don't even send the first email until an hour after a form fill -- if it's automated. If follow-up is manual, the average first touch is closer to 24 hours.

Here's the problem: when a prospect fills out a form, they're in the problem-solving mindset. Actively thinking about the solution. That window is short.

Five minutes later, they're on the next tab. The next task. The next thing competing for their attention.

By the time most teams reach out, the prospect's brain has already moved on.

What smart B2B teams are doing about it:

First, automate the first touch. A personalized email within seconds of a form fill isn't aggressive -- it's just meeting someone where they are.

Second, trigger based on intent, not just forms. If someone hits your pricing page three times in a week, that's a signal worth acting on. Not after you notice it in analytics. Now.

Third, use AI agents for the first response. The goal isn't a sales pitch -- it's acknowledging interest and opening a door while the door is open.

Speed isn't just a nice-to-have. It's a conversion variable.
