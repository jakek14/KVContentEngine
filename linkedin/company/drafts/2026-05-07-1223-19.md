## Post

Google wants you to hand the keys to AI Max.

More signal sharing. Broader query matching. Less manual control over which searches trigger your ads.

The pitch: trust the algorithm — it knows your customers better than you do.

For large advertisers with millions of conversions feeding the model, that's probably right.

For everyone else, it's worth asking a harder question first: does my account have enough conversion data for AI Max to make better decisions than I can make manually?

---

AI-driven targeting learns from your conversion signal.

If that signal is sparse — a few hundred conversions, a narrow ICP, specific keywords where intent actually matters — the algorithm doesn't have enough to work with.

It expands into adjacent searches that look similar but convert differently. Budget bleeds into lower-intent traffic. CPCs rise. The quality of visitors reaching your site drops.

This isn't a knock on Google's automation. At scale, it consistently outperforms manual management.

The issue is that "at scale" is doing a lot of work in that sentence.

---

Before you extend AI Max's reach on your next campaign, get clear on one thing:

Is your account teaching the algorithm what good looks like — or is the algorithm still guessing?

If you're guessing, you're paying for the lesson.
