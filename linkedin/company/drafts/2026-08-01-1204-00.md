## Post

"Visited your website" is the weakest retargeting signal you have.

It means someone loaded a page. That's all. No intent. No buying signal. Just a cookie fired.

You're paying to serve ads to anyone who wandered in -- including your competitors, job candidates reviewing your company page, people who clicked the wrong link.

The B2B teams getting the best retargeting ROI right now are building audiences on signals that actually indicate interest:

Spent 3+ minutes on the site. Not a bounce. They were reading something.

Viewed pricing AND a case study in the same session. High-intent combination -- they're evaluating.

Returned two or more times in a 7-day window. Came back on their own. That's worth noticing.

Engaged with problem-aware pages: feature comparisons, ROI calculators, solutions pages.

Same budget. Better audiences. Dramatically different conversion rates.

The problem isn't that retargeting doesn't work. It's that "visited your website" is too blunt an instrument to be worth much on its own.

Sharpen the signal before you raise the spend.

What signal is currently driving your highest-converting retargeting audiences?
