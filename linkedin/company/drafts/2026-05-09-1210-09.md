## Post

LinkedIn video is outperforming static content by 69% in 2026.

Most B2B brands are still posting graphics.

---

Here's what the data shows for B2B video on LinkedIn right now:

Average engagement rate for video: 5.60% — significantly higher than static posts.

The average high-performing B2B video has compressed from 168 seconds in 2024 to just 76 seconds today. Shorter, not longer.

Vertical format generates 34% higher engagement and 34% longer dwell time compared to standard landscape.

Captions aren't optional — LinkedIn videos autoplay without sound. If your message lives in the audio, most people never hear it.

---

The mistake most B2B teams make with LinkedIn video: repurposing content that wasn't built for the format.

A clipped webinar panel isn't a LinkedIn video. A polished brand reel isn't a LinkedIn video.

What actually works: educational explainers under 90 seconds, direct takes from a founder or practitioner, honest behind-the-scenes content that shows how your team thinks.

The goal isn't production value. It's trust-building at scale.

---

The highest-intent leads your video content drives aren't the ones who comment.

They're the ones who quietly watch, get curious, and visit your website.

That's the connection most teams miss — the journey from video viewer to site visitor isn't tracked unless you're actively identifying who shows up.
