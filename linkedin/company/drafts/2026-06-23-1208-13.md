## Post

88% of marketers now say they're using AI in their day-to-day work.

Only 35% have moved beyond isolated experiments and scaled it across their operations.

That gap is the whole story.

Most teams have AI tools. They use them for one-off tasks -- draft this email, generate this image, summarize this report. The workflow around those tasks stays the same. Output might improve. The process doesn't.

The organizations pulling ahead have done something different. They didn't add AI to their workflow. They rebuilt the workflow around AI.

That's a different kind of investment. Not in tools -- in rethinking how the work gets done.

At KnownVisitors, we built our data pipeline to be AI-native from the start. Identification, verification, compliance checks, delivery -- automated end to end. The teams seeing the most value from what we surface are the ones who've done the same with their marketing operations.

If AI is still a tab you open when you need something quick, the gap is going to keep widening.
