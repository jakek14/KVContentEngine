## Post

Your contact form is converting about 2% of visitors.

That number doesn't mean the form needs better copy or fewer fields.

It means 98% of people who clicked your ad, read your page, spent real time -- said no to the form and walked away.

Here's what nobody talks about: intent doesn't disappear when someone skips the form. They still visited. They still read something. They still had a problem that brought them there.

Forms create an asymmetry. Visitors who fill them out identify themselves. You can follow up. Everyone else stays anonymous forever.

The most forward-thinking B2B marketing teams aren't just optimizing their forms. They're questioning why the form is the only moment they learn something about who visited.

Your website isn't a funnel with a gate at the bottom. It's full of people who showed up for a reason.

Treat it that way.
