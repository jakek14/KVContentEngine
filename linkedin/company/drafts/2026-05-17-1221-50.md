## Post

Your next customer might find you inside ChatGPT, Gemini, or Google AI Mode before they ever search on Google.

Shopify just made this real — millions of merchants now sell directly inside AI chatbots. But you don't need to be a Shopify merchant for this to matter.

---

The shift has a name: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). And the teams who get ahead of it now will have a compounding advantage as organic and paid channels get more crowded and expensive.

Here's what actually moves the needle:

Answer the exact question your buyer is asking. AI tools surface content that directly answers a query — not just what's well-optimized for keywords. If your pricing pages, case studies, and feature breakdowns are written in the language your buyer uses when comparing options, you show up.

Own your category vocabulary. The brands consistently cited in AI answers are the ones who defined the terms first. Publish original research, frameworks, and data your category refers back to. You can't be cited if you haven't created something citable.

Build consistent, structured data. Product pages, review signals, third-party mentions, consistent business information — AI models synthesize structured information. Fragmented or thin data means invisible.

The channel is still early. The brands doing this work now will own the AI result when the category gets crowded.
