## Post

The EU AI Act reaches full enforcement in August 2026.

Penalties: up to €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover — whichever is higher.

That's on top of GDPR, which has now crossed €7.1 billion in total fines — with €1.2 billion in 2025 alone.

For the first time, enforcement isn't just targeting the biggest players. Spain issued over 1,000 enforcement actions last year. Most of them hit mid-market companies.

---

Here's the part most marketing teams haven't fully processed:

The EU AI Act doesn't just apply to AI products. It applies to AI used in workflows that affect people.

Marketing automation. Behavioral targeting. Algorithmic lead scoring. Personalization engines. Predictive CRM models.

If your stack uses any of these to make automated decisions about users, you may already be in scope.

Not maybe. Probably.

---

There are two kinds of companies right now.

The ones that treated compliance as overhead. They're now running retroactive audits with legal on speed dial, mapping their entire tool stack for the first time.

And the ones that built clean, consent-based, verified data practices from the start. They're not scrambling. They're competing.

Compliance built ahead of requirements isn't a cost center.

It becomes a competitive advantage.

---

If your team hasn't mapped your marketing stack against EU AI Act risk tiers before August, now is the time to start.

The window for "we'll deal with it later" is closing fast.
