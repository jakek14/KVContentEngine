## Post

Most B2B teams rank their own website near the bottom of their lead gen channel mix.

Cold email. LinkedIn outreach. Paid search. SEO. All ahead of it.

But your website visitors are already warm.

They found you. They read your content, checked your pricing, compared you against alternatives. That's a fundamentally different prospect than someone you cold-messaged who's never heard of you.

The problem: 98% of those visitors never fill out a form. They land, look around, and leave. Most teams can't do anything with that -- no follow-up, no routing to sales, no precision retargeting.

The shift happening in 2026 is treating website visitors the same way you'd treat any qualified lead. Because that's exactly what they are. They came to you. You just can't see them yet.

That's the problem KnownVisitors was built to solve. Every visitor who lands on your site, we identify -- name, email, company, location. No form required. You own the data and decide what to do with it.

Your best lead gen channel is already getting traffic. Most teams just can't see who's on it.
