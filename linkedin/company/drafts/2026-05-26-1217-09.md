## Post

For the first time in history, Meta is projected to surpass Google in global ad revenue this year.

$243 billion vs. $239 billion. Meta growing at 24%. Google at 12%.

That's not a trend. It's a structural shift.

---

For years, the default B2B marketing assumption was: Google owns intent, Meta is consumer territory.

The logic made sense. Someone searching "B2B software for X" is in buying mode. Capture them there.

Meta was written off as the wrong context for business decisions — too casual, wrong mindset, wrong audience.

That assumption is aging quickly.

B2B buyers don't stop being people when they open Instagram or Facebook. The brand they've seen repeatedly on social is already in their consideration set when they sit down to search.

Awareness happens before intent. Most teams are only showing up for the intent.

---

The companies winning at B2B growth right now aren't choosing between intent and awareness channels.

They're running both.

Capturing demand on search. Building it on social.

The platform growing 24% while your competitors treat it as a consumer channel is, by definition, an opportunity gap.

---

The Meta vs. Google revenue flip isn't just an ad industry story.

It's a map of where attention is moving.

The question for B2B marketers: are your buyers on Meta?

They almost certainly are.
