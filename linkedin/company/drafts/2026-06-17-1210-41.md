## Post

Retail media networks will hit $163 billion in global ad spend this year.

Amazon alone brings in $60 billion. Walmart grew 46% last year to $6.4 billion. Together they capture nearly 90% of incremental US retail media dollars.

That's a staggering amount of budget moving toward these platforms. Here's what the number actually represents.

Brands aren't paying for better creative tools or smarter bidding interfaces.

They're paying for data.

Amazon knows who searched for a product. Who clicked. Who bought. Who returned it and bought something else. That closed-loop view of customer behavior -- from intent signal to purchase decision to lifetime value -- is what makes Amazon's ad platform worth $60 billion. The ads are just how they monetize what they know.

Most brands paying into that system don't have the same view of their own customers.

They spend money acquiring traffic, converting a small fraction, and watching the rest disappear without knowing who they were, what they looked at, or whether they came back.

That's the gap retail media fills -- and that's why brands keep paying.

Here's the reframe: the data advantage retail media sells isn't exclusive to retail media platforms. It lives on your website.

Every visitor who lands on your site, browses your products, reads your comparison page, or comes back three times without converting is carrying the same signal Amazon captures. Intent. Behavior. Return visits. Buying stage.

The difference is whether you know who they are.

The brands winning on their own channels -- not just renting Amazon's -- are the ones building first-party audience data from the traffic they already own. Identified visitors feeding their email flows, their retargeting audiences, their sales team follow-up queues.

That's what we built KnownVisitors for. Not to replicate what Amazon does -- but to give companies the same closed-loop view of their own visitors that retail media charges a premium to provide.

Retail media's advantage is real. You don't have to keep renting it from someone else.
