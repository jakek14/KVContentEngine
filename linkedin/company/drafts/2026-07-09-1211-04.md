## Post

Google just announced an AI agent that reads your website in real time and answers buyer questions inside the ad.

The Business Agent handles things like 'does this work with Shopify?' before a buyer ever clicks. Then it hands them off with a pre-filled lead form.

The ad becomes the first sales conversation.

Buyers who reach your site have already gotten answers. They arrive warmer. The gap between 'interested' and 'moved on' is getting shorter -- not because buyers are less patient, but because the process is faster.

For B2B companies, this changes two things.

Your website copy matters more than it ever has. The agent reads it and mirrors it back to buyers. Vague, jargon-heavy language produces vague, unhelpful answers. Clear and specific produces clear and helpful.

Knowing who is in-market matters more. When someone clicks through after a conversation with the agent, they're not a casual browser. They've already pre-qualified themselves. Understanding who that person is has more value than it did before.

Most advertisers will focus on optimizing the click. The ones who get ahead will optimize the entire experience that follows.
