## Post

Threads just opened ads to every advertiser globally. 400 million monthly users.

B2B brands are mostly sitting this one out because it "feels casual."

That was the exact logic applied to LinkedIn in 2010. And Instagram in 2015.

The brands that moved early on each of those platforms built audiences and cost efficiency that later entrants couldn't replicate. CPMs are always cheapest before the rest of the market shows up.

Threads isn't LinkedIn. The content that works there is shorter, more conversational, and less polished than what B2B brands typically produce. But that's an advantage for companies willing to engage like a real company instead of a corporate account.

400 million monthly users includes a significant slice of the same decision-makers your LinkedIn campaigns are targeting -- at a fraction of the CPM.

Early data suggests this is a first-mover window. Those windows don't stay open indefinitely.

The question isn't whether Threads is the right fit for your brand. It's whether you want to test and find out now, or watch someone else figure it out first.
