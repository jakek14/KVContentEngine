## Post

Most marketing budgets are built around channels.

The real allocation question is different.

It's not "how much for Google vs. LinkedIn vs. Meta?"

It's "where does human judgment create irreplaceable value — and where should automation be doing the work instead?"

The teams getting the most from their budgets in 2026 aren't outspending anyone.

They're ruthlessly honest about where humans shouldn't be involved at all.

Creative direction? Human.

Media buying optimization? Automation.

Customer insight and positioning strategy? Human.

Campaign monitoring and bid management? Automation.

The mistake isn't always spending on the wrong channels.

Sometimes it's spending human hours on work a system runs better — and then wondering why the output doesn't reflect the talent in the room.

What's one thing your team still does manually that you know should be automated by now?
