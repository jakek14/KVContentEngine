## Post

83% of B2B buyers only reach out to sales after they've already completed 70% of their research.

That means the most important sales conversation your company is having isn't the demo call.

It's the anonymous session on your website two weeks before anyone contacts you.

By the time a prospect fills out a form, they've already compared you to competitors. They've read your pricing page. They've decided whether you seem legit. That entire evaluation happens without a single conversation.

The problem: most B2B companies have no visibility into that phase. Someone spent 14 minutes on your pricing page, read two case studies, and left. You have a session in your analytics. You don't have a name.

That's the gap KnownVisitors closes -- identifying the people doing that research before they ever raise their hand, so your team can reach out while the decision is still being made.

The best time to reach a B2B buyer isn't when they're ready. It's while they're still deciding.
