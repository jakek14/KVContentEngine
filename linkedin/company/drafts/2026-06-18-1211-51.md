## Post

20 US states now have comprehensive privacy laws.

Indiana, Kentucky, and Rhode Island joined the list this year. And CCPA went further -- mandatory privacy risk assessments are now required before any data processing that poses "significant risk" to consumers. New rules specifically for cookies and pixels. Stricter data broker registration requirements, effective January 1, 2026.

Most B2B marketing teams set their compliance framework once -- sometime in 2021 or 2022 -- and haven't revisited it since.

The landscape is materially different now. Here are three questions every marketing team should be able to answer before the end of this quarter:

Do you have a current data inventory? The new CCPA risk assessment requirement means you need to document every vendor or pixel touching visitor data on your site. Not theoretically. Actually, with a written record. If you can't name every third-party tool on your pages, you're already behind.

Are your cookies and pixels still compliant? The 2026 CCPA update added new rules specifically for cookies and pixel-based tracking. If your consent management platform hasn't been reviewed since 2022, it's likely insufficient for the current requirements.

What's your data broker exposure? New data broker registration requirements took effect January 1. Any enrichment vendor or contact list provider you're using needs to meet the current standard. Buying non-compliant data is no longer just a vendor problem -- it's yours.

Privacy law is not moving backward. The gap between teams treating compliance as a checkbox and the ones treating it as infrastructure keeps getting more expensive to close.
