## Post

Ecommerce CAC climbed 40-60% since 2023.

Most marketing teams treat that as an acquisition problem -- more budget, better targeting, cheaper clicks.

Here's the reframe: when acquisition costs rise, the value of every high-intent visitor already on your site goes up proportionally.

Someone who landed on your pricing page isn't a random user. They paid for the click. They showed intent. If you can identify who they are, you have a prospect who's already raised their hand -- without spending another dollar to acquire them.

That's the math most B2B and e-commerce teams aren't running.

The question isn't "how do we get more traffic." It's "how much of the intent already arriving are we actually capturing?"

When CAC climbs, the ROI gap between a visitor who converts to a known lead and one who leaves anonymously grows with it.

That's why we built KnownVisitors -- to close that gap by identifying who lands on your site before they leave.

In 2026, visitor identification isn't a nice-to-have.

It's what makes the acquisition math work.
