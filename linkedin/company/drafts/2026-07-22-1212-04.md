## Post

80% of B2B companies are running some kind of ABM program.

Most of them are targeting the wrong accounts.

Not wrong in terms of ICP -- they've got the firmographic profile right. Wrong in terms of timing.

ABM works when target accounts are in some form of active evaluation. But the average program runs on a static list and hopes the outreach lands when intent happens to be present.

The data tells the story: tier-1 ABM programs see 3.4x engagement lift over non-ABM cohorts. Companies with strong programs attribute 73% of total revenue to ABM efforts.

We'd argue the gap between "running ABM" and "ABM drives 73% of revenue" comes down to one thing -- behavioral signal on top of firmographic fit.

Firmographic targeting gets you to the right company.

Behavioral signal gets you there at the right time.

Most ABM programs have the first. The ones hitting 73% revenue attribution have both.
