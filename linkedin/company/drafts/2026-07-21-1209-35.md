## Post

GDPR cumulative fines hit €7.1 billion.

The EU AI Act reaches full enforcement for high-risk AI systems in August -- adding a second penalty layer that can reach €35 million, or 7% of global revenue.

And the European Data Protection Board just named the right to erasure as a 2026 enforcement priority.

Three signals landing in the same quarter.

For B2B companies running any kind of data-driven marketing -- visitor tracking, behavioral targeting, personalization, lead enrichment -- this isn't abstract. The enforcement shift is from "did a breach occur" to "do you have structural controls in place." Missing encryption, weak vendor management, inadequate documentation: these are now penalty-worthy on their own.

The companies getting caught aren't all negligent. Some just never built the compliance infrastructure to match the data practices they adopted.

A few things worth auditing before August:

Data you're collecting and how it's stored. Vendor contracts and what your processors can do with the data. Your erasure process -- can you actually fulfill a deletion request within 30 days? Cross-border transfer mechanisms if you're moving EU data to US systems.

Enforcement is no longer theoretical. The fine is the signal that regulators have moved to action, not warning.
