## Post

New customer acquisition cost rose 14% last year.

Median SaaS growth rates compressed to 26%.

That math creates a slow squeeze. You're spending more to grow less.

The companies still standing in three years won't necessarily be the ones with the biggest ad budgets. They'll be the ones who built a moat around their existing traffic and customers.

Here's what the smart ones are doing instead of just spending more:

Investing in organic reach over paid — content, SEO, community, partnerships

Building owned audiences — email lists, CRM segments, retargeted customer bases

Activating website visitors before they bounce — not relying solely on forms to capture intent

Focusing on expansion and retention over net-new acquisition only

The brands running purely on paid acquisition are on an expensive treadmill. The brands building owned data and owned channels are building a compounding asset.

CAC doesn't have to keep rising if your baseline keeps warming up.

What's working for your team right now?
