## Post

Hightouch just raised $150 million to build AI agents that run marketing campaigns autonomously.

$2.75 billion valuation. The bet: give the AI a goal and let it execute.

Hot take: this round isn't really about the AI.

It's about who owns the data underneath it.

AI marketing agents are only as useful as the context they have access to. Customer behavior. Purchase history. Site intent signals. The full picture of who your prospect is and what they've done.

Without that data layer, the agent is just a faster way to reach the wrong people.

The companies that win the agentic marketing era won't necessarily have the best model.

They'll have the best inputs.

Data is still the moat. The AI just made that truth more visible.
