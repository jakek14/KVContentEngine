## Post

As of August 1, California's Delete Act went fully operational.

The DROP platform -- a centralized deletion mechanism -- is now live. Any consumer in California can submit one deletion request and every covered data broker must comply.

For B2B teams that buy prospect lists: this matters more than it sounds.

A significant portion of the contact data being sold by list vendors has California residents in it. That data is now subject to mandatory deletion at the consumer's request, with enforcement teeth behind it. If your outbound strategy relies heavily on purchased lists, your data quality problem just got structurally worse.

The teams navigating this cleanly are the ones who moved earlier -- toward first-party, consent-aware data. Contacts who opted in, filled out a form, engaged with content. People who actually want to be reached.

What we build at KnownVisitors sits at this intersection. Every contact delivered is scrubbed against TCPA, CAN-SPAM, and GDPR/CCPA frameworks before it reaches you. Compliance isn't a feature we added on. It's how the product works.

The companies treating compliance as a cost center are about to find out it's actually a competitive advantage.
