## Post

98% of your B2B website visitors never fill out a form.

That's not a copywriting problem. Not a UX problem.

It's a data infrastructure gap -- and most marketing teams haven't named it yet.

The visitors landing on your pricing page, returning twice in a week, reading your case studies -- they're signaling real buying intent. You just can't act on it because you can't see who they are.

The shift happening in 2026: the best B2B marketing teams aren't waiting for prospects to raise their hand. They're identifying the anonymous visitors who showed purchase intent before the form fill.

Person-level identification, not just company-level. Not "a visitor from a mid-size tech company viewed your pricing page." The specific person, with a verified email and contact information, scrubbed for compliance before delivery.

That's the infrastructure gap between teams with full pipelines and teams wondering why their traffic doesn't convert.

We built KnownVisitors to close it -- surfacing the identity of anonymous site visitors so marketing and sales teams can act on intent that was already there.

The signal exists. It just needs to be connected to a person.
