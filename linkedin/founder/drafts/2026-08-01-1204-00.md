## Post

US privacy law just got a lot more complex.

January 2026 brought significant CCPA amendments. Connecticut nearly tripled its covered business count in July. We're now at 20 state comprehensive privacy laws -- and enforcement is no longer theoretical. GDPR fines alone have passed €5.88 billion.

Most founders still treat this as a legal checkbox. But the actual risk is operational. And the ones getting ahead of it are turning compliance into positioning.

Three things your product and marketing team should be doing right now:

Honor Global Privacy Control signals automatically. The January CCPA rules require it. GPC is already supported in most consent management platforms -- if you're not recognizing it, you're out of compliance in California.

Audit your pixels and cookies. The new CCPA rules introduced data broker requirements and changed how pixels are classified. A tracking script you added in 2023 might need a different consent framework today.

Document what data you actually collect. Not what your privacy policy says -- what's actually being collected, from where, and by which third-party scripts. Founders who've done this audit are consistently surprised by what they find.

The brands that move proactively on this earn a genuine trust advantage. "We're compliant before we're required to be" is a message your buyers notice.

Most of your competitors are still reacting. That window doesn't stay open.
