## Post

Vibe coding gets talked about as a way to build products faster.

That's not the most interesting thing about it.

The interesting part is what happens to a company's operational layer when coding becomes accessible to everyone -- not just engineering.

I'm not talking about the core product. I'm talking about the reporting dashboard you never got around to building. The internal tool your team has needed for six months but wasn't worth scoping a sprint for. The automation that would have required three handoffs just to write the ticket.

Those things now ship in hours.

Most of the conversation around vibe coding focuses on founders building MVPs or prototypes. And that's real. But the actual leverage -- for a company that's past the prototype stage -- is in the unglamorous infrastructure.

The internal tooling. The workflow automations. The connector that links two systems your team has been bridging manually in spreadsheets every week.

That's where I've felt the most acceleration. Not from building faster. From building things we never would have built at all.
