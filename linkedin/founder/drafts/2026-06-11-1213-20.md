## Post

If you post on YouTube, Google just gave you a free remarketing audience.

As of June 10, Google automatically linked YouTube channels to Google Ads for anyone who hadn't connected them manually. Your video viewers -- everyone who watched your tutorials, product demos, founder stories, anything -- is now a retargetable audience sitting in your ad account.

No setup. No new pixels. No new tools.

The implication is bigger than it sounds. Most brands treat YouTube views as one-time impressions: someone watches, it's gone. But if those viewers now live in your remarketing pool, every video you've ever published retroactively becomes a lead gen asset. Every view you've accumulated is an audience you can now run ads to directly.

Content compounds when the platform is built to let it.

The founders building on YouTube with this in mind are going to look smart in 12 months. The ones who haven't started yet now have a specific reason to. You don't need a massive channel. You need enough content to build a warm audience -- then let paid amplify what organic already started.

The gap between "I post on YouTube" and "YouTube is feeding my paid funnel automatically" just closed. Worth knowing about.
