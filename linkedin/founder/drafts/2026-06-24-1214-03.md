## Post

The companies commanding premium multiples in 2026 aren't the ones that grew fastest.

They're the ones that stayed efficient.

Series A used to mean $1-3M ARR. In 2026 the standard is $5-10M. That bar doubled in two years.

But the deeper pattern is more interesting than the threshold.

The founders hitting those numbers and raising at premium multiples aren't the ones who ran lean out of necessity. They ran lean by design -- every hire had a job, every dollar had a return, every product decision tightened rather than scattered the focus.

Efficiency as a philosophy produces a different kind of company. The balance sheet looks different. The team dynamic is different. The customer relationship is different -- because when you can't spend your way to retention, you have to earn it.

There's a version of this story that frames the current market as investors getting conservative. I think it's the opposite. They got clearer. They're rewarding companies that figured out how to build something durable.

The founders who didn't raise in 2021 and 2022 are often in the best position right now. Not because they got lucky. Because they had to build right from the start.
