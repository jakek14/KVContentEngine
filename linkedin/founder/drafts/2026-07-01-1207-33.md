## Post

Google updated its Ads Terms of Service today for the first time since 2018.

Seven changes. Most founders who run Google Ads haven't read the original, let alone the update.

The one worth understanding: Google now has explicit rights to use your ad performance data, your creative, and your conversion signals to train their AI systems. Smart Bidding, Performance Max, and Demand Gen all run on this data. The new ToS makes that usage explicit and contractual.

Opting out of automated campaign types doesn't exempt you from data usage. If you spend on Google Ads, your signals are part of the model.

Second change worth knowing: Google can now add jurisdiction-specific regulatory fees as separate line items on your invoices. If you run campaigns in Austria, France, Spain, Italy, or Turkey, there's a new cost variable to model into your unit economics.

My actual take on this: don't panic, but do read it.

The platforms you depend on have always used your performance data to improve their systems. That was always true. The difference now is that it's explicit -- which is actually more honest than before.

What this changes isn't what Google does with your data. It's that you now know exactly what you've agreed to when you run a campaign.

Read the update. Understand your agreements. Build your strategy from there.
