## Post

Traffic without identity is just a number on a dashboard.

Most founders celebrate traffic milestones. Makes sense -- traffic feels like proof something is working. But I've noticed the founders who consistently build pipeline are more interested in different questions than raw visitor counts.

Three things worth watching more closely than total sessions:

Pages visited per session. Someone who hit your pricing page, your comparison page, and two case studies in a single session is a completely different signal than someone who hit your homepage and bounced. Traffic counts don't separate these. Page depth does.

Return visitor rate. Someone who came back twice this week is telling you something specific -- and they're almost never in your lead count yet. That return visit is intent you can act on if you're paying attention.

Who actually came. Not company-level. Person-level. If you can see that a specific person spent time on your pricing page twice in the last 10 days, you have someone worth reaching out to. If your analytics only shows you aggregate session counts, you have a number with nowhere to go.

Traffic is a proxy for interest. The question is what's underneath it.

The first two things on this list are free to track -- most analytics platforms have them. Most founders aren't watching them. The third one is what KnownVisitors was built for.

But you don't need to get to the third one to start using this frame. The first two will change what you look at every morning.
