## Post

Microsoft and Google are both making major moves in AI coding right now.

Going head-to-head with Anthropic and OpenAI for the developer market.

I don't think most people have absorbed what this actually means yet.

When the two most powerful software companies on the planet decide this is where they need to win, it's not a product announcement. It's a confirmation that AI-assisted development is becoming the default infrastructure of how software gets built.

For founders building with AI tools -- which at this point is almost everyone -- here's what I think changes.

The four-way competition between Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft guarantees these tools improve fast. Whatever you're working with today will be meaningfully better in 6 months. The advantage compounds continuously if you're paying attention.

But there's a harder shift here.

The question isn't whether AI can ship production code anymore. 95% of YC's latest batch has AI-generated code. Enterprise adoption of AI coding platforms grew 340% in two years. That debate is settled.

The real question now: what do you build when everyone can build?

Speed-to-product is no longer the moat.

The moat is knowing your customer. Having the distribution. Understanding the problem deeply enough that you're building the right thing -- not just building fast.

The founders who win in this environment aren't the ones with the best AI setup. They're the ones who combine that speed with genuine clarity about who they're building for and why it matters.

The giants just put billions into validating that the tools aren't the hard part.

They never were.
