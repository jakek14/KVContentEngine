## Post

D2C first-time orders grew 33% on Shopify over the last two years.

Returning shoppers grew 59%.

Repeat buyers are outpacing new buyers by nearly 2 to 1.

That's not a coincidence. It's a signal about where the leverage actually is in scaling a consumer brand right now.

CAC has gone up 40 to 60% since 2023. Every channel reaching new customers is getting more expensive.

The acquisition machine is running harder and delivering less per dollar.

But the customers you already have are showing up again at a faster rate than ever.

Which means the brands winning right now aren't necessarily the ones with the best acquisition engine.

They're the ones with the best experience after the first purchase.

The post-purchase journey -- onboarding, win-backs, loyalty loops, personalized follow-up -- is where LTV actually gets built.

Most brands still treat it as an afterthought.

If you're spending 80% of your budget on acquisition and wondering why CAC is compressing your margins, this data deserves more of your attention.
