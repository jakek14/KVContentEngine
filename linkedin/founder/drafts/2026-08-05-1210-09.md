## Post

$510 billion in venture capital in the first half of 2026.

Every headline called it a record. Most founders saw the number and felt the market warming up.

Then I looked closer.

OpenAI and Anthropic combined took $217 billion of that. Forty-three percent of all venture capital deployed in the first six months of the year went to two companies.

The remaining $293 billion was split among tens of thousands of startups across every stage and sector. By the math, the average company's share of that remaining pool is smaller than the headlines suggest. And for traditional SaaS founders -- companies building workflow tools, vertical software, B2B infrastructure -- the market is more constrained than the record implies.

VCs are not uniformly optimistic. They're selectively optimistic. The optimism is concentrated in AI-native companies, particularly at the frontier model and infrastructure layer. For everyone else, the standard diligence questions haven't gotten easier: rule of 40, LTV:CAC, net revenue retention, payback period. Public multiples are still compressed versus 2021 peaks. Private pricing is still anchored to fundamentals.

What this means if you're thinking about raising:

The $510B headline isn't your market. Your market is whatever's left after the AI infrastructure round-up. That's still a real market -- there's still plenty of capital for the right company at the right stage. But the competitive set is every other non-AI startup trying to access the same pool.

The founders I've watched navigate this well share a few things. They know their numbers cold. They can answer the retention, expansion, and payback questions without fumbling. They don't pitch narrative first -- they lead with metrics and let the story follow. And they've built enough direct distribution (email list, community, personal credibility) that the fundraising conversation is about adding fuel, not finding product-market fit.

The founders who struggle are the ones who showed up expecting the mood to match the headlines.

The $510B record is real. But unless you're building frontier AI infrastructure, it's not your record.

Know which market you're actually in.
