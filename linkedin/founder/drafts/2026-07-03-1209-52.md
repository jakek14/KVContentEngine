## Post

Vibe coding is not what it was 12 months ago.

The conversation used to be about generating code. Asking AI to write something and being amazed when it mostly worked.

The conversation now is about shipping. Launching real products. Getting customers. Making money.

That shift happened faster than I expected. And it exposed something I think a lot of people missed: the bottleneck was never writing code.

There's a solo founder making $20k/mo from a product they completely vibe-coded -- no engineers, no technical co-founder. The barrier to building dropped to almost nothing.

But here's what I keep watching: two people can use the same AI tool, get the same output quality, and end up with completely different results.

One ships something that finds customers. The other ships something that doesn't.

The difference isn't the AI. It's what they bring to it.

Product sense. Understanding what actually needs to exist and why someone would pay for it. The ability to look at something, know it's not quite right, and iterate until it is.

None of that got easier. If anything, it matters more now -- because execution is cheap and the tools are evenly distributed.

What actually changed: the cost of a wrong bet dropped dramatically. You can discover in a week what would have taken months and serious capital to find out before.

More experiments. Faster kills. That's the real leverage -- not "build faster."

The founders winning in this environment are the ones who think clearly about problems first and use the tools second.
