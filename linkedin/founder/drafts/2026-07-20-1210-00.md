## Post

OpenAI and Anthropic absorbed 43% of all global startup funding in the first half of 2026.

Two companies. $217 billion.

The headline reads "record-breaking funding environment." The fine print reads "unless you're building something that isn't a frontier AI lab."

For founders building vertical SaaS, e-commerce tools, B2B data, or anything outside the AI infrastructure layer -- the conversation with most VCs is shorter than it was three years ago. Less patience for unproven revenue. Faster to a "no."

That isn't a complaint. It's useful information.

It means the path to scale for most founders isn't going to run through a Series A right now. It runs through customers. Revenue. Proof of demand so specific that capital becomes a tool you pick up when you need it -- not a lifeline you're waiting on.

The founders building interesting things in 2026 mostly aren't talking about their fundraise. They're talking about their churn rate.

## Context

H1 2026 set a global startup funding record, but the number is dominated by a handful of mega-rounds at the frontier AI layer. For most founders outside that layer, the effective VC market is tighter than any headline suggests.
