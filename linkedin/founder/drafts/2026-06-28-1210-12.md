## Post

For most of ecommerce history, the website was where the sale happened.

The journey started somewhere else -- search, social, email -- but the conversion happened on the page you owned.

That assumption just broke.

Shopify's Spring '26 update ships checkout directly inside AI assistants. A buyer searches in Google's AI Mode or Gemini, finds your product, pays without ever landing on your site.

The site isn't the finish line anymore. For a growing number of buyers, it's not even a stop on the route.

Most founders I talk to haven't fully absorbed what this means yet.

Your website traffic is a lagging indicator now, not the whole story. Distribution strategy has to include AI ecosystems -- not just websites, landing pages, and paid channels.

The brands figuring this out first have a window before it becomes obvious.

What's your agentic commerce strategy?
