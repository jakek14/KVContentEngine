## Post

94% of B2B buyers say trust is the primary driver of their vendor choice.

Not price. Not features. Not the pitch.

Trust.

Here's the part most marketing teams get wrong: trust isn't built on the sales call.

By the time someone books a demo, they've already formed an opinion. They read your blog. Looked at your LinkedIn. Spent time on your website comparing you to alternatives you don't even know they were considering.

The trust decision happened before you ever knew they existed.

And for most companies, that entire research phase is invisible. The visitor landed, evaluated, and left without raising their hand. No alert. No lead record. No signal that they were ever there.

The window where trust is won isn't on a call. It's when someone is quietly reading your content and deciding whether you're worth talking to.

That's the window I built KnownVisitors for. Not to interrupt that process -- but to make it visible. To know who's in the research phase so you can follow up at the right moment, before the decision goes to a competitor who happened to reach out first.

The sale almost always starts before the first conversation. Most teams are just blind to when it begins.
