## Post

Claude Code went from zero to $2.5 billion in ARR in 9 months.

That's not just a milestone. It's the fastest product category creation in enterprise software history.

And Anthropic just filed for an IPO.

The take I keep seeing: "ship with AI, move fast, big outcomes follow."

The take I'm not seeing: Claude Code grew because Anthropic already had distribution before the product shipped. Millions of developers who immediately understood what it did the moment it was available. The product met an audience that already existed.

Vibe coding removed the building bottleneck. It didn't remove the trust bottleneck.

AI will write the code. It won't build the reputation that makes a stranger trust you enough to try it. It won't create the clarity about who the product is for and why they should switch from what they're already using.

That part is still yours.

The founders winning with vibe coding aren't moving fast and hoping for the best. They're moving fast because they already knew who they were building for.

The moat hasn't changed. The speed of getting there has.
