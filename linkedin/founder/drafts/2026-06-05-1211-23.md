## Post

65% of CMOs expect their role to be disrupted by AI.

Only 32% say significant skill changes are needed to respond to it.

That gap is the whole story.

You can't simultaneously believe your role is being disrupted and that your daily approach doesn't need to change. One of those beliefs is wrong. And in my experience, it's rarely the one about disruption.

This isn't just a CMO problem.

I've watched the same pattern in founders. They see the shift happening. They follow the trends, share the articles, nod along at the conference. But when it comes to how they actually build -- what they ship, how they think about their team, which problems they spend time on -- nothing changes.

Expecting disruption while insulating your workflow from it is a specific kind of trap. It feels like awareness. It operates like denial.

The founders who've actually gotten ahead of this aren't the ones who saw it coming the earliest.

They're the ones who changed what they do on Mondays.
