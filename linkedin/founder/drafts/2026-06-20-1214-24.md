## Post

Your sales team is showing up for the last 30% of the buyer decision.

That's not a criticism. That's just how B2B buying works now.

Buyers spend the bulk of their research phase invisible to you. They're reading your blog, comparing you on G2, watching competitor demos on YouTube, coming back to your pricing page three times without filling out a form.

By the time someone requests a demo, they've mostly decided.

The companies investing in that 70% phase -- the brand presence, the content that surfaces during independent research, the social proof that shows up when someone Googles your name at 11pm -- are building something that compounds over time.

The ones relying entirely on outbound and paid acquisition are renting demand they don't own.

I've watched a lot of founders debate lead gen channels. Which platform, which sequence, which playbook. That conversation mostly misses the question underneath it: does your company exist with credibility in the spaces your buyer researches before they're ready to talk?

Presence in the 70% isn't a soft metric. It's the foundation your sales team is standing on in every single call.

What are you doing to show up in that invisible phase?
