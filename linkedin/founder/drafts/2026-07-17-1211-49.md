## Post

Retargeting converts at 3.8%.

Cold prospecting at 1.5%.

Companies adding retargeting to their paid strategy report a 150% increase in overall conversions. That's not incremental. That's a different tier of performance from the same traffic.

So why do most founders skip it?

Usually framed as a resource issue -- creative, copy, segmentation. But the real bottleneck comes earlier.

You can't retarget people you don't know visited.

Google's pixel degrades over time. Meta's match rates have dropped as privacy protections increased. A lot of the traffic in your analytics is genuinely invisible to the platforms you'd retarget on.

The visitors who hit your pricing page. The ones who read the same case study twice. The company that sent three people to your demo page in one week. They're in your analytics as sessions -- no name, no email.

That's the gap.

When you can match a visit to a real identity, retargeting stops being "reach people who look like your visitors" and becomes "reach the specific people who were already there."

KnownVisitors closes that gap. Identify the visitor first. Then retarget someone real.

What's your experience -- does retargeting actually outperform prospecting for you?
