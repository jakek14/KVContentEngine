## Post

37% of product discovery now starts in ChatGPT or Perplexity.

Not Google. Not a blog post. Not a LinkedIn ad.

An AI chatbot that answers the question, cites a source or two, and moves on.

Here's what's interesting about that traffic: ChatGPT referrals convert at 7.1%. That's nearly identical to paid search, which runs around 7.8%.

The buyer who arrives from AI search has already been pre-qualified by the answer. They came because the AI said you were relevant. That's a different quality of intent than someone who clicked a banner ad.

Most founders I talk to are still thinking about this as an SEO evolution. It's not.

SEO is about ranking. You're one of ten results. The user decides whether to click.

AI search is about being the answer. Either you're what the model cites, or you don't exist in that interaction.

What determines whether you get cited:

It's not your DA or your backlink profile. It's whether your brand appears consistently alongside the right topics across multiple surfaces -- your website, LinkedIn, third-party coverage, review platforms, community discussions.

LLMs form preferences through pattern recognition. The brands that show up consistently in the right contexts, with clear positioning on specific problems, are the ones AI treats as authoritative answers.

The window to establish that kind of authority before this channel is saturated is now. Not next year.

Question for anyone who's tracking this: are you seeing meaningful AI referral traffic in your analytics yet?
