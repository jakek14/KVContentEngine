## Post

Google just added a Generative AI report to Search Console.

For the first time, you can separate AI Overview impressions and clicks from traditional organic search data.

What you're likely going to see when you look:

AI Overview impressions are already high on most sites. The click-through rate is a fraction of what traditional listings deliver. Google surfaces the answer. Most people never visit the source.

This report turns a feeling into a number. And for founders who've been watching organic traffic stagnate while publishing consistently, that number is going to be uncomfortable.

The implication worth sitting with: ranking for a query used to mean getting the visit. Now it can mean Google cites you without a click going anywhere useful.

The response most people are defaulting to is "produce more content, optimize better." That addresses the symptom. The bigger question is whether organic search should still be your primary distribution channel for content that AI can now answer directly.

This report makes that question answerable with data instead of intuition.

Check it before you finalize the content plan for next year.

The number you find might change a few assumptions.
