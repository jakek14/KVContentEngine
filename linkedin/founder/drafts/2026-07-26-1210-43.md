## Post

97% of CMOs are increasing creator marketing budgets in 2026.

Not influencer-marketing-is-back energy. The real kind.

Most B2B founders still think this is a consumer play. Skincare brands. Gaming companies. Not software.

Wrong.

The creators who move B2B aren't the mega-influencers. They're the LinkedIn writers with 18,000 subscribers who trust their product reviews. The newsletter editors who've been covering your category for years. The YouTube analyst whose 45,000 followers actually buy the tools they recommend.

When those creators endorse something, their audience doesn't experience it as an ad. They experience it as a recommendation from someone they already read every week.

Compare that to your Display spend. Or your average cold email open rate. Or what it costs to get 45,000 intentional views from your exact target market on any paid channel.

I think B2B founders are late here because "creator" still sounds like a TikToker to most CFOs. That's a category error.

Creator marketing ranked above AI-driven search and paid social as CMOs' #1 investment priority this year. That's data from enterprise and SaaS CMOs, not consumer brands.

The companies building creator relationships now will have something hard to replicate: trust at scale, in a specific market, built over years. You can't buy that kind of credibility with a Display campaign. You have to earn it through relationships.

Are you seeing B2B creator marketing actually work in your space? Curious what it looks like when it lands.
