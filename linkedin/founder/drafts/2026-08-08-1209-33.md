## Post

Meta just made AI content disclosure mandatory for all ads globally.

If your creative was generated or modified by AI -- any of it -- you're now required to disclose that.

I've been running through what this means for the campaigns we're running, and here's the short version.

It covers more than most founders realize.

AI-generated product images. AI-edited photos. Ad copy that was touched by any LLM in your workflow. If AI was involved in production, the disclosure requirement likely applies.

Meta has built a disclosure toggle directly into Ads Manager. It's not a painful flow. But it requires you to actually know which of your assets used AI -- and most teams haven't done that audit.

Two things worth doing before your next campaign goes live:

Map which tools are touching your creative. Canva AI, Adobe Firefly, any image generator, any LLM in your copy workflow. That's your disclosure scope.

Then run a quick creative audit. Not a big project. 30 minutes is probably enough to get it right.

This isn't going to tank your performance. Meta isn't penalizing AI creative -- just requiring transparency about it.

But getting flagged for non-disclosure after the fact is a headache you don't need.

Better to get ahead of it now.
