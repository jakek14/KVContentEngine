## Post

Organic search traffic is down 2.5% in the last year.

Everyone's panicking.

But AI search referrals are up 527% in the same window. And those visitors convert at 4.4 times the rate of regular organic traffic.

I don't think this is a traffic crisis. I think it's a reshaping of who shows up.

The audiences finding you through AI tools are smaller but sharply more qualified. They arrived because an AI recommended your product or content -- not because they clicked a blue link from a results page at random.

What this means practically:

Going quiet on content because "traffic is down" is optimizing for the wrong number. Volume was never the goal -- buyers were the goal. Informational queries that drove most organic traffic historically are now being answered by AI before anyone ever clicks through.

The content worth producing is the content AI cites and recommends. Which looks a lot like the authoritative, specific, genuinely useful material that built SEO domain authority in the first place. Same inputs. New destination.

The funnel got narrower. The buyers inside it got more serious.
