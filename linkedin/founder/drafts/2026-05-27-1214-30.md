## Post

Google AI Overviews just cut organic click-through rates by 38%.

Zero-click searches -- queries where someone gets an answer without clicking anything -- hit 72% of all Google searches in early 2026.

For B2B tech, 70% of Google queries now trigger an AI Overview.

If you've been building your inbound pipeline on content and SEO, here's how I'm thinking about it.

---

First, the headline isn't as bad as it looks.

The visitors who still click through to your site -- past an AI answer that already summarized the basics -- are showing real intent. They wanted more than the summary. They're evaluating.

Semrush research puts it clearly: AI search visitors convert at 4.4x the rate of average organic visitors.

Fewer visitors. Higher intent per visit.

The math doesn't necessarily get worse. But it does change. Volume as the primary metric stops being the right frame.

---

Here's what I've been building around instead:

**Email.** Your email list is the only marketing channel you actually own. No algorithm, no search ranking, no AI summary between you and your subscriber. B2B newsletters average 20-35% open rates. The ROI benchmark is $36-46 for every $1 spent. If you haven't been building your list aggressively, start now -- while you still have inbound traffic to convert.

**Brand search.** The most defensible B2B companies have one thing in common: people know them by name before they're in buying mode. HubSpot, Gong, Salesforce -- they all show direct traffic in the 65-72% range. That's people typing the URL directly. No channel dependency, no algorithm risk. Getting recognized in your category is worth more now than it's ever been.

**Original research and POV content.** The content that ends up cited in AI Overviews is authoritative, specific, and hard to replicate. First-hand data, original frameworks, genuine expertise. Volume plays on thin content are dead. One well-researched piece with real insight reaches further than twenty templated posts on the same keywords.

**Visitor identification for the traffic you do have.** If every visitor arriving on your site is now higher-intent than before, letting them browse anonymously and leave is a bigger miss than it used to be. This is part of why we built KnownVisitors -- to catch the signal in the visits that happen before anyone raises their hand.

---

The organic traffic model that worked from 2015 to 2023 is compressing.

The companies that will be OK are the ones that built direct channels alongside their SEO.

The ones that treated organic traffic as owned traffic -- not rented traffic -- are going to have a harder 18 months.

The window to build the direct channel isn't closed. But it's narrower than it was.
