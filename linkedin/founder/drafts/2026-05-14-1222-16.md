## Post

PE firms entered 2026 with $3.7 trillion in dry powder earmarked for acquisitions.

$1 trillion of that targeted at US deployment alone.

SaaS consolidation is accelerating. Every week, another familiar product gets rolled up, repriced, or quietly deprecated by its new owners.

---

Here's what this means for founders building independently:

Staying independent is a strategic position. Not a consolation prize.

When consolidation is high, independence is rare. And rare things have pricing power.

Customers are increasingly burned by the acquire-and-strip playbook. They've had tools they loved bought out from under them. They've been force-migrated to inferior products. They've watched pricing double post-acquisition with nowhere to go.

An independent product, built by a founder who isn't going anywhere, is a genuinely differentiated offer.

Not just in the features — in the trust.

---

The founders who feel pressure because they haven't raised or haven't exited are sometimes missing what they actually have.

No acquirer's timeline. No enshittification path. A relationship with customers that isn't up for sale.

In the consolidation era, that's a story the market values.

---

Are you treating independence as a feature? Or still apologizing for not raising?
