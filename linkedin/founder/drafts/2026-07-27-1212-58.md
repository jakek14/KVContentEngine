## Post

Intent data has become a $2 billion industry.

Companies pay hundreds of dollars a month to know which organizations are researching their category on third-party platforms -- which buyer personas are consuming content, which accounts are entering an active evaluation window.

That signal is useful.

But there's a higher-intent signal most companies completely ignore.

People already on their website.

Someone reading your pricing page has shown more intent than someone who read a category article two weeks ago. Someone who hit your comparison page, bounced, and came back three days later is in an active evaluation right now. Someone on your ROI calculator is doing the math as you read this.

That's not a cold prospect. That's a buyer.

The problem is that most companies can only see this as a number. Sessions. Pageviews. Bounce rate. No names. No contact info. No way to follow up.

You're paying to find people who might be in-market. The highest-intent visitors are already on your site -- you just can't see them.

That's the problem KnownVisitors solves. Every visitor identified with contact information, before they leave.

The hottest lead in your pipeline might be invisible to you right now.
